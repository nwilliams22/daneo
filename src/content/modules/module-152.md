# Korean, Word-First — Module 152: Repairs, Seasons and Working Lives

The bulb is changed; now the clock needs repair. Water cools before it freezes. A new employee buys household supplies. The words in this pack follow things and people through changes you can actually observe.

**How to use this:** Learn the words and their notes first. Then read each sentence from natural English through the Korean-order gloss to Korean. Trace matching chunks instead of translating one word at a time.

> **Warm-up — transfer from Module 151.** Say: (1) Find a kettle after turning on the heating. (2) Watch a recording after a cultural visit. (3) Hear out a debater who speaks emotionally. Build these new scenes from the previous pack's blocks.

---

## Part 1 — Who causes the change?

식히다 cools something; 얼리다 freezes something. Keep the object visible. 전시되다 and 추가되다 point the other way: a work is exhibited, an item is added. The result becomes the subject even when the person responsible stays offstage.

수리하다 repairs an object. 풀어지다 describes something coming undone. They both involve change, but one names an intentional job and the other can simply report what happened.

## Part 2 — Life comes in different sizes

생활용품 covers useful everyday goods; 식료품 is the food shelf. 부피 measures the space a package takes up, not how much it weighs. 와이셔츠 is another useful radar word: its English ancestor says white, but the Korean shirt can be any color.

올가을 points to this year. 한겨울 points to the depth of the season. 한평생 stretches the time window across a whole life. Read the first piece before deciding how long the scene lasts.

## Part 3 — Building sentences

No new glue. Familiar frames carry the new vocabulary; notice which noun is the subject and which action closes each sentence.

**Spot the recycling:** 전구, 주전자 and 관람 from M151 return in repairs, cooling water and an extended exhibition visit. The old objects now take new actions.

- **-는 김에 from M91 joins two practical jobs.**
- **-고서 from M85 orders cooling and freezing.**
- **-(으)므로 from M85 gives a stated reason.**

## Practice

1. Give 수리하다 an object that can break.
2. Use 주전자 with 식히다 in a new sentence.
3. Make 전시되다 describe a picture.
4. Compare 부피 with 무게.
5. Explain why a blue 와이셔츠 is not a contradiction.
6. Build a new 관람 scene with extra time.
7. Distinguish 올가을 from 한겨울.
8. Explain what 큰아들 tells you about a family.

**Gap deck:** 와이셔츠 and 큰아들 need more than a word-by-word reading. Explain the real meaning before turning the card over.
