# Korean, Word-First — Module 97: The Economy Page

Band 8 opens here, and it opens the way Band 6 did: no new glue. M85 through M96 own every Ring 3 pattern; from now on the packs are frequency slices of the Grade-C list, sorted by root, and the sentences RECYCLE the machinery you already hold. This first slice is the newspaper's economy page — thirty-four words in three drawers: PRODUCTION (산업 공업 생산 생산하다 노동 농민 농산물 식량 업체 중소기업 건설 건설하다), MONEY (소득 수요 공급 물가 예산 재정 주식 증권 소비 상표), and GROWTH (성장 성장하다 추진 추진하다 진행 운영하다 구입하다 개발되다 선진국 경향) — plus two strays the rank filed here, 조미료 and 등장하다, which turn out to share a sentence. M43 clocked in for the working economy (사업, 공장, 소비자, 세금, 수출, 수입, 상품, 제품, 경영); this module hands you the page those words are printed on.

**How to use this:** decode first — every word here is Sino-Korean and you own a half of nearly every one (産 業 工 生 勞 動 農 民 物 食 體 中 小 建 設 所 要 供 給 價 豫 算 財 政 式 證 消 費 商 標 成 長 進 運 營 入 開 發 先 國 向 調 味 料 登 場); the firsts are 糧 得 需 株 券 推 購 傾. Then read the shelf as one machine: the 하다-noun at full speed. 생산/생산하다, 건설/건설하다, 성장/성장하다, 추진/추진하다 are the pairs M91 drew, and every noun on the page has a 하다 or a 되다 twin you can now build yourself. The sentence deck is eight headlines, each riding one Band 7 ending — read the note to find which.

## Part 1 — The economy page

Three drawers, and one rule that opens all of them.

- **PRODUCTION (산업 공업 생산 생산하다 노동 농민 농산물 식량 업체 중소기업 건설 건설하다):** the sector (산업 — 産 in its produce-job, the one M43's 부동산 banked as property), the factory half of it (공업 — the 工 of M43's 공장, against M59's 농업), and the making (생산, with its 하다). 노동 is labour as the union counts it; 농민 is M59's 농사 as a class; 농산물 is what that class sells; 식량 is food counted by the ministry, with 糧 — rice measured — as the shelf's first new character. Then the firms: 업체 (the operator, the vendor), 중소기업 (the half M90's 기업 note said was frozen), and 건설 (the 建 of M50's 건축 with the 設 of M50's 시설), with its 하다.
- **MONEY (소득 수요 공급 물가 예산 재정 주식 증권 소비 상표):** what you are taxed on (소득 — 所 read as a character at last), the pair every textbook opens with (수요와 공급 — 수요 pays nothing and radars everything; 공급 pays M78's 신혼부부), the level of the tags (물가, the word M93's 예상되다 note wrote frozen), the count made beforehand (예산 — M76's 년도 filed it), the governing of wealth (재정), the stump that became a share (주식), the paper that proves it (증권 — the 證 of M83's 학생증), the act M43's 소비자 stood on (소비), and the mark on the goods (상표 — the third 표 M75's 표시하다 note filed).
- **GROWTH (성장 성장하다 추진 추진하다 진행 운영하다 구입하다 개발되다 선진국 경향):** becoming long (성장, frozen in M88's 이룩하다 note, with its 하다), the shove (추진, with its 하다 — 推 is a first), the going (진행 — dismounted from M61's 진행되다 and M64's 진행하다 at last), keeping a thing running (운영하다 — the 運 of M36's 운 with the 營 of M43's 경영), the receipt's buying (구입하다), the developer removed (개발되다 — M72's 개발하다 through the flip), the country that got there first (선진국), and the report's tends-to (경향 — -는 경향이 있다).

**Receipts come due.** Band 8 is where the frozen riders of forty modules thaw, and this shelf cashes eight:

- M58's 노동자 carried labour-勞 frozen and named 노동절 — **노동** lands the root.
- M90's 기업 wrote **중소기업** frozen as "the other half of every economic headline" — today the half arrives.
- M78's 신혼부부 rode the housing lottery's 특별공급 — **공급** is the word inside it.
- M93's 예상되다 wrote the economy page's own sentence with **물가** frozen — 물가가 오를 것으로 예상됩니다 — and the deck runs its twin.
- M76's 년도 filed a 2025년도 예산 with **예산** frozen.
- M88's 이룩하다 achieved a dazzling **성장**, frozen — and M61's 진행되다 froze the parent that M64 then landed, so **진행** completes a three-module relay.
- M48's 인물 listed the 등장인물 — **등장하다** is the verb those characters do.
- M95's 투자 invested 주식에 with **주식** marked "a C word riding frozen" — the C word lands, one band later.

**The pair rule, one shelf later:** the M91 md named the thesis — Korean names structure with 하다/되다 twins — and M70's 마련되다 note gave the mechanism: "되다 doesn't check pedigree, only the 하다 it replaces". The economy page runs on it: 생산하다/생산되다, 건설하다/건설되다, 추진하다/추진되다, 공급하다/공급되다, 운영하다/운영되다, 개발하다/개발되다. The 하다 side names the company; the 되다 side is what the newspaper prints, because the newspaper is not the company. Where this shelf lists only one side (개발되다, 운영하다), the other rides free — build it.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 수요 is demand (需要), and M5's 수요일 is Wednesday — the water-수 M17's 수영 note found is not in the demand · 물가 is 物價, the price level; M1's 물 and M1's 가 do not add up to it, and the native 물가 (the waterside) is a homograph riding frozen · 주식 is 株式, shares — NIKL lists only that row — but 主食, the staple food, wears the same sound and rides frozen: 쌀이 주식이에요 is about rice · 소득 is what you are taxed on; M43's 수입 is what comes in — same money, one form later · 성장 is 成長, and M23's 성 is a castle — the 成 is the one M35's 성공하다 and M91's 구성 share · 추진 pushes a plan; M60's 진하다 darkens a coffee · 진행 is the going, 추진 the pushing · 업체 is the vendor, M90's 기업 the institution, M2's 회사 the office · and 조미료 is the bottle you buy, M40's 양념 the paste you mix.

**Calibration, three ladders.** The firms: 회사에 가요 (M2's 회사 — the office you go to) · 기업이 발표했어요 (M90's 기업 — the institution announcing) · 업체를 선정했어요 (today — the vendor chosen; 선정 riding frozen). The growers: 아이가 자랐어요 (M35's 자라다 — grew up) · 회사가 발전했어요 (M35's 발전하다 — advanced) · 경제가 성장했어요 (today — grew, on a chart). The buyers: 샀어요 (M2's 사다 — the mouth) · 구입했어요 (today — the receipt) · 판매했어요 (M43's 판매하다 — the other side of the counter). Same act, one register per rung, and the economy page always speaks from the top one.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 ending, named in its note — 에 따르면 from M90 opens the survey and -는 경향이 있다 closes it; -(으)ㄹ 것으로 예상되다 from M93 forecasts 공급 against 수요; -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 turns 소득 into 소비; 에 비해 from M86 measures Korea's 노동 시간 against the 선진국; -(으)므로 from M85 stalls a 건설 사업 on its 예산; -기는 하다 from M92 concedes the 성장 the 농민 never felt; -(으)ㄴ/는 셈이다 from M87 reckons what 물가 did to a 월급; and -(으)ㄴ 결과 from M90 brings the two strays, 조미료 and 등장하다, onto the same stage with 개발되다 in between. Underneath, the frames you have held since Ring 1: the double-subject 한식은 반찬이 많아요 shape from M24, the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24, the comparison 보다 from M22, -(으)면 from M20, and the 합니다체 from M4 on the two printed sentences.

Two of the eight are printed (예상됩니다, 어렵습니다) and six are said; every note names the one ending that made the difference.
