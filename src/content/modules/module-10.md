# Korean, Word-First — Module 10: Can, Must, and Because

The last module of the grammar engine. Three final pieces of glue — **ability** (can/can't), **obligation** (have to), and **reason** (because) — plus the completed question-word kit. After this module, the beginner grammar of Korean is *yours*: everything from here to fluency is vocabulary, practice, and nuance riding on machinery you already own.

**How to use this:** The study-themed vocabulary is self-referential on purpose — 단어 (word), 문법 (grammar), 연습하다 (practice) are the words for what you're doing. Learn the tools in the tools' own language.

## Part 3 — The glue

### 1. Can: -(으)ㄹ 수 있어요 / 없어요

Same hook as the future (-(으)ㄹ), then 수 (a way) + 있다/없다:

- 수영**할 수 있어요**? — can you swim? *(a swim-way exists?)*
- 운전**할 수 없어요** — I can't drive

Ability as existence — the 있다 factory's grandest product (gap deck). Module 3's **못** is the everyday shortcut for the negative: 운전 못 해요.

### 2. Must: -아/어야 돼요

The 아/어 fork you know, plus **야 돼요**:

- 가**야 돼요** — I have to go
- 공부해**야 돼요** — I have to study
- 약을 먹어**야 돼요** — I have to take my medicine *(Korean eats medicine)*

Meet **되다** (become / work / be allowed) — obligation is literally "only then does it become okay." Its negation is the universal veto: **안 돼요** — not allowed / no way (gap deck).

### 3. Because: -아/어서 and -(으)니까

Both weld a reason onto the front; the split is what follows:

| Ending | Use with | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| -아/어서 | statements | 늦**어서** 죄송합니다 — sorry I'm late |
| -(으)니까 | requests & commands | 바쁘**니까** 나중에 전화해 주세요 — I'm busy, so call later |

Rule of thumb: if the sentence ends in 주세요/-(으)세요/-(으)ㄹ까요, the reason takes **니까**. 그래서 (M7) remains the between-sentences version of the same idea.

## Part 5 — Practice

Cover the right column and translate; then flip and build the Korean.

1. Can you swim? → 수영할 수 있어요?
2. I can speak a little Korean. → 한국어를 조금 할 수 있어요.
3. I have to do homework today. → 오늘 숙제를 해야 돼요.
4. Sorry I'm late. → 늦어서 죄송합니다.
5. I'm busy now, so please call later. → 지금 바쁘니까 나중에 전화해 주세요.
6. Please speak slowly. → 천천히 말해 주세요.
7. What does it mean? → 무슨 뜻이에요?
8. I can do it alone. → 혼자 할 수 있어요.

Graduation exercise: answer these, out loud, truthfully — 뭐 할 수 있어요? 뭐 할 수 없어요? 오늘 뭐 해야 돼요? 왜 한국어를 공부해요? You now possess every word in the questions *and* enough for the answers.
