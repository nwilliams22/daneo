# Korean, Word-First — Module 62: The Adverb Drawer

Sixty-one modules built you sentences; this one hands you the tuning pegs. The adverb drawer opens all the way — thirty-five modifiers the themed shelves kept stepping past — and every Korean sentence you own can now be dialed: how strongly, how sneakily, how full, how lucky the timing. Nothing here is a list; everything is a CALIBRATED SET — each new adverb lands next to the one it must be told apart from, because in this drawer the meaning IS the difference: 마침 vs 마침내, 별로 vs 그리, 가득 vs 잔뜩.

**How to use this:** drill in pairs, never alone — the calibration is the content. And give 막 double reps: one syllable, two B-list dictionary rows (just-now and wildly), sorted only by its neighbors.

---

## Part 1 — Six trays, pre-sorted

- **The degree ladder gets its ends:** M11 gave you the middle rungs (아주, 너무, 진짜, 정말). Now the strong pair — 무척 feels, 몹시 suffers — plus 너무나 (너무, sighing in lyrics), 꽤 (the understatement professional), and the floor: 그리 measures a not-that (where M17's 별로 just shrugs), 겨우 barely clears it, 단지 deflates it, 적어도 names the minimum and defends it.
- **The manner corner:** 마구 has no brakes, and its street clip is 막 — which ALSO means just-now: 막 도착했어요. Around them, the stealth-and-boundary squad: 함부로 (without respect — almost always negated), 몰래 (M4's 모르다 in a trench coat), 살짝 (sneakily-lite, lightly), 깜짝 (the jump itself, exactly as M26's 놀라다 note staged it).
- **The quantity tray:** 가득 fills a container, 잔뜩 loads a person or a sky, 온통 paints a whole scene, 조금씩 doses over time — M9's 조금 + M43's 씩, already folklore via 티끌 모아 태산.
- **The time rack:** 마침 is the lucky coincidence; M35's 마침내 is the long-awaited release. 끝내 closes waits that never paid off; 인제 is M26's 이제 with a folksy bend; 점차 is M35's 점점 in a suit; 흔히 states the world's often where M8's 자주 counts yours.
- **The space pair:** 멀리 and 가까이 — M6's 멀다/가깝다 through the adverb press — plus 깊이 (both adverb and M57-grade depth) and 따로, apart on purpose.
- **The emphasis rack:** 분명히 swears, 정말로 testifies, 절대로 forbids, 똑같이 equalizes, 유난히 singles out, 충분히 certifies — and 게다가/더구나 pile clause on clause where M56's 또한 only lectures.

## Part 3 — Three factories, finally named

### 1. The -이 press — adjectives become adverbs

같이 (M9), 많이 (M17), and 빨리 (M12) came off this line before it had a name. Today it stamps four more: 멀다 → **멀리**, 가깝다 → **가까이** (the ㅂ softening out, as 가까워요 taught), 깊다 → **깊이**, 똑같다 → **똑같이**. Radar: M57's ruler factory uses a DIFFERENT -이 that makes nouns (길이, 높이) — and 깊이 rolls off both lines, keeping both jobs: think deeply, measure depth.

### 2. The -로 tail

바로 (M28), 서로 (M24), 새로 (M35), 스스로 (M56), 별로 (M17) — the tail was always there. Today it signs 정말**로**, 절대**로**, 함부**로**, 따**로**: an intensity notch on 정말 and 절대 (M39's note already spelled '절대(로)'), a fossil on the other two.

### 3. The -히 rack fills

M10's 열심히/천천히, M32's 특히, M33's 솔직히/조용히, M42's 확실히/당연히 — and now 분명히 (promised word-for-word by M34's 분명하다 note), 충분히, 유난히, 흔히. None of these factories takes new orders from learners — but every -이/-로/-히 adverb you meet from here on parses itself on sight.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** M40's -아/어 놓다/두다 banks a secret party and loses an umbrella, M53's -는데 stages the betrayal, M38's -자마자 times the door, M39's 밖에 co-signs 겨우's tiny commute, M43's 씩 doses the daily ten, and M25's -(으)ㄹ 때 catches 막 in the act — twice.
