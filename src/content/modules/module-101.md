# Korean, Word-First — Module 101: The Trade and the Tool

Band 8 continues down the Grade-C list, and this slice is the workplace: the tools of a trade and the people who hold them. Thirty-four words in four drawers — THE LOANS (소프트웨어 시스템 코드 텍스트 서클: five English words the office uses, each standing next to the Korean word it did NOT replace), THE CREW (차량 운전사 선장 뱃사람 일행 여인 처녀 수석), THE LEDGER (품질 품목 업종 장비 기획 검토 대출 액수 생산력 국제화 연합), and THE CRAFT (연출 고전 의학 창조 기법 자판 뇌 근육 인격 본성). The organizing thesis: the suffix machines Ring 1 built are still running on the long tail. The job-사 that M4 hung on 요리사 and 의사 names the 운전사; the chief-長 that M39's 장점 stretched literal and M98's 교장 laddered names the 선장; the goods-品 of M43's 제품 and 상품 files the 품질 and the 품목; the work-業 of M43's 사업 sorts the 업종. Nothing here is a new machine — every word is an old machine on a new noun.

**How to use this:** decode first. You own a half of nearly every Sino word here (車 運 轉 士 長 一 行 女 人 處 首 席 品 質 目 業 種 裝 備 企 劃 檢 討 出 數 生 産 力 國 際 化 聯 合 演 醫 學 造 技 法 字 肉 格 本 性); the firsts are 輛 貸 額 古 典 創 筋 腦, with 船 and 板 stepping out of words you already hold (우주선, 칠판). Then read the five loans with the M79 md's rule — "every loanword in Korean lives next to a Korean word it did NOT replace" — and spend your reps on the neighbour, not the spelling: 시스템 beside 체계, 텍스트 beside 본문 and M20's 문자, 서클 beside M69's 동아리, 소프트웨어 beside M32's 프로그램, and 코드, which is two English words in one Korean coat. The sentence deck is eight lines, each riding one Band 7 ending — the note names it.

## Part 1 — The trade and the tool

Four drawers, and the rails that run through them.

- **THE LOANS (소프트웨어 시스템 코드 텍스트 서클):** the converter's shelf, one generation on from M79. 소프트웨어 is the category M32's 프로그램 is one instance of; 시스템 is the machine where the textbook writes 체계; 코드 is the computer's code, the wedding's dress code, and the power cord M61's 뽑다 pulls; 텍스트 is the text of a document — never the text message, which stays M20's 문자; and 서클 is the club your parents joined, where you join a 동아리. Two of the five date the speaker (서클, and the ledger's 국제화), and that is part of what the shelf teaches: loans have decades too.
- **THE CREW (차량 운전사 선장 뱃사람 일행 여인 처녀 수석):** the -사 and -장 rails. 운전사 pays M78's 운전기사, which filed it as "the older form", and sits on the licence side of the two job-사s that note sorted; 선장 puts the chief-長 on a ship, one rung along from M58's 총장 and M98's 교장; 뱃사람 is the same sailor in native wood — M3's 배 + linking-ㅅ + M1's 사람, the wedge M82's 바닷물 wore. 차량 is the traffic report's cars, 일행 the restaurant's party, 수석 the head seat that M44's 합격 sits in. Two women's words close the drawer, and both need handling: 여인 is the novel's woman, 처녀 the folk tale's maiden — read them everywhere, say them almost nowhere, as M58's 아가씨 note ruled for its own word.
- **THE LEDGER (품질 품목 업종 장비 기획 검토 대출 액수 생산력 국제화 연합):** the 品 and 業 rails, then the bank. 품질 pays M81's 질 — that note's "품질 (frozen) is the label's word; 질 is the mouth's" — and 품목 puts the eye-目 of M33's 제목 on each row of the inventory; 업종 is M43's 사업 sorted by M57's 종류. 장비 is what the job wears (the dress-裝 of M80's 포장), 기획 is planning as a department (the 劃 M75's 계획하다 left frozen), 검토 is M44's 검사 and 토론 fused into a review — 검토 중, the gap deck's non-answer. Then the money: 대출 lends out, 액수 counts the sum, 생산력 puts the strength-力 of M39's 노력 on M97's 생산, 국제화 is the slogan 세계화 retired, and 연합 is the UN in Sino and the wire service on every byline.
- **THE CRAFT (연출 고전 의학 창조 기법 자판 뇌 근육 인격 본성):** the stage (연출 — the act, where M96's 감독 is the person), the shelf (고전 — old and still read), the sciences (의학, with the doctor-醫 M86's 의존하다 note pointed at; 창조 on the make-造 of M91's 구조; 기법 as the named way inside an art), and the body and the self: 자판 the keyboard (not M80's 자판기), 뇌 the brain the doctor names where the mouth says M8's 머리, 근육 on the flesh-肉 of M92's 육체, and the pair the module ends on — 인격, the frame of a person that M91's 형성 carried frozen, and 본성, what you are under the 성격.

**The rails, named.** Three suffix machines carry the crew and the stock, and all three are Ring 1's. The job-사 is two hanja, as M78's 운전기사 note sorted them: teacher-師 (M4's 요리사 and 의사, M14's 간호사) against licensed-士 (M44's 박사, M16's 변호사, M48's 기사 — and today's 운전사). The chief-長 is the ladder M98's 교장 note laid out — "The 長 ladder: 부장 runs a section, M9's 사장님 a company, 교장 a school" — and 선장 adds the rung at sea. The goods-品 runs 제품 made and 상품 sold (M43), then 품질 graded and 품목 listed (today). Read the suffix and the word half-decodes itself.

**The office's two vocabularies.** Every loan on the shelf has a neighbour it left standing, and the pairs sort by where you are: 시스템 in the newspaper, 체계 in the textbook · 텍스트 on the screen, 본문 in the editor's margin · 소프트웨어 the category, M32's 프로그램 the item · 코드 with a plug, M99's 선 without one · 서클 then, M69's 동아리 now · 키보드 in the shop, 자판 in the settings menu. The loan took one job each; the older word kept the rest — the M79 verdict, one shelf further along.

**Receipts come due.** Six frozen riders thaw: M78's 운전기사 wrote **운전사** as the older form; M81's 질 wrote **품질** as the label's word; M91's 형성 wrote **인격** frozen in 인격 형성; M92's 우주 carried the ship-船 frozen in 우주선, and **선장** lands it; M93's 드러나다 left the transitive 드러내다 waiting, and **본성** is the noun it takes; and M72's 자동 built 자판기 in passing, which is why **자판** needs its radar.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 코드 is code AND cord — the computer's, the dress code's, and the one you pull from the wall; the converter cannot tell them apart, so the room must · 텍스트 is a document's text; the text you SEND is M20's 문자, always · 자판 is 字板, the keyboard; M80's 자판기 is 自販機, the vending machine — same three letters, different word, and only the 기 shows it · 서클 and M69's 동아리 are the same club a generation apart; the geometric circle is 원, a homograph of M9's 원 · 일행 is 一行, one's party — not M2's 일 (work) with a 행 behind it · 처녀 shares the place-處 with M86's 처지 and M36's 상처, but the word is a maiden, and dated · 뇌 is the organ, M8's 머리 the head — 머리가 좋아요 is smart, 뇌 is neuroscience · 검사 (M44) checks a thing, 검토 reviews a proposal · 성격 (M19) is how you act, M45's 성질 is your fuse, 본성 is what you are.

**Calibration, two ladders.** The driver: 운전자 (M50 — anyone at the wheel, on paper) · 운전사 (today — the job, in the older word) · 운전기사 (M78 — the job, in the current word) · 기사님 (the radar in M48's 기사 note — the man in the mirror). The woman: 여자 (M4 — the mouth's) · 여성 (M32 — the form's) · 여인 (today — the novel's). Same person, one register per rung.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 ending, named in its note — -다가 from M94 interrupts the cleaning to pull a 코드; -(으)므로 from M85 stalls 생산 on a 품질 검토; 에 비해 from M86 sets a 소프트웨어 against an old 시스템 inside the double-subject frame; -더라고요 from M88 reports a 서클 senior turned 선장; -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 slopes the 뇌 like a 근육; 에 따라 from M90 varies a 대출 액수 by 업종; -(으)ㄹ 테니까 from M93 readies the 차량 for a 일행; and 반면에 from M95 weighs 본성 against M19's 성격. Underneath, the frames you have held since Ring 1: -아/어 버리다 from M52, the long negation -지 않다 from M28, the can-machine from M10 twice, the past badge -(으)ㄴ from M24, 처럼 from M41, -아/어지다 from M35, the 르-family from M7, -아/어 주세요 from M9, and the 합니다체 from M4 on the one printed line.

One of the eight is printed (없습니다); seven are said — at the desk, the bank, the tour bus and the gym. Every note names the one ending that made the difference.

**Gap deck:** the host's first question at the door (일행이 있으세요?), the email reply that is neither yes nor no (검토 중), and the newspaper's yearly genre (수석 합격).

## What's next

Band 8 keeps going down the Grade-C list: the next slices sort the native verbs, the house and the office, and the adverbs of the page. No new endings — the grammar closed at M96, and from here the words are the work. Read a job posting with today's shelf and you will find it is nothing but this module: 업종, 장비, 기획, 검토, 품질 관리 — and, at the bottom, 검토 후 연락드리겠습니다. 계속 읽으세요.
