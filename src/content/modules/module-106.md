# Korean, Word-First — Module 106: Persons, Places and the -적 Shelf II

Thirty-two Grade-C nouns, no new grammar, and one claim you can check on every card: **the long tail is compounds of halves you already own.** 환경오염 is M32's 환경 on M99's 오염. 자연환경 is M22's 자연 on that same 환경. 가치관 is M87's 가치 under the see-觀 of M35's 습관. 학용품 is the study-學 of M15's 학원 with the use-用 of M43's 비용 and the goods-品 of M43's 제품. Four drawers hold them: THE PERSONS (인 소유자 피해자 학부모 원장 농부 마누라 동행), who arrive on the person-suffixes 人 and 者; THE PLACES AND TIMES (초원 강남 북부 한가운데 기원전 말기 이달 애초 개국), which are the map and the calendar a register above M31; THE -적 SHELF II (창조적 절대적 합리적 필수적 전반적 부정적), the second run off the M45 factory; and THE COMPOUNDS (사회주의 증권사 환경오염 자연환경 가치관 학번 학용품 호기심 등등), nine sums with nothing new in them but the spacing.

**How to use this:** read every decode before you drill, because on this shelf the decode IS the word — almost nothing here has to be memorised as a whole. Spend your reps on the three sorts instead: the wife-ladder (아내 / 부인 / 마누라), the period-ladder (초기 / 중기 / 후기 / 말기), and the -적 pairs (긍정적 / 부정적, 상대적 / 절대적). Every sentence in Part 2 recycles one Band 7 ending and names it; if a name is unfamiliar, that module's md is one tap away.

## Part 1 — Persons, places and the -적 shelf II

Two person-suffixes run the first drawer, and you have been reading both for a hundred modules without being told they were a system:

| Suffix | Character | First seen | On this shelf |
|---|---|---|---|
| -인 | 人 a kind of person | M23's 외국인 | 인 itself, bare and printed |
| -자 | 者 a role or a trade | M27's 환자 | 소유자 · 피해자 |
| -부 | 夫 a man | M78's 형부 | 농부 |
| -장 | 長 the chief | M39's 장점 | 원장 |

- **The persons (인 소유자 피해자 학부모 원장 농부 마누라 동행):** 인 is the bare 人 at last — M96's 지식인 filed its job ("-인 (人) marks a kind of person (지식인, 외국인, 정치인 — M32's 정치 note built that one)") and M32's 인간 put M17's 인기 on the same character. The 者 rail pays two debts in one drawer: M98's 소유 left 소유자 standing and M86's 피해 left 피해자. Then the institutions — 학부모 is the school's word for parents (M4's 부모님 is the address), 원장 is whoever runs a building ending in -원, 농부 is M97's 농민 divided by the number of farmers in the field. 마누라 and 동행 close it: one wife at the bottom of a four-rung ladder, one companion who is not M101's 일행.
- **The places and times (초원 강남 북부 한가운데 기원전 말기 이달 애초 개국):** 초원 is grass-原 and not M25's 초; 강남 is M22's 강 in its river-江 coat with the 南 of M27's 남쪽; 북부 finally collects the car M98's 남부 left frozen, now that M103's 중부 has taken the middle; 한가운데 is M28's 가운데 with the intensifying 한- that also makes 한여름 and 한밤중. Then four rungs of calendar — 기원전 (BC, the museum caption), 말기 (the last period, after M96's 후기), 이달 (the printed this-month against M16's 이번 달), 애초 (the very start, and the gap deck's adverb) — and 개국, a bound counter for nations on M5's 개.
- **The -적 shelf II (창조적 절대적 합리적 필수적 전반적 부정적):** the M45 factory, second run. That md set the intake rule — "The stock is Sino-only; that's why 사회, 경제, 역사, 과학 all take it and native nouns don't." — and six Sino nouns you own walk in: M101's 창조, M39's 절대, 합리 (M44's 합격 meets M34's 이해), 필수 (M86's 필요성 meets a須), 전반 (M31's 전국 meets M45's 일반적), and M98's 부정. Two of the six land pairs Band 5 and Band 8 held open: 부정적 was promised by M45's 긍정적 and named by M98's 부정; 절대적 was held frozen by M99's 상대적. Four of the six live mostly as adverbs in -적으로.
- **The compounds (사회주의 증권사 환경오염 자연환경 가치관 학번 학용품 호기심 등등):** nine sums. 사회주의 is M32's 사회 on the -주의 M90's 민주주의 decoded; 증권사 is M97's 증권 clipped onto the company-社 of M100's 사설; 환경오염 and 자연환경 stack 환경 with 오염 and 자연; 가치관 pays M87's 가치 and M91's 형성하다 at once; 학번 is the study-學 on M12's 번 and the entry-year caste the gap deck files; 학용품 is that 學 with 用 and 品; 호기심 pays M93's 지적; and 등등 is S2's 등 doubled, exactly as promised.

**Who actually says these.** Half this shelf is read before it is ever spoken. 소유자 is on a deed, 피해자 in a bulletin, 학부모 on a school notice, 기원전 under a museum case, 개국 in an Olympics headline, 이달 on a printed calendar, and 전반적으로 in the second paragraph of a report — the four-noun-no-verb register S2 taught you to read. The mouth reaches for M27's 주인, M4's 부모님, M16's 이번 달 instead. The exceptions are the two that live entirely in speech: 마누라, which only a husband says, and 등등, which only a speaker says — a written list closes with S2's 등 and stops.

**The space that isn't there.** Four of today's words are two taught nouns with the gap closed — 환경오염, 자연환경, 학용품 and 이달 — and the closing is what makes them dictionary words instead of phrases. M99's 오염 note wrote the compound spaced (환경 오염) because it was describing a phrase; NIKL files the headword shut. The same law sorts 이달 from M16's 이번 달: the weld takes the space out, and the written register takes the weld. When you meet a new Grade-C noun that looks impossibly long, try the scissors first — cut it in two and check whether you own both halves. On this shelf you will, nine times out of nine.

**The -적 hardware, once more.** A stamped word is still a NOUN, so it needs gear to act, and the M45 md installed all three settings: -적이다 to predicate (필수적이에요), -적인 + noun with the badge from M24 (합리적인 가격, 창조적인 생각), and -적으로 to adverb (전반적으로, 절대적으로). Count where each of today's six actually lives and the shelf sorts itself: 전반적 is an adverb almost always, 합리적 and 부정적 ride mostly on the badge, 필수적 and 절대적 like to predicate, and 창조적 takes all three without complaint. The bare roots are not interchangeable with the stamped words: 절대 needs a negative behind it, 필수 is a determiner that refuses the 적 (필수 과목), and 전반 barely exists alone.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 초원 is grass-草 on the plain-原 — NOT M25's 초 (the candle) plus M9's 원 (the won), and the 原 is not the 園/院 of 유치원 and today's 원장 · 애초 is the very beginning; M47's 애 is a kid, and there are no children in an 애초 · 강남 is south-南 of the river-江; M32's 남성 filed the other 남 (man-男), and 강남구's 구 is the district-區 of M98's 구역, never M2's 구 · 마누라 is a man's OWN wife and nobody else's — the register warning on that card is the load-bearing one in this module · 인 is 人 here and in M17's 인기 and M19's 인사하다, but M86's 원인 is the cause-因 and M31's 인천 is 仁 · 개국 counts countries (個國); the 개국 that founds a state is 開國 and is not on the list · 학번 is a number AND a year, and the year is the one that matters · 부정적 is the 否 that denies, not the 不正 that cheats · and 말기 has nothing after it, which is why it is said softly.

**Calibration, three ladders.** The wife: M27's 아내 on the form · M27's 부인 about another man's · M58's 사모님 about the boss's · 마누라 about his own, and only his. The period: M88's 초기 at the start · 중기 (frozen) in the middle · M96's 후기 late · 말기 at the end, with nothing after it. The owner: M27's 주인 behind the counter · 소유자 on the deed · M98's 관계자 on the door. Three sorts, and in each one Korean is filing by a distinction English leaves to context.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 ending and names it — -기 마련이다 from M87 makes the victims' reaction inevitable, -더라도 from M95 refuses the owner an absolute right, -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 puts a noun on the dial (창조적인 사람일수록), -기는커녕 from M92 denies the environment its improvement and then denies the smaller thing too, -던 from M88 looks back at a way of life on the northern steppe, -는 김에 from M91 hitches a brokerage question to a trip to 강남, -고자 from M90 gives a 학원 director his podium voice, and -길래 from M93 lets a parent watch a child lose everything and then buy it again. Under them ride frames you have held since Ring 1: the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24 on four of the -적 words, the -기 nominalizer from M13, -아/어지다 from M35, the long negation from M28, M27's 들, and 에 대해 from M32.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the entry year that is also a rank (학번), the adverb that cancels an argument's premise (애초에), and the one word for wife that a learner should recognise and never say (마누라).
