# Korean, Word-First — Module 72: The Laboratory

M32 handed you 연구 and 결과; this module builds the room between them. The pool's two highest-ranked nouns live here (기계, 에너지), with the people and buildings of research (과학자, 연구자, 연구소), the machine shelf (전자, 석유, 산소, 자동, 도구, 모델), and the process verbs every textbook chapter and news feature runs on — 분석하다, 관찰하다, 개발하다, 연결하다, 확대하다. The organizing thesis: **nearly every word here is hanja you already own, and every -하다 arrives with its -되다 standing next to it.** M56 built the news-verb engine; M57 the measuring kit; M61 the 되다-row's register. Today: the lab, with the pairs wired side by side.

**How to use this:** read the decodes before you drill — the person-者 and place-所 factories, the launch-發 family, and the tie-結 knot each build three or four words on this shelf, and the X-rays do the memorizing. Spend your reps on the use-trio (사용/이용/활용), the two 발s (발달/발전), and the 하다/되다 pairs: Band 6 words earn their rank by refusing to be interchangeable.

## Part 1 — The factories run the lab

This shelf is mostly receipts:

- **The person-者 payroll hires its scientists:** M27's 환자 note introduced the suffix, M43's 소비자 formalized it, M48's 저자/독자 extended it — 과학자 and 연구자 clock in today. M46's 장래 note already wrote 과학자 on the school form.
- **The place-所 zooms out:** M27's 장소 and M52's 숙소 held the place-所; 연구소 is the institute — M32's 연구 note filed 연구실 as the room. And 산소 hides a second 所: the hillside grave.
- **The launch-發 family completes:** M8's 출발하다, M35's 발전하다, M56's 발견하다/발달하다/발생하다 all decoded 發; today its nouns dismount (발달, 발견, 발생) and 개발하다 adds open-開 in front of it.
- **The knot untangles:** M32's 결과 tied off effort with 結; M56's 해결하다 warned its own 결 was decide-決. 연결하다 is the tie-結 again — link and tie — and 해결 stands bare beside it.
- **The three uses:** M10's 사용하다 used a thing, M56's 이용하다 used a service; 활용하다 makes the most of — and the use-用 inside all three is the one M63's 소용없다 note isolated.

## Part 3 — Two machines, no new grammar

### 1. The 하다/되다 pair, taught as a pair

The 되다-row (M36) — swap the 하다, remove the agent — is this shelf's spine, and today the pairs sit adjacent so the swap is visible: 확대하다 → **확대되다** (you widen it / it widens), 연결하다 → **연결되다** (you connect it / it's connected), and 이용 → **이용되다** (the noun straight to the passive, the way M61's 사용되다 already stands). Read the row in headlines and hear it on PA systems: 범위가 확대되고 있습니다, 와이파이가 연결됐어요.

**Calibration:** the 하다 side names a doer; the 되다 side hides one. Print prefers hiding (사업이 확대되었다); speech prefers a doer unless the doer is a machine — which is why 연결됐어요 is the one 되다-verb here you'll say every day.

### 2. The register pairs — one meaning, two altitudes

M56 modeled the pair with 나다/발생하다; this shelf adds three: **변하다 (M26) / 변화하다** — the world changes in speech, society changes in print; **뜻 (M10) / 의미하다** — 무슨 뜻이에요? asks, 이것은 …을 의미한다 answers on paper; **기름 (M40) / 석유** — the tank vs the commodity. And the adverbs ride free on M6's 으로: 자동으로 (automatically), 실제로 (actually) — two words the settings screen and the essay both repeat.

**Calibration:** none of this is new grammar. -하다/-되다, -(으)로, the bare -ㄴ다 of print — you own all three. What lands today is the vocabulary that makes a science paragraph readable: source (에 따르면, M32), process (분석하다, 관찰하다), result (발견, 해결, 발생).

## What's next

The long tail keeps unspooling, root by root. You can now read the paragraph under the graph — 연구 결과에 따르면 … 것으로 나타났다 — the way its author built it, and you know why the 와이파이 sentence and the 석유 sentence use the same passive.
