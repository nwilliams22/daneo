# Korean, Word-First — Module 102: Native Verbs II

M94 opened the native verb shelf with the verbs of motion and mishap; this is the second shelf, and it is the hand's and the body's — thirty-seven Grade-C words, no new grammar, and one thesis that does most of the memorizing for you: **native Korean makes a new verb by welding two old ones.** 주고받다 is M9's 주다 + M9's 받다, 바로잡다 is M28's 바로 + M28's 잡다, 살아남다 is M4's 살다 + M35's 남다, 내다보다 is M28's 내다 + M1's 보다, 뒤따르다 is M6's 뒤 + M32's 따르다, 잇따르다 is M88's 잇다 + the same 따르다, 불러일으키다 is M19's 부르다 + M56's 일으키다 — and you own every half. Around the welds sit four drawers: THE HAND (스치다 훔치다 굽히다 박다 박히다 짚다 바치다 주고받다 바로잡다 깔리다 잠기다 트이다), THE BODY IN TROUBLE (살아남다 숨지다 주저앉다 잇따르다 뒤따르다 내다보다 불러일으키다 두드러지다 마땅하다), THE SINO VERBS (택하다 짐작하다 제거하다 기여하다 적용되다 전개되다 부정하다 지시하다 공개하다 분포하다 — ten 하다/되다 verbs whose noun or root you already hold), and THE NATIVE NOUNS (구석 논 떼 가르침 거꾸로 대충).

**How to use this:** decode before you drill. Read the weld table once and every compound on the shelf becomes two words you already know; then run the party check on the rest — 박히다, 깔리다 and 잠기다 are the passive party of M36, 굽히다 is the causative party of M37, 두드러지다 rides the -지다 wing from M35, 가르침 came off the -(으)ㅁ freezer of M67. The ten Sino verbs each stand on a root you have decoded: 택 from M33's 선택하다, 제거 from M93's 제외하다 and M35's 과거, 부정 from M98's 부정 — and five of them pay a note that explicitly left the verb frozen (전개되다, 공개하다, 지시하다, 부정하다, 박히다). Then read the Radar patrol slowly: this shelf has more sound-alike traps than any since M94. Every sentence in Part 2 recycles one Band 7 ending and names it in its note.

## Part 1 — Native verbs II

The weld table first. Two old verbs, or an adverb and a verb, joined on -아/어 or -고 or bare, and the meaning is the sum:

| Weld | = | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| 주고받다 | M9's 주다 + 고 + M9's 받다 | give and receive = exchange |
| 바로잡다 | M28's 바로 + M28's 잡다 | grab it straight = correct |
| 살아남다 | M4's 살다 + 아 + M35's 남다 | live and be left = survive |
| 내다보다 | M28's 내다 + M1's 보다 | put the gaze out = look out, foresee |
| 뒤따르다 | M6's 뒤 + M32's 따르다 | follow behind; a consequence comes with |
| 잇따르다 | M88's 잇다 + M32's 따르다 | joined and following = one after another |
| 불러일으키다 | M19's 부르다 + 어 + M56's 일으키다 | call up and raise = provoke, arouse |
| 숨지다 | M41's 숨 + M17's 지다 | the breath is lost = die (the news's word) |

Then the four drawers, and what each one is for:

- **THE HAND (스치다 훔치다 굽히다 박다 박히다 짚다 바치다 주고받다 바로잡다 깔리다 잠기다 트이다):** contact that does not stay (스치다 — the verb two gap decks ran frozen, in 월급이 통장을 스쳐 간다 and 옷깃만 스쳐도 인연), the thief's verb (훔치다, on M36's 도둑), the body bent (굽히다 — 허리를, 무릎을, and 뜻을 for backing down), the nail in and the nail stuck (박다 / 박히다 — the M91 gap's 틀에 박히다 lands its verb), the hand set down (짚다 — the M84 gap's 번지수를 잘못 짚다 lands its verb too), the gift upward (바치다, on the elevation of M9's 드리다), the two welds, and three happen-verbs: 깔리다 (spread, or run over), 잠기다 (submerged, or locked — two verbs, one spelling), 트이다 (a road, a chest, a tongue opening).
- **THE BODY IN TROUBLE (살아남다 숨지다 주저앉다 잇따르다 뒤따르다 내다보다 불러일으키다 두드러지다 마땅하다):** the survivor's verb and the caption's death, the knees giving (주저앉다, on M9's 앉다), the news's run of events (잇따르다) and the consequence that follows (뒤따르다), the gaze out of the window or into M25's 미래 (내다보다), the controversy provoked (불러일으키다), and two adjectives — 두드러지다 (prominent, praised or not — beside M89's 뛰어나다 and M94's 띄다) and 마땅하다 (what ought to be, beside M73's 적당하다 and M87's 적절하다).
- **THE SINO VERBS (택하다 짐작하다 제거하다 기여하다 적용되다 전개되다 부정하다 지시하다 공개하다 분포하다):** each on an owned root. 택하다 is the pick-擇 of M33's 선택하다 alone; 짐작하다 pours and measures wine (two firsts); 제거하다 is the 除 of M93's 제외하다 with the 去 of M35's 과거; 기여하다 sends on the 與 of M86's 여건; 적용되다 flips M64's 적용하다 through the 되다-row; 전개되다 pays M91's 전개, 공개하다 pays M95's 공개, 부정하다 and 지시하다 pay M98's 부정 and 지시; 분포하다 spreads the 分 of M32's 부분 across a map.
- **THE NATIVE NOUNS (구석 논 떼 가르침 거꾸로 대충):** the inside corner, the wet field M59's 밭 left frozen, the flock, the lesson frozen from M15's 가르치다, and two adverbs — upside down, and roughly.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 굽히다 bends a back; M18's 굽다 grills pork — the 히 belongs to a second, frozen 굽다 (to be bent), never to the grill · 잠기다 is two verbs — a door 잠기다 (locked, from frozen 잠그다) and a village 잠기다 (sunk) — and neither contains M28's 잠 · the 못 in 못을 박다 is a nail, a homograph of M3's 못 (can't): 못을 못 박아요 is grammatical · 바치다 is not M87's 바 + M17's 치다 — one native verb, no striking · 훔치다 is likewise not 훔 + 치다 · 짚다 sets a hand down, M66's 집다 picks a thing up — 짚따 and 집따 · 두드러지다 stands out, M66's 두드리다 knocks · 논 is the paddy; the 논 of M44's 논문 is argument-論 · 떼 is a flock; M61's 떼다 detaches and the 라떼 of S1's 꼰대 note is coffee · 대충 is roughly; M85's 대체로 is on the whole — one is the mouth's, one the page's — and neither is M55's 대 · 마땅하다 has no M22's 땅 in it · 거꾸로 carries the 로 of M6 as a fossil; do not add another.

**Calibration, two ladders.** The pickers: 골라 보세요 (M34's 고르다 — the shop) · 선택하세요 (M33's 선택하다 — the form) · 택했어요 (today — the essay). The deaths: 죽었어요 (M14's 죽다 — the mouth, and the hunger and the homework) · 목숨을 잃었어요 (M92's 목숨 with M28's 잃다 — the drama) · 숨졌어요 (today — the caption under the photo). Same act, one register per rung; the shelf you are on speaks from the top one.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence recycles one Band 7 ending and names it — -(으)ㄹ 뻔하다 from M94 nearly fells you on a road that 낙엽이 깔린; -(으)로 인해 from M86 files the fog behind the accidents that 잇따랐고 and the one who 숨졌어요; -더라도 from M95 concedes everyone's objection before 뜻을 굽히지 않을 거예요; -던 from M88 remembers the friend you 문자를 주고받던; -(으)며 from M85 lets the government 공개하며 and promise to 바로잡겠다고 in one breath; -(으)ㄹ 듯하다 from M87 hedges the 책임이 뒤따를 after a 대충 짐작; -다시피 from M91 opens 아시다시피 on a village whose 논이 many go 물에 잠겨요; and -기는 하다 from M92 concedes that 한국어가 트이기는 했지만 the newspaper is still 대충밖에. Underneath ride the frames you have held since Ring 1: the badges of M24, -지 않다 from M28, the quote machine -다고 from M32, 밖에 from M39, 마다 from M31 and the try-machine from M16.

**Gap deck:** the gate of speech opening (말문이 트이다 — the learner's breakthrough), the corner doubled into thoroughness (구석구석), and the adverb doubled into an accusation (대충대충).
