# Korean, Word-First — Module 126: Daily Measures and Public Choices

A daily routine is measured by costs, convenience, and the choices people make when those change. The ranked slice reaches from the city to a farm, with public labels and personal work beside them.

**How to use this:** learn each word's exact sense in the notes; then read the eight sentences as new uses of old Ring 3 grammar. This pack adds no new glue.

---

## Part 1 — The daily measure

The public shelf holds 경비, 폐지, 편의, and 담당자. A report may contain a 답변, a 해설, or a 묘사: one answers, one explains, and one paints a scene. The everyday shelf contrasts 도심 with 농사짓다 and pairs today’s 치아 with the firmness word 단단하다 from M60.

**Root and form map:** M44's 전공 becomes 전공하다, the act of majoring; M39's 만족하다 yields the noun 만족. M56's 묘사하다 yields 묘사. The -적 shelf adds labels such as 질적 and 문화적 without inventing new roots or new grammar.

::vocab::

**Homograph radar:** 경비 here is 經費, expenses, not 警備, guarding. 대비 is 對備, preparation, not 對比, comparison. 대체 is the emphatic question adverb, not a replacement noun. 모금 is one sip, not 募金 fundraising.

**Register:** 희생 is serious loss and deserves a serious context. 미혼 names marital status without implying a judgment.
