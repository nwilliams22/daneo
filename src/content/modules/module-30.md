## Part 1 — Your thirties happen in native numbers

M5 stopped at 스물 (twenty). The tens continue: **서른, 마흔, 쉰, 예순, 일흔, 여든, 아흔** — thirty through ninety, and 서른 살 is how every Korean thirtieth birthday is pronounced. Ages, that is; money stays Sino (삼만 원), and so do dates. Two systems, unchanged jobs.

The ordinals stack on native roots: **첫째, 둘째, 셋째** (first, second, third — 넷째, 다섯째 continue the pattern) — and they double as birth order: 첫째 is also *the firstborn*, 막내 the youngest (M12's 막-syllable, again).

And the **measure machine** takes its last passengers: 미터, 퍼센트, 달러 ride like M5's counters (이 미터, 이십 퍼센트, 십 달러 — Sino numbers all). **장** counts flat things — 종이 한 장, 사진 두 장, 표 세 장. **개월** counts months Sino-side: 삼 개월 = 세 달 — the same hybrid split as M5's clock, one more time.

## Part 3 — The glue

### 1. 만 — only

Attach 만 and everything else falls away: 하나**만** 주세요 (just one), 조금**만** (just a little), 너**만** (only you — half of K-pop in one particle). It usually REPLACES 을/를 and 이/가, like M2's 도: 커피만 마셔요, not 커피를만.

### 2. (이)랑 / 와·과 — the and-with registers

M2's 하고 was the neutral *and/with*. Its register siblings arrive:

- **(이)랑** — casual: 딸기**랑** 포도 (vowel→랑, batchim→이랑). Friends, texts, and 반말 territory (M19's rules apply)
- **와/과** — formal: 딸기**와** 포도 (vowel→와, batchim→**과** — yes, backwards from what you'd guess). Books, news, wedding invitations — 함께's natural companion (M29)

Same jobs as 하고 — listing and accompanying — three suits for one particle. The register ladder now dresses particles too.

### 3. The 개월/달 rule

Sino + 개월, native + 달: 삼 개월 = 세 달, both three months. Duration then takes M28's 동안: 삼 개월 동안 한국어를 배웠어요. M5's hybrid-clock logic, still the law.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** 여름에는 수박이 가장 맛있어요 crowns the melon with M29's formal superlative, 칫솔하고 치약을 꼭 사야 돼요 hardens M10's obligation with M29's 꼭, and 그 피자는 매우 커요 points M29's bare 그 and 매우 at dinner.

## Part 5 — Practice

Cover the right column and translate; then flip and build the Korean.

1. I'm thirty years old. → 저는 서른 살이에요.
2. Just one, please. → 하나만 주세요.
3. I bought strawberries and grapes. → 딸기랑 포도를 샀어요.
4. A hamburger and a cola, please. → 햄버거랑 콜라 주세요.
5. Please take two photos. → 사진 두 장 찍어 주세요.
6. I studied Korean for three months. → 삼 개월 동안 한국어를 배웠어요.
7. In summer, watermelon is the tastiest. → 여름에는 수박이 가장 맛있어요.
8. I know a thousand words! → 단어를 천 개 알아요!

Age drill: say every family member's age in native tens (서른여덟 살…), then their birth years in Sino (천구백…년) — the two systems, side by side, never touching.
