# Korean, Word-First — Module 103: The Abstract Shelf III

M97 opened Band 8 with a promise — that the frozen riders of forty modules would thaw here — and M99 showed how a Sino syllable is a row in a table. This module puts the two together: **the abstract shelf is built from halves you already own.** Thirty-four Grade-C nouns, and nearly every one is a taught hanja plus one new one — 정면 is M44's 정답 meeting M43's 면접, 지출 is M65's 지점 meeting M43's 출근, 자존심 is M32's 자기 and M34's 의심 around a single new 尊. Three drawers: WHERE AND WHEN (정면 중반 직전 최종 대륙 중부 양국 동포 등장), HOW MUCH (실체 적용 비율 변동 추가 지출 확보 제약 무리 극히 궁극적 불가피하다), and THE HEART (자존심 애정 정성 자극 항의 덕 향 소음 생물 근원 실태 해당 대신하다). No new grammar: every sentence recycles one Band 7 ending and names it.

**How to use this:** read each note for the half you own first — the decode names it, and the new character is usually the only thing left to learn. Then spend your reps on the receipts, because nine of these words were already riding frozen in an earlier note and the note quotes the line that promised them: 직전 in M88's 직후, 중부 in M98's 남부, 정성 in M94's 들이다, 소음 in the M54 gap deck, 등장 in M97's 등장하다, 해당 in M86's 해당하다, 적용 in M64's 적용하다, 비율's 率 in M97's 성장, and 덕 in M31's 덕수궁. Two words carry two NIKL rows (무리, and the 실체/실태 pair share a 實) — read those by the row, as M99 taught.

Twelve of the twenty need no new character at all; the rest need exactly one. Then the drawers:

- **WHERE AND WHEN (정면 중반 직전 최종 대륙 중부 양국 동포 등장):** the front face (정면 — correctness-正 on face-面), the middle stone (중반 — the 盤 of M46's 초반, whose note already wrote "초반/중반/후반"), the seconds before (직전 — M88's 직후 flipped), the last of the line (최종 — M32's 최근's 最 on M38's 종일's 終), the land the sea cannot surround (대륙, against M22's 섬), the forecaster's middle (중부 — the compass syllable M98's 남부 left frozen), the diplomat's pair (양국 — the both-兩 M63's 양 note filed), the same-womb nation (동포 — beside M52's 교포), and the entrance as a noun (등장 — M97's 등장하다 dismounted).
- **HOW MUCH (실체 적용 비율 변동 추가 지출 확보 제약 무리 극히 궁극적 불가피하다):** the shelf the report measures with. 실체 is what a thing really is; 적용 is M64's 적용하다 as a noun; 비율 lands the 率 M97's 성장 left on credit; 변동 is change on a chart where M32's 변화 is change in a life; 추가 is the line the bill grows by; 지출 is the second column beside M43's 수입; 확보 is having got it and holding on; 제약 is the rope, where M64's 제한 is the ceiling; 무리 is both overdoing it (無理) and a crowd (native) — two NIKL rows; 극히 is the report's very; 궁극적 is the last analysis; and 불가피하다 is three taught halves (不 可 避) and no new character at all.
- **THE HEART (자존심 애정 정성 자극 항의 덕 향 소음 생물 근원 실태 해당 대신하다):** what the essay feels with. 자존심 gets 상하다-ed; 애정 is love you can show or withhold; 정성 is the care the 제사 table is set with (M94's 들이다 spent it frozen); 자극 is the needle — food, ambition, news; 항의 is 반대 said out loud; 덕 is the virtue M31's 덕수궁 and M69's 덕분 were built on; 향 is the incense M41's 향기 decoded; 소음 is the sound ruled against; 생물 is the living thing that covers M35's 식물 and M22's 동물; 근원 is the root under the causes; 실태 is the state that gets surveyed, where M86's 실정 pleads; 해당 is the form's THE; and 대신하다 is M39's 대신 with 하다 welded on.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 무리 is 無理 when you overdo it and native when it is a crowd — and M39's 아무리 is neither · 제약 is 制約 the constraint; the drug company's 제약 is 製藥, and NIKL lists only the first · 덕 is 德 virtue; S1's 덕후 landed a Japanese syllable on it by accident · 향 is 香 incense here, 鄕 home in M27's 고향, 向 facing in M31's 방향 — three 향s, one burns · 애정 is 愛 + 情, not M47's 애 (the kid) + 정 · 궁극적's 궁 is 窮 exhaustion, not the 宮 of M23's 궁 · 자극's 자 is 刺 a needle, not M71's 자 nor the 自 of 자존심 · 실체 and 실태 share 實 and split on the second half: 體 the body, 態 the posture · and 근원's 원 is 源 the spring — not the 原 of M86's 원인, not M9's 원.

**Calibration, two ladders.** Change: 바꾸다 (M9) — you swap it · 변화 (M32) — it becomes different · 변동 (today) — the number moves. Before: 전에 (M13) — any time before · 직전 (today) — the moment before · 직후 (M88) — the moment after.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 ending, named in its note — -(으)ㄴ 탓에 from M86 blames the 층간 소음 for an 항의 전화; -(으)ㄹ 정도로 from M89 measures how far a 자존심 got hurt 회의 직전에; -더라고요 from M88 reports what 삼십 대 중반 did to 지출 and 수입; 에 의해 from M90 hands the 최종 비율 to 양국 정부; -기는커녕 from M92 drops from 추가 자리 확보 to no ticket at all; -더라도 from M95 concedes a taste for 자극적인 음식 before 무리하지 마세요; -다 보니 from M94 keeps surveying the 동포 실태 until the 근원 shows; and -기 마련이다 from M87 promises that an 극히 작은 변동 will reach the price. Underneath, the frames you have held since Ring 1: the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24, -(으)니까 from M10, the comparison 보다 from M22, -지 마세요 from M14, the -기 nominalizer from M13, and 도 from M2 sitting where the case particle sat.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the line every clinic ends on (무리하지 마세요), the hurt Korean names before anger (자존심 상하다), and the adverb on the home-style restaurant's wall (정성껏).

## What's next

Band 8 keeps unspooling the Grade-C list by rank and by root, and the shelf you built today is the method for the rest of it: find the half you own, learn the half you don't, and read the compound as a sentence of two characters. No new endings — the grammar is finished and the words are the work. 궁극적으로, 극히 드문 일이 아니에요: you have read thirty-four abstract nouns and recognized most of them on sight. 다음 모듈로 갑시다.
