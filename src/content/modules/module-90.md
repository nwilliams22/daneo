# Korean, Word-First — Module 90: State and Society

The news is written in the passive. Nobody in a Korean headline does anything: a law IS PASSED, a result IS DECIDED, damage IS SUFFERED — and the four frames this module teaches are how the report names who did what without ever saying so. M32 turned on the television and gave you 정치, 정부, 사회, 국민, 대통령 — the abstract floor. This module gives you the rest of the state: the INSTITUTIONS (기업 국회 정당 법원 기관 기구 조직 집단 민간), the PEOPLE inside them (의원 장관 후보 위원 위원장 주민 백성 담당), the LAW (법률 판결 재판 규정 권리 통제 죄 범죄 폭력), and the PUBLIC that pushes back (선거 시위 여론 공식 명예 의도 민주주의 민주화 지배하다). Thirty-five words, all Grade C, and the glue that runs them: **-고자** (the formal intention), **에 따라 / 에 따르면** (according to, depending on), **에 의해 / 에 의한** (by — the agent, at last), and **-(으)ㄴ 결과** (as a result of). The grammar of no one in particular.

**How to use this:** decode first — thirty-two of these words are two-hanja compounds and you own at least one half of nearly every one (國 會 政 法 機 民 判 決 規 權 力 選 意 主 化). Then read Parts 2–5 as one lesson: four frames, each with a spoken twin you already have (-(으)려고, 에 따르면, 한테, 노력의 결과), each promoted to print. The sentence deck is eight headlines; read them as a newspaper, not as a drill.

## Part 1 — Four shelves, one building

Every shelf here pays a debt. M64's 대기업 decoded 企業 before 기업 existed; M58's 국회의원 decoded 國會議員 "end to end" and left 국회 and 의원 waiting; M78's 외교관 note carried 장관 frozen; M69's 행사 filed 권리를 행사하다 with 권리 frozen; M46's 앞두다 wrote 선거를 앞두고; M33's 상황 and M85's 따라서 both pointed at 에 따라서; and M56's 의하다 taught 에 의하면 and promised 에 의해. Today they all land.

- **The institutions (기업 국회 정당 법원 기관 기구 조직 집단 민간):** 기업 is the firm as the economy sees it (회사 is the mouth's); 국회 the Assembly; 정당 the party; 법원 the court; 기관 an agency (machinery with a gate); 기구 an organization OR an apparatus — NIKL lists both 機構 and 器具 and the note sorts them; 조직 the body you are IN; 집단 a group with a boundary; 민간 everything that is not the state.
- **The people (의원 장관 후보 위원 위원장 주민 백성 담당):** elected (의원), appointed (장관, 위원, 위원장), running (후보), living there (주민), ruled from a throne (백성 — the sageuk's word for 국민), and in charge of your file (담당).
- **The law (법률 판결 재판 규정 권리 통제 죄 범죄 폭력):** 법률 is the law as written; 규정 the rule with a document number; 재판 what happens in the 법원, 판결 what comes out; 권리 what you hold, 통제 what is held over you; and the three wrongs — 죄 (the sin), 범죄 (the crime), 폭력 (the force).
- **The public (선거 시위 여론 공식 명예 의도 민주주의 민주화 지배하다):** how the people answer — the ballot (선거), the street (시위), the poll (여론) — and the words the answer is argued in: 공식 (official), 명예 (honour), 의도 (intent), 민주주의 (the creed), 민주화 (the becoming), 지배하다 (the verb a state fears).

**Radar patrol:** 민주주의's 주의 is 主義 (-ism), not the 注意 (caution) of S2's 주의 · 정당 the party ≠ 정당하다 fair (正當, frozen) · 폭력 is 暴力, not M60's 폭 (幅, width) · 후보's 후 is 候 (wait), not the 後 of M13's 후에 · 여론's 여 is 輿 (the crowd), not M78's 여 (女) nor the 旅 of 여행 · 기구 is 機構 when you join it, 器具 when you lift it · 죄 is the 罪 M4's 죄송합니다 carried undecoded · 집단's 집 is 集, not M1's 집 · and 의도 shares no character with the 여의도 M58 parked the Assembly on.

## Part 2 — The glue: -고자 (하다), the formal intention

M50 gave you intention out loud and set the register in one line: "-(으)려고 is what mouths actually say". -고자 is the same intention in a suit: verb stem + 고자, no vowel/consonant fuss (하고자, 먹고자, 살고자), then either a clause — 문제를 해결하고자 새 법을 만들었다 = made a new law in order to solve the problem (M7's 문제, M56's 해결하다) — or 하다 for the standalone plan: 말씀드리고자 합니다 (M71's 말씀드리다) = I wish to say, the opener of every speech, and 발표하고자 합니다 = we intend to announce. Nobody says -고자 across a table; every press release, every thesis introduction (본 연구는 … 살펴보고자 한다 — 본 the this-, frozen), every minister at a podium does. Same machine as M50's, different paperwork — and the -기 위해(서) from M44 sits between them: 위해서 for the vision board, -고자 for the gazette.

## Part 3 — The glue: 에 따라(서) / 에 따르면 — according to, depending on

M32's 따르다 (to follow; to pour) has been living in one frame since M32 — "[source]에 따르면 = according to" — and M33's 상황 note filed the other one early: "상황에 따라서 (M32's 따르다) = depends on the situation". Today both are yours. **[noun]에 따라(서)** = in accordance with, depending on: 법에 따라 (M39's 법) by the law, 규정에 따라 by the regulation, 나이에 따라 (M4's 나이) depending on age, 경우에 따라서는 (M32's 경우) in some cases — the 서 drops in print. **[source]에 따르면** = according to: 여론 조사에 따르면, 정부에 따르면, and the shape the M86 sentence s_m86_research_effect already ran, 연구 결과에 따르면, usually with the quoting -다고 하다 from M32 closing the sentence so the writer asserts nothing. M85's 따라서 note drew the line that keeps the two 따라서s apart — "[noun]에 따라서 mid-sentence follows" — while 따라서 alone at the head of a sentence concludes. The 에 의하면 frame from M56 is 에 따르면 in full hanja dress; use either.

## Part 4 — The glue: 에 의해(서) / 에 의한 — by (the agent)

The passive party of M36 taught the verbs — 잡히다, 열리다, 보이다 — and gave the agent its spoken particle: M36's 잡히다 note ran 경찰한테 잡혔어요 and said of M27's 한테 that it "picks up the 'by' job". Speech mostly drops the agent altogether. Print names it, and M56's 의하다 note said how: "[agent]에 의해(서) = by, the passive agent of print". So: 법원에 의해 결정되다 (M70's 결정되다) = decided by the court; 경찰에 의해 발견되다 (M61's 발견되다) = discovered by the police; 국민에 의해 선택된 (M33's 선택하다 through the 되다-row, in the badge) = chosen by the people. Wear the badge -(으)ㄴ from M24 and the agent modifies a noun: **에 의한** + noun — 폭력에 의한 피해 = damage caused by violence, 정부에 의한 통제. RADAR against M86: 로 인해 / 로 인한 files a CAUSE (사고로 인한 피해 — the accident made it happen); 에 의해 / 에 의한 files a DOER (경찰에 의해 — someone did it). A typhoon gets 로 인해; a government gets 에 의해.

## Part 5 — The glue: -(으)ㄴ 결과 — as a result of

M32's 결과 ("結果 tie-fruit") has taken nouns since M32 — M39's 노력 note wrote 노력의 결과. Today it takes a clause: verb + the past badge -(으)ㄴ from M24 + 결과, no particle after it — 조사한 결과 (M56's 조사하다) = as a result of investigating, 노력한 결과 (M26's 노력하다) = as a result of the effort, 확인한 결과 (M20's 확인하다) = on checking. The report's favourite hinge: what was done, comma, what came of it — and the second half is usually a 되다-verb or an 없다, because the report did not do it either. Radar: -(으)ㄴ 나머지 from M86 is a result of an EXCESS (너무 기쁜 나머지); -(으)ㄴ 결과 is a result of a PROCEDURE.

## Part 6 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** the quoting -다고 하다 from M32 closes the opinion poll under 에 따르면; the 합니다체 from M4 puts a minister's -고자 하다 in its podium voice; M70's 결정되다 takes the court as its 에 의해 agent; M86's 피해 sits under 에 의한 and the badge -(으)ㄴ from M24 carries both -(으)ㄴ 결과 sentences; the double-subject frame reports 죄가 없었어요; and M35's 이루어지다 gives 민주화 its happen-form.
