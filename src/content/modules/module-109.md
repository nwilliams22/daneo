# Korean, Word-First — Module 109: The Flip Machine, and the Loanword Shelf II

By now the passive is arithmetic. Thirty-four Grade-C words, and twenty-six of them are a machine you already own being run one more time on a root you already own: ten 되다 flips on nouns from M33 to M108, five 히/기 products on native verbs from the M36 party, and eleven Sino verbs that are a noun plus 하다 and nothing else. M70's 마련되다 wrote the licence for the first column in one line — "되다 doesn't check pedigree, only the 하다 it replaces" — and that md called the result exactly what it is, "the tense of committees, hotels, and headlines". So the notes below are receipts rather than lessons: ten of today's entries were already riding frozen inside somebody else's note, and each one quotes the line that promised it. The eighth drawer is the other shelf M79 opened — eight more loanwords the office and the clinic use, each filed beside the Korean word it did NOT replace.

**How to use this:** do not learn the flips one at a time. Take the table below as a single exercise — say the noun, say the 하다 side, say the 되다 side, then ask who disappeared — and the ten are one word with ten bodies. The party products take the same treatment against their parents (적다/적히다, 뽑다/뽑히다, 쫓다/쫓기다), and two of the five parents are frozen verbs you meet only through their products, which is the M36 lesson holding: "There's no rule — only membership." For the loans, spend the reps on the NEIGHBOUR, not the spelling — every one of them has a Korean twin doing the job it left alone.

## Part 1 — The flip machine, and the loanword shelf II

**The flip, run ten times.** The noun is yours already; only the ending moves.

| 요구 | 요구하다 (M56) | 요구되다 — is called for | M86 |
| 오염 | none — 오염시키다 | 오염되다 — is contaminated | M99 |

| 지속 | 지속하다 (frozen) | 지속되다 — persists | M100, inside 지속적 |

Two rows have no 하다 side at all, and the gap is worth a minute: nothing 오염한다 or 확산한다 in Korean, so the active job goes to -시키다, the machine M37 named — "Attach it to any 하다-noun and someone else is doing the work." One column names a doer, the other does not, and Korean officialdom lives in the third column.

**The party products, beside their parents.** Five native verbs from the M36 guest list, each filed next to the verb it flips: 적다 → **적히다** (the name is written down), 뽑다 (M61) → **뽑히다** (is elected), 쫓다 (M104) → **쫓기다** (is chased), 가두다 → **갇히다** (is shut in), 꼽다 → **꼽히다** (is counted as). Three of the parents are frozen — 적다 in its write-it-down sense, 가두다, 꼽다 — which is the party's oldest rule showing through: you meet some of these verbs only from the far end, where the thing happens and nobody is named.

**The Sino verbs.** Eleven noun-plus-하다 verbs, and eight of the eleven decode entirely from characters you own: 전개하다 (M91's 전개), 창조하다 (M101's 창조), 작성하다 (作 of M48's 작가 + 成 of M35's 성공하다), 강요하다 (强 of M64's 강제 + 要 of M56's 요구하다), 부담하다 (M86's 부담), 진출하다 (M75's 진출), 통과하다 (通 of M31's 통일 + 過 of M33's 사과하다), 상징하다 (象 of M41's 인상 + 徵 of M45's 특징). Only three bring genuinely new characters: 파괴하다 (破壞), 모색하다 (摸索) and 면하다 (免).

**The loans, and their twins.** M79 said it in one line, and that md's thesis is restated here: "every loanword in Korean lives next to a Korean word it did NOT replace." Learn the pair, not the word.

**Radar patrol:** 적히다's parent 적다 (to write down) is not M7's 적다 (to be few), and neither is M45's 적 — the -적 stamp, and the enemy standing alone · 면하다 is 免, a fourth hanja on a syllable where M99's 면 already holds three (the face-面, the township, the cotton-綿) · 모색 is not M99's 모 plus M7's 색, however loudly lookup --comp says so · 디스크 is two words in one loan, the drive and the injury, and Korean names the injury after the cartilage · 코스 is not M14's 코 with a 스 on the end, and 장르 is not M30's 장, and 발레 is not M14's 발 — three loans that borrow a syllable you own and mean nothing by it · 하드웨어 is never the hardware store, which is 철물점 (frozen) · 파괴하다 has no relation to M40's 파, the scallion · and 창조하다 makes what was not there while M3's 만들다 makes things out of materials.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** eight sentences, eight Band 7 patterns, one apiece and named in every note — 에 의해 from M90 puts the factory back into an 오염된 river, which is the only way print names an agent; -(으)나 from M85 sets 요구되다 against 제시되다 in one written breath and names nobody twice; -(으)ㄹ 만하다 from M87 rates a film worth being 꼽히다 in its 장르; -더라고요 from M88 finds a friend 갇히다 in a lift; 조차 from M89 finds that not even the name was 적히다 on a form somebody 작성하다-ed; -(으)로 인해 from M86 files 오염 as the cause of a 질병 that 확산되다; -다시피 from M91 opens the line in which a 법률 finally 통과하다-s M90's 국회; and -(으)ㄴ/는 데다가 from M92 stacks a small 디스크 on an old 하드웨어. Underneath run the frames you have held since Ring 1: the long negation -지 않다 from M28, the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24, the double-subject frame M24 first showed, the honorific -(으)시- from M4, and 로 and 에서 doing their ordinary work.

Twelve of today's words are in the deck, and five of the eight sentences close on a verb that names nobody at all. That is not a stylistic choice; it is what this module is about.

**Gap deck:** the pursuer every Korean office reports to (시간에 쫓기다), the loan that names an injury instead of a body part (허리 디스크), and the meal that arrives in an order somebody else chose (코스 요리).

Band 8 keeps unspooling the Grade-C list. Today's frozen riders are next quarter's entries — 가두다 and 꼽다 are still waiting for their own rows, 확산 and 상징 and 강요 are nouns the list has not reached yet, and the loan shelf has more floors.
