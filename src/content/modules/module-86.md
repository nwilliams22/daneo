# Korean, Word-First — Module 86: Cause and Grounds

Korean builds its prepositions out of verbs. "Because of" is a verb (인하다), "compared to" is a verb (비하다), "despite" is a verb (불구하다), "including" is a verb (비롯하다) — each one a dictionary entry that nobody ever conjugates, because each lives inside a single frame: 로 인해, 에 비해, 에도 불구하고, 을 비롯해. Once you see the verb inside the preposition, the newspaper opens. This module is the written register's cause-machinery: four grammar-verbs, the have-it-done-to-you verb 당하다, eight GROUNDS nouns (원인 근거 바탕 탓 영향 작용 결론 필요성), five words for "the situation" sorted by register (사정 형편 처지 실정 여건), eleven PRESSURE nouns (피해 부담 압력 위기 우려 반응 요구 대책 방안 and the verbs 해당하다/의존하다) — and the causal endings that Ring 2 never owned: -는 바람에, -느라고, -(으)ㄴ 탓에/덕분에, -(으)ㄴ 나머지. Thirty-one words, all Grade C, and a dozen of them are IOUs an earlier note wrote.

**How to use this:** read Part 2 first if you read Korean news at all — the four frames are the highest-frequency grammar on the C-list and you have been paraphrasing around them for thirty modules. Then take the nouns in shelves: the grounds words pair with verbs you own (미치다, 이르다, 마련하다), the circumstance shelf is one word sorted five ways, and the pressure shelf is what the headline does to you. Part 3 finishes the because-family M10 opened.

## Part 1 — The nouns: grounds, circumstances, pressure

Every noun here is a receipt. M72's 발생 note wrote 발생 원인 with 원인 riding frozen; M61's 미치다 filed 영향을 미치다 the same way; M81's 부작용 decoded 副 + 作用 before 작용 existed; M38's 이르다 told you to recognize 결론에 이르다 and not chase it; M57's 가능성 and M81's 중요성 left 필요성 as the last thing on the -성 belt; M40's 밥솥 named the 압력밥솥; M56's 마련하다 and M70's 마련되다 both carried 대책 frozen; M78's 강도 rode 당하다; and 탓 was owed twice — by the M63 gap deck and by M69's 조상. Today they all land.

- **The grounds shelf (원인 근거 바탕 탓 영향 작용 결론 필요성):** 원인 is the cause that IS (M24's 이유 is the reason you give); 근거 is what an argument stands on; 바탕 is the ground you build on; 탓 is the cause with blame in it; 영향 is a shadow and an echo; 작용 is the working itself; 결론 is the argument tied off; 필요성 is need, bottled.
- **The circumstance shelf, sorted against M33's 상황:** 상황 is neutral (how it looks) · **사정** is personal (why — the one you explain) · **형편** is means (whether you can pay) · **처지** is plight (where you stand, usually badly) · **실정** is the audit (what is actually the case, no person in it) · **여건** is the givens (what you have to work with).
- **The pressure shelf:** what the world does to you — 피해 you suffer, 부담 you carry, 압력 you are under, 위기 you weather, 우려 the editorial has about it, 반응 you give back, 요구 that gets sent to you, 대책 and 방안 that answer it, and 해당하다/의존하다 for which case fits and what you lean on.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 원인 ≠ M9's 원 (won) — a cause is never a price · 피해 ≠ M14's 피 (blood) + M11's 해 (sun) — only harm in it · 비하다 ≠ M11's 비 (rain) — only the 에 keeps 비에 비해 apart · 대책 ≠ M1's 책 (book), though 策 was once a bamboo slip · 여건 ≠ M78's 여 (female-) and ≠ the 旅 of 여행 · 사정's 정 is 情, the jeong of M26, NOT the 政 of M32's 정치 · 위기's 기 is 機, the pivot, not the 氣 of M26's 분위기 · 방안 ≠ M8's 방 + M6's 안 · 반응's 응 is 應, not M71's 응 · and 근거's 거 is 據, not M17's 것.

## Part 2 — The glue: four verbs that are really prepositions

M32 gave you the first frame-verbs — 대하다 ("You'll almost never conjugate it raw — it lives in the frame [noun]에 대해서 (about)"), 위하다, 통하다 — and M56 added 의하다. Today the shelf gets four more, and every one of them works the same way: a Sino root + 하다, frozen in -아/어(서) or in the badge -(으)ㄴ from M24, sitting after a noun and its particle.

| Verb | Frame | Meaning | Spoken twin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 인하다 (因) | [cause]으로 **인해(서)** / 인한 + noun | because of, owing to | [noun] 때문에 (M29's 때문) |
| 비하다 (比) | [standard]에 **비해(서)** / 비하면 | compared to | [noun]보다 (M22's 보다-particle) |
| 불구하다 (不拘) | [obstacle]에도 **불구하고** | despite | -아/어도 from M39; M39's 그래도 |
| 비롯하다 | [chief example]을/를 **비롯해(서)** / 비롯한 + noun | including, starting with | [noun] 같은 (M24's 같다) |

Read the column on the right: every frame has a spoken version you already own, and the difference is register — 눈 때문에 is what you say, 폭설로 인해 is what the sign says (폭설 — heavy snow, riding frozen). The badge forms do the same work in front of a noun: 사고로 인한 피해, 서울을 비롯한 대도시 (M50's 대도시). And 비롯하다 has a 되다-twin, 비롯되다 — [source]에서 비롯된 = stemming from — which is the verb the cause-shelf sits on. The fifth verb, 당하다, is not a preposition but a passive: the passive party of M36 bolted 이/히/리/기 onto verbs; 당하다 bolts onto a NOUN — 사고를 당하다, 피해를 당하다 — Korean's get-[noun]-ed, which is why the report prefers it.

## Part 3 — The glue: the causal endings, and the full because-family

- **-는 바람에** — because (something unexpected happened), and it went badly: 버스를 놓치는 바람에 늦었어요 (M6's 버스, M12's 놓치다). M11's 바람 (wind) in a dependent-noun job — in the wind of the bus leaving — and it is always a mishap; you cannot pass an exam -는 바람에.
- **-느라고** — because (I was busy doing), same subject, a cost paid: 공부하느라고 못 잤어요. Verb stems only, the speaker's own activity, and almost always ending in something that did not get done; clipped to 하느라 in speech.
- **-(으)ㄴ/-는 탓에 / -(으)ㄴ 덕분에** — blame and credit. 늦게 잔 탓에 늦었어요 files a fault; 도와준 덕분에 합격했어요 hands out the credit. M69's 덕분 note fixed the polarity — "[Person] 덕분에 = thanks to them, and it ONLY credits" — and 탓 is its mirror: it only blames. Between them, M29's 때문 (its note: "credit or blame; tone decides").
- **-(으)ㄴ 나머지** — as a result of (an excess of): 너무 기쁜 나머지 울었어요 = so happy that she cried. M57's 나머지 (the rest, remainder) as a dependent noun: the remainder of having felt too much is the thing you did.

| Ending | Register | Attitude | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| -아/어서 | spoken, neutral | never before a command | Ring 1 |
| -(으)니까 | spoken | the one before an order | M10 |
| -기 때문에 | written, formal | objective | M84 |
| -(으)므로 | written, formal | the essay's | M85 |
| [noun]으로 인해 | written | a cause on file | this module |
| -는 바람에 | spoken | a mishap | this module |
| -느라고 | spoken | busy, and paid for it | this module |
| -(으)ㄴ 탓에 / 덕분에 | both | blame / credit | this module |
| -(으)ㄴ 나머지 | written | an excess | this module |

The rule M10 wrote still governs the top rows — "if the sentence ends in 주세요/-(으)세요/-(으)ㄹ까요, the reason takes **니까**" — and none of today's endings can precede a command either: a mishap, a cost, a blame, or an excess is a report, not an instruction.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** M8's 입다 wears damage instead of clothes under 로 인해; M44's 합격 verbs itself again behind 에도 불구하고; M38's 훨씬 finishes the comparison 에 비해 opens; -아/어지다 from M35 and the double-subject frame carry the 탓에 sentence; 도 from M2 does its even-job on the lunch -느라고 cost; the 되다 frame from M10 takes a whole badge-clause as its complement around 당하다 and 의존하다; -는 것 from M17 wraps what gets given up after -(으)ㄴ 나머지; and 에 따르면 with the quoting -다고 하다 from M32 carry 영향을 미치다 in a full health headline.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the proverb's second half, quoted alone as an accusation (못되면 조상 탓), the adjective that means no-shape-at-all (형편없다), and the request that asks bureaucracy to look at your circumstances (사정을 봐주다).
