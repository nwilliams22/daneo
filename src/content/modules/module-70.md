# Korean, Word-First — Module 70: The Becoming Machine

Module 35 built the change machine and kept it small: -아/어지다 on adjectives ("this module keeps to adjectives; the party is next door"). Module 36 threw the party and, at the door, pointed at a tail of guests it had no chairs for — "recognize them and nod." Today the tail graduates. Twenty-eight verbs, zero new machinery: eleven adjectives you have owned since Ring 1 ride the **conveyor** (높다 → 높아지다, 싫다 → 싫어지다), eight verbs go through the **-지다 wing** (쏟다 → 쏟아지다, 정하다 → 정해지다), five 하다-verbs take the **되다-row** swap M36 named by name (준비되다, 포함되다), three party guests M36 said to nod at get seats (읽히다, 먹히다, 섞이다), and one causative that looks exactly like a passive (벗기다) tests whether you read invitations. Every word here is a parent you already know plus a machine you already own.

**How to use this:** the notes are the module. Each one names the parent, the parent's owner, and the conjugation the parent forces — the ㅂ that surfaces in 짧아지다, the 워 that survives in 가까워지다, the silent ㅎ in 많아지다 — so drill the parent's polite form first and the change-verb is free. Then spend your reps on the three radar pairs: 작아지다 (size) vs 적어지다 (amount), 끊어지다 (snaps by itself) vs M36's 끊기다 (cut off by something), and 벗기다 (the M37 party) vs the 기 passives it impersonates.

## Part 1 — Three machines, one policy

Korean would rather tell you what happened than who did it, and this module is that policy at scale:

- **The conveyor (-아/어지다 from M35, on adjectives):** take the polite -아/어 form, weld 지다 on. Whatever the parent does to its stem, the change-verb inherits — 넓어요 → 넓어지다 (both ㄼ letters awake, M13), 어두워요 → 어두워지다 (the ㅂ-irregular family, M13), 익숙해요 → 익숙해지다 (the workhorse M42's 익숙하다 promised). Pace it with 점점 (M35); stack the -고 있다 progressive from M8 on top.
- **The -지다 wing (the same machine, on verbs):** M36 named it while seating 깨지다, 켜지다, 꺼지다, 사라지다. You 쏟다 the cup; the rain 쏟아지다-s. You 정하다 the date; the date 정해지다-s. 세워지다 pays the M37 decode of 세우다 (서다 + 이우 — "parking is making the car stand"), and 이뤄지다 is M35's 이루어지다 with a syllable squeezed out.
- **The 되다-row (swap the 하다):** "swap the 하다, get the happen-form" — the M36 line, M53's 걱정되다 receipt, the M61 graduating class (발견되다, 진행되다, 사용되다). Today: 준비되다, 결정되다, 포함되다, 마련되다, 소개되다 — the tense of committees, hotels, and headlines.

## Part 2 — The bricks

Read the shelf in its four rows — eleven conveyor verbs, eight wing verbs, five 되다-row verbs, four party guests — and read each note for its parent before its meaning: the parent's polite form is already the first half of the word.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 작아지다 shrinks a SIZE (M1's 작다), 적어지다 shrinks an AMOUNT (M7's 적다) — two Ring 1 adjectives, one letter apart, and the conveyor keeps them apart. 끊다 (M20) has two passives: M36's 끊기다 is cut off BY something (전화가 끊겼어요), 끊어지다 snaps on its own (줄이 끊어졌어요) — the party's twin-invitation case. And 벗기다 is the trap: the same 기 that made 끊기다 done-to makes 벗기다 make-do (M37's 맡기다, 남기다, 숨기다 row) — membership, not rule, exactly as both parties warned.

## Part 3 — The guests, seated

### 1. The three M36 said to nod at

The M36 md filed 먹히다 ("get eaten — the food chain's verb") and 읽히다 ("get read") as guests "covered by their neighbors' notes." Seated today: **먹히다** runs the food chain and the idiom 말이 안 먹혀요 (my words don't get through); **읽히다** reviews books — 잘 읽히는 책 wears the badge from M24 — and doubles as the M37 causative (아이한테 책을 읽히다 = make the child read; the M37 md: "읽히다, 씻기다 — same shape, both parties"); **섞이다** is M40's 섞다 turned around: 물과 기름은 안 섞여요. Say them with the sound rules on — 읽혀요 is 일켜요, 먹혀요 is 머켜요, 섞여요 is 서껴요.

### 2. The one that looks like a passive

**벗기다** = M21's 벗다 + 기, and it is MAKE-do: 아이 옷을 벗기다 (undress the child), 사과 껍질을 벗기다 (peel the apple — M40's 껍질 comes off because someone took it off). The M37 md listed the bedtime routine 눕히다, 씻기다, 신기다 as table-only guests; 벗기다 is the step before all three.

**Calibration:** the conveyor and the wing are everyday speech — 점점 추워져요, 비가 쏟아져요, 날짜가 정해졌어요 belong at any table. The 되다-row is what the M61 md called "formal furniture": natural in print and on microphones, stiff at dinner — with one exception everyone says, 준비됐어요?

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** the [noun]이/가 되다 frame from M35 opens winter under the -(으)면 from M20; the -는데 hinge from M53 swings a friendship from far to near; the -고 있다 progressive from M8 counts the learners; M36's 젖다 takes the -아/어 버리다 tail from M52 after the sky opens; M9's 주다 in its do-for-me job asks for a book; the M37 order machine (목욕을 시키다) finishes the bedtime routine; and M39's 아무리 with -아/어도 fails to stir M40's 젓다 into mixing oil and water.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the proverb that runs 멀어지다 twice (눈에서 멀어지면 마음에서도 멀어진다 — out of sight, out of mind) and the downpour that is sleep (잠이 쏟아지다).
