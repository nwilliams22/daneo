# Korean, Word-First — Module 82: Out of Doors

M22 handed you the landscape (강, 나무, 새, 물고기) and M49 walked you into it (빛, 나뭇잎, 호랑이, 곰, 토끼). This module adds a third floor to both: thirty-two native words for the world outside the door, in three drawers. The WELDS — 강물, 바닷물, 빗물, 나뭇가지, 달빛, 뒷산, 새소리, 꽃잎, 산속, 불꽃, 동물원, 줄기, 먼지 — are nature named by joining parts you own, most of them with the linking-ㅅ. The ANIMALS AND FLOWERS — 쥐, 뱀, 개구리, 여우, 나비, 오리, 원숭이, 사자, 코끼리, 개나리, 진달래 — each arrive with the proverb or the folk tale they live in, and three of them pay IOUs frozen since M22, M42, and M59. THE WALK — 큰길, 네거리, 길가, 정거장, 이발소, 책방, 등산로, 이곳저곳 — is the route from the front door to the 뒷산. The organizing thesis: **native Korean names nature by welding and animals by their stories** — the machine M3's 김밥 note called "the name is the recipe" runs on water, light, and branches too, and every animal here is a proverb with legs.

**How to use this:** read the welds as arithmetic — twelve of them are two words you already own plus a wedge — and spend your reps on the pronunciation party the wedge throws (바단물, 빈물, 꼰닙, 뒫싼). Learn each animal WITH its idiom; the story is the mnemonic. Then walk the last nine in order, door to hill.

## Part 1 — The wedge that builds the outdoors

M49's 나뭇잎 was the template — "나무 + linking-ㅅ + 잎 (leaf — the bare word waits its own turn; today it rides the compound)" — and this shelf runs the same mortar four more times, and throws the ㄴ-party once without it:

- **The ㅅ-welds:** 바닷물 (from M11's 바다 + M1's 물), 빗물 (from M11's 비), 나뭇가지 (from M22's 나무 + a third 가지), 뒷산 (from M6's 뒤 + M11's 산) — the wedge M40's 고춧가루, M46's 오랫동안, and M80's 찻잔 all wore. It lands as a ㄷ and then does whatever the next consonant asks: nasalizes before ㅁ (바단물, 빈물), tenses before ㄱ/ㅅ (나묻까지, 뒫싼).
- **The ㄴ-insertion party:** 꽃잎 says 꼰닙 exactly as 나뭇잎 says 나문닙 — an ㄴ sneaks into 잎, and the consonant before it matches.
- **No wedge when the seam is closed:** 강물, 달빛, 새소리, 산속 join without ㅅ — the mortar only fits between two vowels, and 강, 달 already end in consonants.
- **The other factories keep running:** 산속 is the fifth product of the X-속 line M65 built from M28's 속; 불꽃 is M13's 불 + M7's 꽃; 동물원 pays M22's 동물 and sorts two 원s (the garden-園 of M6's 공원 vs the house-院 of 병원); 줄기 is M55's 줄 grown a syllable; 먼지 walks out of M11's 미세먼지.
- **The walk is arithmetic too:** 큰길 = M1's 크다 in the badge + M12's 길 (the 큰일 mold from M67); 네거리 = M5's 넷 + M27's 거리, the native twin of M23's 사거리; 길가 = 길 + the edge-가 of M23's 바닷가; 등산로 = M17's 등산 + the road-路 of M31's 종로; 이곳저곳 = M29's 이곳 doubled, the written third of M50's 곳곳/여기저기 set.

## Part 3 — Animals with their stories, flowers with their order

### 1. Every animal is a proverb with legs

M22's 물고기 note said "Korean names the animal by its destiny"; this shelf says it names them by their stories, and the stories were already half-taught. 쥐 arrives from the cramp in M41's 쥐다 note (다리에 쥐가 났어요) and the mouse hole in the M42 gap deck; 개구리 climbs out of the well in the M22 gap deck (우물 안 개구리); 원숭이 falls from the tree in the M59 gap deck (원숭이도 나무에서 떨어진다) — three frozen animals, thawed. The rest bring their own: 여우 charms and 곰 (M49) endures, 오리 holds out the wrong foot (오리발 내밀다), 뱀 slides over the wall, 코끼리 is a nose with an animal attached, and 사자 exists only in the 동물원 and the 라이온킹.

### 2. The zodiac band, and the spring order

Four of these animals are zodiac years — 쥐띠, 뱀띠, 원숭이띠, and M49's 토끼 before them (the 띠 M79's 벨트 note left frozen in 허리띠 stays frozen; the pattern is yours). And two are the calendar: Korea watches spring arrive in a fixed order — **개나리** (yellow, March — M49's 노랗다 told you the color) → **진달래** (pink, April — 김소월's poem) → M11's **벚꽃** (the two-week snowfall of 꽃잎). Say the sequence and you can read the bloom forecast.

**Calibration:** the linking-ㅅ is a habit, not a law — 새소리 and 강물 do without it, 바닷물 and 빗물 cannot. Do not add wedges by analogy; learn each weld as spelled and let the ear sort the pronunciation. And the animals are all counted with M5's 마리 — 오리 두 마리, 원숭이 세 마리 — never 개.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** -(으)면서 from M38 and 마다 from M31 walk the 등산로 with birdsong, the try-machine -아/어 보다 from M16 meets the 코끼리, -자마자 from M38 and -기 시작하다 from M15 open the 개나리 season, verb--고 from M10 and -(으)세요 from M9 count 두 정거장, the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24 and the passive party from M36 light the 달빛 over the 강물, -(으)니까 from M10 with -아/어도 from M39 turn the monkey's fall into comfort, 께서 from M47 sends 할아버지 to the 이발소, and -아/어 버리다 from M52 lets the 빗물 finish the 먼지.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the wrong foot held out in denial (오리발 내밀다), the fox-wife and the bear-wife (여우 같은 아내, 곰 같은 아내), and the contrary frog who cries when it rains (청개구리).
