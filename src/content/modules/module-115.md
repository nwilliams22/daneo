# Korean, Word-First — Module 115: The Nouns Dismount

A Korean noun is very often the verb with its 하다 taken off. You have been riding six of today's thirty-six words for modules without knowing it: M72's 활용하다, M64's 허용하다, M93's 극복하다, M74's 불편하다, M11's 건조하다 and M114's 탄생하다. Take the tail off each one and the noun is simply there — 활용, 허용, 극복, 불편, 건조, 탄생 — with no new sound and no new meaning to learn. That is the whole module: once the dismount is visible, the rest of the Grade-C long tail stops being a list of words and starts being a machine you already own.

**How to use this:** read the dismount table first and say each pair out loud, verb then noun, because the only thing you are adding is a grammatical slot. Then read the persons and the powers as three rails rather than five words — -자 makes a person, -력 makes a strength, -장 makes a chief, and all three have been running since Ring 1. Everything after that is hanja arithmetic on characters you already hold; the radar patrol at the end of Part 1 is where the traps are, and there are six of them.

**Radar patrol:** 이사장's 이사 is 理事, a board director — NOT M54's 이사, which that note decoded "移徙 move-migrate", and not one character is shared · 건조 is dry-乾 plus 燥, and M99 ran both halves for you — "M11's 건조하다 is dry-乾, M50's 건축 is build-建", with M14's 건강 as sound-健 · 표준's 표 is the signpost-標 M75's 표시하다 filed third, not M12's 표, the ticket-票 · 단편's 단 is short-短 from M39's 단점, NOT the 單 of M55's 단, which sat one entry away from the 편 it is now paired with · 심판 is the referee on the pitch and the judgement at the end of the world, and Korean does not feel the gap · 전반 sweeps a thing entire while 전반적 wears the M45 stamp, and a second 전반 is 前半, the first half of a match · 양심's 양 is good-良, not M63's 양, the amount-量 · 이자's 자 is child-子, not the person-者 that hires 생산자 and 기술자 on the shelf above.

**One more test, and it is the module's:** when a Korean noun you do not know ends in a syllable that could take 하다, try it. 활용하다, 허용하다, 극복하다, 탄생하다 all exist and you ride four of them already; 이자하다 and 쾌감하다 do not, and that failure tells you something real about the word — it is a THING, not an action. The dismount runs both directions.

**Calibration, two ladders.** Permission: M64's 허용하다 lets it through · 허용 is the thing let through · M110's 허가 is the permit an office stamps and hands you. Feeling: M7's 기분 is your inner weather for the day · 예감 is a feeling about what has not happened yet · 쾌감 is one spike, and it needs a cause.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 pattern and names it, and no two come from the same module — -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 grows 가치 as 공공 자료 gets more 활용; 에 따르면 from M90 lets a survey say 평균 수명 has risen 전국적으로; -(으)며 from M85 puts a 공장 taking on 기술자 and its 냉동 식품 생산 side by side, in the plain -다 of the page; 에 비해 from M86 measures this year's 거액의 이자 against 작년; -(으)ㄹ 듯하다 from M87 has even the 심판 acknowledging a 선수's 재능; -더라고요 from M88 reports the 교훈 of a 단편 소설 as something witnessed; -(으)ㄴ/는 데다가 from M92 piles fast 진급 on top of good 교양 교육; and 반면에 from M95 balances a taste for 도전 against a weak 신념. Underneath sit the frames you have held for months: the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24 inside three of the connectives, 도 from M2 sitting where the case particle sat, the -아/어지다 machine from M35 closing 커져요, and the 의 of possession doing quiet work in four places.

## What's next

Thirty-six words, and the expensive part of almost none of them was new. Six were verbs you already rode with the tail cut off; four more were written out inside somebody else's note and sent away frozen; the rest were two characters you owned, set next to each other. That is what the long tail of a language actually looks like from the inside — an inventory of recombinations, not an inventory of words — and it is why the dismount is worth keeping as a habit: when you meet a Korean noun you do not know, try putting 하다 on the end before you look it up. Band 8 carries on down the list, and the traps stay the same shape — a syllable is not a character, and only the whole word tells you which one you are holding. 오늘은 새 단어가 별로 없었던 셈이죠.
