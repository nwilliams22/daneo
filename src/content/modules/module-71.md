# Korean, Word-First — Module 71: The Spoken Floor

Module 19 built the register ladder and Module 29 opened the interjection drawer — 아, 아이고, 그래, 그럼. This module fills it with the noises Korean makes BETWEEN sentences: 야 (rank 821 on the frequency list, the highest-ranked word this app had not taught), 응, 자, 음, and the rest of the floor. Nothing here is hard to say; every one of them is easy to say to the wrong person. So the organizing idea is not meaning but WHO: each note tells you which floor the word lives on — the 반말 floor, the every-register floor-holders, the gear-shift adverbs, and the humble verbs that set the register in the first place.

**How to use this:** read the register line in each note before you read the meaning. 야 and 응 will stick after one drama episode; knowing that you may never send either upward is the actual lesson. Learn 새끼 for the kittens and for the warning, and learn 뵈다 with both of its dictionary entries — the sentence deck uses each once.

---

## Part 1 — Every noise has a floor

Sort the shelf by who may hear it and the module teaches itself:

- **The 반말 floor** — 야, 응, 으응, 어때, 그렇지, 아니야. M19's 반말 rules apply without exception: friends, juniors, kids, never upward. Most of these are subtraction — 어때요 minus 요, 아니에요 reshaped to 아니야 — the same removal Module 19 taught, now applied to the noises.
- **The floor-holders** — 자, 음, 글쎄, 어머, 하하, and 아뇨. Any register: they are aimed at the room or the event, not at a listener, so they carry no rank. (글쎄 is the exception — its 요 comes back upward.)
- **The gear-shifts** — 하여튼, 아무튼, 분명, 아마도, 깜빡, 자꾸만. Adverbs change topics and certainties, not relationships; M33's 어쨌든 gets its two siblings and the anyway-trio is complete.
- **The humble verbs** — 말씀드리다, 뵈다, 수고하다, plus the speech verbs 답하다 and 대화하다 and the after-work pair 한잔/한잔하다. Here direction is everything: 드리다 lowers you, -(으)시- raises them, and 수고하세요 may only travel sideways or down.

## Part 2 — The bricks

Radar first: three of these are homographs of things you own. The 야 of 야근하다 (M16) and 야구 (M17) is hanja; today's 야 is breath. The 음 of 음식 (M1) and 음악 (M2) is hanja; today's 음 is a hum. And 자 is neither 자다's stem nor the person-者 of 소비자 (M43) — it means nothing, and it moves a whole room.

::vocab::

**The no-ladder, complete:** 아니 (M29) among friends, 아니야 to close a question, 아뇨 upward, 아닙니다 in uniform — four shapes of one refusal, sorted by listener. **The anyway-trio:** 어쨌든 (M33, neutral), 하여튼 (a shade formal), 아무튼 (the mouth's favorite); all three share the -든/-튼 that M33's 어쨌든 note said was 'still in the mail' — it still is.

## Part 3 — The register machines

### 1. Subtraction — the floor drops when the 요 does

Module 19's rule, applied to the drawer: 어때요 → 어때, 글쎄요 → 글쎄, 그렇죠 → 그렇지. The bare -지 tag the M51 markdown filed ('반말 keeps just -지') is what turns M29's 그렇다 into 그렇지? — right? — and what lets you ask yourself 어떡하지? mid-sentence. Same words, one collar looser; the WHO does not change, only the shape.

### 2. Direction — 드리다 lowers, -(으)시- raises

Two humble verbs, one rule. 말씀 + M9's 드리다 = 말씀드리다: I speak, lowered. 말씀 + the honorific -(으)시- from M4 = 말씀하시다: you speak, honored. Same noun, opposite direction — 말씀드릴 게 있어요 goes up, 말씀하세요 invites down. And 뵈다/뵙다 are one humble verb in two coats: 뵈다 before vowel endings (봬요, 뵈러), 뵙다 before consonant endings (뵙겠습니다) — M47's 뵙다 note put it as 'Before vowels it writes 뵈-'; the dictionary calls it two verbs. Either way, it only ever goes up.

### 3. The 수고 rule

수고하다 rates effort, and rating is a superior's privilege: 수고하셨습니다 and 수고하세요 travel sideways (colleagues, shop staff, the delivery driver) and downward — never up at a boss. **Calibration:** leaving a senior, say 내일 뵙겠습니다; leaving an equal, 수고하세요; leaving a friend, M1's 가다 in its flattest coat: 간다.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** M67's 생각나다 loops on 자꾸만, which is M38's 자꾸 with M30's 만 bolted on; M15's 잊다 rides the -아/어 버리다 ending from M52 behind 깜빡, M47's 낳다 counts kittens with M5's 마리, M27's 말씀 collects its Ring 2 IOU through the prospective -(으)ㄹ, and M29's 그럼, 내일 봐요 gets promoted to 봬요.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the eye that sees nothing (눈에 뵈는 게 없다 — blind with rage, and the contracted 보이다 surviving in the wild), and the hedge that agrees with everything (글쎄 말이야 — tell me about it).
