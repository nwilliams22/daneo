# Korean, Word-First — Module 124: Routes, Resolve and Weather

A railway makes a route, weather can break it, and people decide how to respond. The ranked slice also supplies everyday actions and words from work; the notes keep their senses and register distinct.

**How to use this:** read the shelves, then check the word notes for the exact sense. Each sentence reuses grammar already learned in Ring 3; this pack teaches no new glue.

---

## Part 1 — Routes and responses

The route shelf pairs 철도 with 곧장 and 발자국. 홍수 and 가뭄 describe opposite water disasters. 한눈 and 바퀴 need particular care: one is a fixed glance phrase; the other is a counter for circuits in the listed sense.

**Decision shelf:** 결심 is resolve itself, while 개선되다 reports the resulting improvement. 건네다 moves a book or a first word between people; 요약하다 gives the short version afterward. 구속하다 and 어기다 speak to rules and their consequences.

**Earlier vocabulary:** 한눈 → M68; 건네다 → M68; 긁다 → M68; 내쉬다 → M68; 발자국 → M68; 뱉다 → M68. These words return in the examples as review; the 30 entries below are this pack’s new checklist.

**Keep the side shelf:** 게 is the animal, 생기 is vitality, and 총각 carries an older register. A ranked slice can place these beside work and weather without pretending they share a root.

**Radar patrol:** M57's 차이 → 차이점 · M34's 결심하다 → 결심 · M104's 개선하다 → 개선되다 · M98's 구속 → 구속하다. The pairs share a root but change the job the word does.
