# Korean, Word-First — Module 99: One Syllable, Many Rows

M55 opened the little-words drawer and confessed that 단 and 대 each carried two hanja — that note's verdict, "Three hanja under one syllable; context referees." — and M76 spent a whole radar patrol on 억, 정상, 통 and 부. This module makes the confession the thesis: **a Sino syllable is a row in a table, not a word.** NIKL's Grade-C list files thirteen one-syllable words here (면 선 골 꼴 기 평 건 조 현 동 올 모 이어), and most of them are two or three rows apiece — 면 is a side AND a township AND cotton, 기 is spirit AND a flag, 조 is an article of law AND a team. Around them sit three drawers the same rank filed: the -적 shelf the M45 factory stamps five more products onto (본격적 효과적 상대적 직접적 객관적, with their nouns 특성 개성 and the adjective 독특하다), the lab (체험 실험 화학 오염 체조 동작 부위 통증 영양), and the old house (제사 지혜 의복 공포 심정). Thirty-five words, no new grammar: every sentence recycles one Band 7 ending and names it.

**How to use this:** read the table before the shelf, and read the one-syllable words BY THE ROW — the way a dictionary does, 면05 then 면04 then 면07 — because the compound you meet next week will tell you which row it wants, and the bare syllable never will. Then spend your reps on the receipts: nine of today's words were already riding frozen in an earlier note (실험, 화학, 오염, 제사, 공포, 효과적, 특성, 꼴찌's 꼴, 노선's 선), and each note quotes the line that promised it. The -적 words obey the M45 md's hardware rule — -적이다, -적인, -적으로 — and four of the five live almost entirely in the third.

## Part 1 — One syllable, many rows

Thirteen syllables, and the list gives them twenty-two rows between them. Read across:

| syllable | row 1 | row 2 | row 3 | not on the list |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 면 | 面 a side (양쪽 면) | 面 a township (읍/면/동) | 綿 cotton (면 티셔츠) | 麵 the noodles of 라면/냉면 |
| 선 | 線 a line (선을 넘다) | | | 先 선생님 · 選 선수 · 鮮 조선 |
| 골 | goal, the loan (골을 넣다) | | | the native 골 of 골목/시골 |
| 꼴 | a shape; a sorry state (무슨 꼴이야) | | | 꼴찌, its tail |
| 기 | 氣 spirit (기가 죽다) | 旗 a flag (기를 들다) | | 技 기술 · 機 기계 |
| 평 | 坪 the floor unit (삼십 평) | 評 a review (평이 좋다) | | 平 평일/평화/평균 |
| 건 | 件 a case (사고 한 건) | | | 乾 건조하다 · 建 건축 · 健 건강 |
| 조 | 條 an article of law (제일 조) | 組 a group, counted (일 조) | 組 a team (우리 조) | 燥 건조하다 · 祖 조상 · 調 조사하다 |
| 현 | 現 current (현 정부) | | | 玄 현관 — and 現 현금, the same row |
| 동 | 同 the same (동 대학) | 棟 a building (백일 동) | | 洞 동네 · 東 동쪽 · 動 동물 |
| 올 | this year's (올 여름) | this year (올 들어) | | 올리다, a verb's stem |
| 모 | 某 a certain (모 대학) | 某 so-and-so (김 모 씨) | | 母 부모님 · 帽 모자 · 模 모양 |
| 이어 | following on (이어 말했다) | | | 다이어트, a converter accident |

Three rules run the table. The **dependent nouns** (평, 건, 조, 동 in their counting rows) take Sino numbers in front — the M76 md's rule, "Sino in front of every unit" — 삼십 평, 이십 건, 제일 조, 백일 동. The **determiners** (현, 동, 올, 모) stamp a noun from the front and never stand alone, like M55's 단 and M46's 옛. And the **homograph rule** is the one you already own from the M55 md's radar patrol — "one-syllable homographs, all mapped" — extended: when a bare syllable arrives, ask which compound it fell out of.

**The -적 shelf**, five more products off the M45 machine — that md: "Sino noun in, quality-word out — and the product is still a noun". 본격적 (本格 — M57's 기본 meets M44's 합격), 효과적 (M57's 효과 stamped, exactly as that note promised), 상대적 (M57's 상대; its opposite 절대적 rides frozen), 직접적 (M56's 직접; 간접적 rides frozen), 객관적 (the guest's view; 주관적 rides frozen). The nouns beside them: 특성 pays M45's 특징 note, 개성 is the unit-個 of 개인적 with the nature-性 of 여성, and 독특하다 is alone-獨 plus special-特 — the polite word for a strange taste.

**The lab** is nine Sino compounds and you own a half of every one: 체험 and 체조 share the body-體 of M41's 체중; 실험 shares the test-驗 of M33's 경험; 화학 is the becoming-化 of M32's 변화; 오염 is the shelf's one double-first (汚染); 동작 is M42's 감동 meeting M48's 작가; 부위 is M32's 부분 meeting M31's 위치; 통증 is M53's 두통 meeting M53's 증상; 영양 is M43's 경영 with a nourish-養. Three of these were promised: 실험 by M90's 기구, 화학 by M86's 작용 and 반응, 오염 by M92's 대기.

**The old house** is five words for what the grandparents keep: 제사 (the night rite M69's 조상 note left frozen — "keeps them fed year-round"), 지혜 (wisdom — what M44's 지식 becomes when used), 의복 (the museum's word for M7's 옷), 공포 (the terror M53's 치과 said the whole country shares), 심정 (the heart's state right now — between M19's 마음 and M42's 감정).

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 면 is 面, 面, 綿 on the list and 麵 in the bowl — four rows, one syllable · 기 is 氣 and 旗 here, 技 in 기술 and 機 in 기계 — the file M83's 국기 drew, "four hanja, one syllable, and the compound tells you which" · 평 is 坪 on the floor plan and 評 on the film page; 平 in 평일/평화/평균 is the third · 조 is 條 in the statute and 組 in the classroom; 燥 in 건조하다 is neither · 동 is 同 before a noun and 棟 after a number; 洞 on the street sign is the row NIKL forgot · 현 is 現 in 현 정부 and 玄 in 현관 — and 현금 is 現 too · 모 is 某, the anonymizer; 母 in 부모님 is a mother, not a mystery · 골 is a goal, 꼴 is a disgrace — the tense ㄲ is the whole difference · and 이어 is 잇다 in its -아/어 coat; the 이어 in 다이어트 is English.

**Calibration, three ladders.** Experience: 경험 (M33) — what you have had · 체험 (today) — what you signed up for · 실험 (today) — what you tested. Pain: 아프다 (M14) — what you say · 통증 (today) — what the chart says · 고통 (M53) — what the soul says. The heart: 마음 (M19) — the organ · 감정 (M42) — the category · 심정 (today) — its state at this moment.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 ending, named in its note — -(으)로 인해 from M86 blames 오염 for 올 여름; -았/었더니 from M88 finds the 통증 gone after the 체조 동작; -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 makes 화학 more interesting with every 실험; -(으)ㄴ/는 셈이다 from M87 reckons that 우리 조 won, 객관적으로; -(으)ㄴ 채 from M94 keeps the 의복 on through the 제사; -(으)ㄹ까 봐 from M93 hides a 심정 so a child's 기 will not die; 반면에 from M95 weighs 영양 against a 독특한 맛; and 에 따라 from M90 puts 현 정부 onto a 건, 본격적으로. Underneath, the frames you have held since Ring 1: the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24, -(으)면 from M20, the try-machine -아/어 보다 from M16, -아/어지다 from M35, the honorific -(으)시- from M4, and the double-subject shape from M24.

**Gap deck:** the spirit jammed like traffic (기가 막히다), the address every apartment dweller recites (백일 동 삼백일 호), and the three necessities in Korean's order (의식주).
