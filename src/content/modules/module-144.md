## Part 1 — Public forms and private choices

**The public scene.** 신청서 is the document; 재학 is enrollment; 학과 is a department. 게시판 carries notices, while 예보 reports what may happen next.

**The physical scene.** 앨범 can hold photographs or music. 전시하다 puts items before viewers; 큰절 is a full respectful bow. 시금치 belongs in the kitchen.

**A sense to guard.** 부채 can be a hand fan or a debt. 영남 names a region. 주관적 describes an individual view; 공통점 names what views share.

**Earlier vocabulary:** 종교적 → M69; 천국 → M69; 큰절 → M69. These words return in the examples as review; the 30 entries below are this pack’s new checklist.
