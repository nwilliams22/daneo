## Part 1 — Public forms and private choices

**The public scene.** 신청서 is the document; 재학 is enrollment; 학과 is a department. 게시판 carries notices, while 예보 reports what may happen next.

**The physical scene.** 앨범 can hold photographs or music. 전시하다 puts items before viewers; 큰절 is a full respectful bow. 시금치 belongs in the kitchen.

**A sense to guard.** 부채 can be a hand fan or a debt. 영남 names a region. 주관적 describes an individual view; 공통점 names what views share.

**Earlier vocabulary:** 종교적 → M69; 천국 → M69; 큰절 → M69. These words return in the examples as review; the 33 entries below are this pack’s new checklist.

## Practice

1. Use M143’s 제출 and 신청서 in a new deadline sentence.
2. Contrast 재학 with 학과: which describes enrollment and which names a department?
3. Make a forecast sentence that distinguishes 예보 from 위험성.
4. Explain 부채 as fan and debt without translating the two senses identically.
5. Change 학교는 앨범을 전시했어요 to a future exhibition.
6. Contrast 주관적 with 공통점 using two opinions about the same event.
7. Use 과외 and 일자리 in one scene about a student’s part-time work.
8. Pair 후회 with a past choice, then say what 여전하다 would mean the next day.

**Gap deck:** 큰절을 올리다 — To give a formal deep bow. 일자리를 구하다 — To look for work.
