# Korean, Word-First — Module 149: Care, Change and Days of Learning

A class ends, a performance moves to a new date, and family members respond to care. This pack watches how a changed plan becomes a changed feeling.

**How to use this:** Learn the checklist, then read each three-layer sentence in order. Trace each Korean chunk through the gloss to the natural English.

---

## Part 1 — Change with a visible result

연기되다 delays an event; 공연되다 presents a work on stage. 감소되다, 심각해지다 and 다양해지다 name different changes of state. Keep the subject clear: a performance is postponed, a cost decreases, and a problem grows serious.

## Part 2 — People and periods

장사꾼 and 낚시꾼 both use -꾼 for a person associated with an activity. 교시 counts a school period; 초저녁 and 초여름 from M77 locate the early part of a day or season. 학비 names the money needed for study.

## Part 3 — Building sentences

No new glue. The sentence frames are familiar; the new work is choosing the precise word for each role.

**Spot the recycling:** M148's 장모님, 촛불 and 산길 return in new outcomes and locations. The sentence order keeps their meanings visible without repeating an earlier line.

- **-았/었더니 from M88 introduces a result the speaker observed.**
- **-자마자 from M38 joins a class ending to immediate play.**
- **-고서 from M85 orders extinguishing a candle before play.**

Eight sentences put the vocabulary into familiar grammar frames.

**Gap deck:** 까먹다 and 어쩜 좋아 carry meanings beyond their word-by-word readings.
