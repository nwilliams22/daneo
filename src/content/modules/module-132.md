# Korean, Word-First — Module 132: Public Measures and Private Hopes

A district office assesses housing, a rating rises during a broadcast, and an interview changes someone's view. This pack joins public measures to the feelings and choices of people living with them.

**How to use this:** Read the words in small shelves, then compare the Korean and English chunks of each sentence. No new glue is required. The rate words deserve a concrete subject and a clear direction.

## Part 1 — Public life, human judgment

**Public measures.** 사업자 runs a business, while 구청 is a district administrative office. 주거 names housing and residence; 제도적 describes what a rule or institution makes possible or difficult. 환율 is a rate between currencies, 강수량 a measured amount of precipitation, and 시청률 a share of television viewers. A rising number needs its unit and comparison point to be meaningful.

**People and commitments.** 소망 is an earnest wish; 자부심 is pride in one's work or group. 편견 is a judgment bent before fair evidence arrives. 중요시하다 treats something as important in a decision, rather than merely noticing it. 여군 and 강사 name roles; 연인 names a relationship. 활기 describes energy that can be noticed in a place or person.

**Sequence and change.** 예선 comes before a final contest; 연장 lengthens time or distance, and 교대 replaces one shift or person with another. 중단되다 makes the halted process the subject. 장차 looks ahead. 깨뜨리다 can break an object or a fixed idea, and 꼼짝 with 못 하다 denies even the smallest movement.

**Sense radar.** 연장 here is 延長, extension, not a tool. 정신과 is psychiatry, a medical specialty. 기성 is 旣成, already formed, as in an established generation. 기도하다 means to pray; a medical airway is a different 기도. 은행나무 is a ginkgo tree, not a tree belonging to a bank.

## Practice

1. Explain why 시청률 is a rate rather than a viewer count.
2. Put 급증하다 with a new measurable subject.
3. Contrast a 제도적 problem with a single person's 편견.
4. Change the 구청 sentence so the subject is stated aloud.
5. Use -더라도 with 예선 and a different outcome.
6. Compare 교대 and 연장: which replaces, and which lengthens?
7. Make a fresh sentence with M131's 허락하다 and this module's 강사.
8. Rebuild the rainfall sentence with 낮아지다 and say what changed.

**Gap deck:** 편견을 깨뜨리다 breaks a fixed unfair view rather than a physical object. 꼼짝 못 하다 means someone cannot move even an inch; the tiny motion word makes the negation strong.
