# Korean, Word-First — Module 35: Becoming

Korean has an entire wing of its grammar for **change over time** — and you've been circling it for thirty modules. 되다 showed up in 안 돼요 (M10) and -(으)면 돼요 (M23) without ever introducing itself properly; this module finally lets it say what it means: *to become.* Around it, two machines: **-아/어지다** turns adjectives into change-verbs, and **-게 되다** turns outcomes into modest announcements. The vocabulary is everything that grows, shrinks, sticks, falls, and flows.

**How to use this:** the glue is assembly again — -게 되다 is M33's -게 plus M10's 되다, and half the vocabulary (이루어지다, 달라지다, 사라지다, 오래되다) has the machinery welded in already. Learn the words and you've half-learned the grammar.

---

## Part 1 — 되다, properly introduced

**[noun]이/가 되다 = to become the noun.** 겨울이 됐어요 (winter has come), 의사가 됐어요 (became a doctor), 어른이 되면… (when I grow up…). Note the particle: you become WITH 이/가, not 을/를 — the thing you become gets the subject suit.

And the comfort formula every Korean deploys, now fully decodable: **다 잘 될 거야** — all (M9's 다) + well + become + will (M8) + 반말 (M19) = *it'll all work out.* Say it to a stressed friend; the grammar itself is the reassurance — things *become* well on their own.

**The do/happen pairs are the preview:** 바꾸다/바뀌다, 이루다/이루어지다 — one member acts, the other just happens. Collect the pattern now; Module 36 industrializes it.

## Part 3 — The glue: two change machines

### 1. [adjective]-아/어지다 — gets X-er

Conjugate to the -아/어 form, add 지다, and the adjective starts moving:

- 춥다 → **추워져요** — it's getting cold
- 좋다 → **좋아졌어요** — it got better
- 많다 → **많아지고 있어요** — there's more and more (M8's progressive stacks right on)
- 예쁘다 → **예뻐졌어요** — the post-haircut compliment

Pace it with **점점** (gradually) — 점점 추워져요 — the pair sells as a set. And a flag for M36: on *verbs*, -아/어지다 builds passives. This module keeps to adjectives; the party is next door.

### 2. [verb]-게 되다 — ends up, comes to

M33's -게 + 되다: circumstances arrange the outcome. 한국어를 배우게 됐어요 = I ended up learning Korean (life did it, not ambition). Three registers of use:

1. **Gradual arrival:** 케이팝을 좋아하게 됐어요 — it grew on you.
2. **The modest announcement:** 서울에서 일하게 됐어요 — new job, zero boasting. This is how Koreans announce jobs, moves, and weddings; humility is grammaticalized.
3. **The set phrase:** 알게 됐어요 = came to know / found out.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** M11's 춥다 learns to move, M26's 실력 finds its growth verb, M8's future machine schedules a diet, and the 2002 World Cup slogan (꿈은 이루어진다) surrenders its grammar.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the three-year-old habit that reaches eighty, love as a hole you fall into, and the dragon that rises from a ditch.
