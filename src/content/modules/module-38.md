# Korean, Word-First — Module 38: While and As Soon As

The engine room is built; what's left is **timing**. This module installs three connectors that set clauses against the clock — doing two things at once (**-(으)면서**), the instant one thing follows another (**-자마자**), and the span something takes (**-는 동안**) — and stocks the shelf with Korean's daily-rhythm vocabulary: dawns, all-nighters, oversleeping, and the fine gradations of *just now* and *in a bit* that Korean slices thinner than English does.

**How to use this:** the glue is light — one of the three is pure assembly (동안 has been yours since M28; the badge since M24), and the other two are single endings with no tense tricks. Spend your effort on the adverb rack instead: 방금/아까/이따가/금방 carry more daily conversation than most verbs.

---

## Part 1 — Korean slices "now" thinner

English has *just now* and *later*. Korean issues a full rack:

- **방금** — seconds ago · **아까** — earlier today (only today) · **이따가** — later today (only today) · **금방** — a moment ago OR in a moment · **곧** (M10) — soon · **나중에** (M8) — someday, unscheduled

아까 and 이따가 bookend your day and expire at midnight; 나중에 is the one without a date. Get these six straight and your small talk gains a gearbox.

And the **매-family** assembles: 매일 (M5), 매주 (M17), **매년**, 매달, 매번 — Sino 每 (every) doing what M31's native 마다 does: 매년 = 해마다. Two everys, one register apart.

## Part 3 — The glue: three clocks

### 1. -(으)면서 — while (same person, two verbs)

Vowel stems take -면서, batchim stems -으면서, and old irregulars hold: 듣다 → **들으면서**. One subject does both things: 음악을 **들으면서** 공부해요. The restriction is the meaning — it's ONE body multitasking. (Public-service corollary: 운전하**면서** 전화하지 마세요.)

### 2. -자마자 — as soon as (zero gap)

Straight onto the stem, no tense inside — the main clause carries the time: 눈을 **뜨자마자** 핸드폰을 봐요 · 도착**하자마자** 전화했어요. Subjects may differ: 경기가 **끝나자마자** 비가 왔어요. The ending's whole promise is *no gap* — Korean's tightest connector.

### 3. -는 동안 — during (the span)

M24's badge + M28's 동안: 기다리**는 동안** 책을 읽었어요. Nouns skip the badge: 방학 **동안**, 회의 **동안**. Unlike -(으)면서, different subjects are fine — 동안 shares the clock, not the body.

**Calibration:** simultaneous action → 면서 · zero-gap sequence → 자마자 · shared timespan → 동안. Three clocks, no overlap.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** M2's ㄷ-irregular holds inside 들으면서, M31's compound machinery yields to the sidewalk (지나가다), M37's alarm gets blamed for 늦잠, and M17's 경기 ends exactly when the sky decides.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** change compressed into one morning (하루아침에), busyness beyond nose-opening (눈코 뜰 새 없다), and the punctuality myth Korea retired (코리안 타임).
