# Korean, Word-First — Module 78: Who's Who

M47 drew the kinship grid (시-, 외-, and the 촌수 arithmetic); M58 built the title machine (長 · 者 · 員 · 生). This module adds a third floor to each. The family grid gets its husbands-by-marriage and its ranks-by-birth (형부, 고모부, 장남, 차남, 시부모, 신혼부부), three children-words and the marked prefix 여 arrive with the words it builds, the title machine hires a floor of jobs (담임, 점원, 운전기사, 외교관, 역사가, 디자이너, 챔피언), and four radar-heavy nouns close the shelf (강도, 수상, 부족, 성별). The organizing thesis: **Korean names a person by their coordinates — whose husband, which side, what rank, what machine stamped the title** — and the suffix payroll M27's 환자 opened ('The -자 is a person-suffix (기자, 학자 — reporter, scholar)') is hiring again. Two characters get read for the first time: 夫 (husband) and 官 (official).

**How to use this:** learn the coordinates, not the list — every family word here is a taught word plus one axis (whose side, whose husband, which birth), and every job word is a taught root plus one factory. Spend your reps on the radar pairs the shelf hides: 강도 the robber vs 강도 the intensity, 수상 the prime minister vs 수상 the prize, 부족 the shortage vs 부족 the tribe, and 애인 vs the 애 in 어린애.

## Part 1 — A person is a set of coordinates

Almost nothing on this shelf is a stranger; it is old words filed on new axes:

- **The husband axis — 夫, a first:** 형부 is 兄 (M47's 형제 finally showed the character) + 夫, husband — the front half of M25's 부부 (夫婦), whose back half M58's 주부 read as 'the bride-婦 of 신부 (M47)'. The -부 rail files husbands by WHOSE husband they are: 형부 (언니's), 고모부 (고모's), 이모부 (이모's — frozen). And the speaker-gender grid M4 started runs straight into the in-laws — a woman's 언니 has a 형부, a man's 누나 has a 매형; the gap deck files the fork.
- **The side axis pays two IOUs:** M47's 고모 note promised 'Her mirror is 이모, mom's sister' — the aunt you have been summoning since the M18 gap deck; M47's 삼촌 note promised 'Your cousin lives one step further: 사촌, the four-degree relative — same arithmetic'. Both land today.
- **The rank axis, formal register:** 장남/차남 put chief-長 (M58's 부장 and 반장) and next-次 (M46's 차례) on the ladder M30's 첫째 filed as 'a lifelong job title in Korean families' — the 장남 inherits, the 차남 is free.
- **One marked prefix:** 여 (女) stands alone at last — the 女 of M32's 여성, M47's 손녀, M58's 소녀 — and builds 여대생, 여고생, 여직원 on M15's 대학생, this shelf's 고교, and M16's 직원. Korean marks the female as the exception; there is no everyday 남직원.
- **The title machine, third floor:** 者 (초보자, 사용자 — the payroll of M27's 환자), 家 (역사가 — the house of M48's 작가), 員 (점원, 여직원 — the member of M43's 사원), 士 (운전기사 — the licence of M44's 박사), 官 (외교관 — the official, a FIRST: the bureaucrat inside M27's 대사관, official-官 in a building-館), and the converter's -er (웨이터, 디자이너) with 챔피언 beside them.

## Part 2 — The bricks

Read the decodes before you drill: over half this shelf quotes a note you already learned, and the receipts do the memorizing. The order is the module's argument — family (이모 first), children and the prefix (고교 before 여고생), then the jobs, then the four radar closers with 성별 last.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** a 강도 who 들다 is a robber (强盜), a 강도 that is 높다 is intensity (强度) · three 수상s — 首相 the prime minister, 受賞 the award (受, a first), 殊常 the suspicious · 부족 is short of something (不足) unless it lives in a documentary (部族) · 애인 is love-person (愛, a first), not M47's 애 · 역사가 길어요 is history being long, not a historian · the 관 of 외교관 is the official (官), never the hall-館 of M23's 박물관 · and two job-사s: teacher-師 (요리사, 간호사, 목사) vs licensed-士 (박사, 기사, 변호사).

## Part 3 — Two machines, no new grammar

### 1. The coordinate grid — whose, which side, what rank

Every family word here is arithmetic on M4 and M47: take a taught person and add ONE axis. Whose husband → -부 (형부, 고모부); which side → 시- and 외- (시부모, 외사촌); what birth → 長/次 (장남, 차남); how new → 신- (신혼부부, the 신 of M25's 신혼여행). The speaker is the origin of the grid: 형부 exists only in a woman's mouth, 장남 only relative to a 차남, 시부모 only from the wife's side. Say **시부모님을 모시고 살아요** and three axes fire at once — his side, honored, cared for upward with M47's 모시다.

### 2. The title machine — 者 · 家 · 員 · 士 · 官 · -er

M58 ran four person-factories; today the payroll shows six stamps. 者 takes a state or an act and makes its person (초보 → 초보자, 사용 → 사용자); 家 takes a field and makes its master (역사 → 역사가); 員 takes a place and makes its staff (점 → 점원); 士 marks a licence (운전 + 기사); 官 marks the state's official (외교 → 외교관, 경찰 → 경찰관); and English jobs skip all five and arrive through the converter with their own -er — 웨이터, 디자이너, 챔피언. Print counts with these words (점원, 종업원, 운전기사); faces get the address words — 직원 (M16), 기사님 (M48), 사장님 (M9) — and this module's 이모.

**Calibration:** 여 stamps the exception, so use it the way the news is learning to — for description (여직원한테 물어보세요, when you mean the woman at the counter), never as a rank. And the title rule holds for every job here: the paper word names, the 님 word summons — 담임 on the form, 담임 선생님 in the hallway; 대학교수 on the 명함, 교수님 in the office.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** 만큼 from M47 measures a brother-in-law against his wife, -아/어 주세요 from M9 summons the 이모, -(으)ㄹ 때 from M25 and 께서 from M47 keep the 담임 at school past dark, -(으)ㄴ 지 from M46 and -는데 from M53 count a diplomat's decade, 에 의하면 from M56 and the quote machine (M32) deliver the prime minister in 합니다체, 위한+noun from M44 and -(으)면 from M20 open the beginner's app, -자마자 from M38 and -게 되다 from M35 hand the eldest son his duty, and 것 같다 from M34, 씩 from M43, and -기로 하다 from M48 diagnose and treat 운동 부족.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the sticker that buys a new driver mercy (초보 운전), the school year that is an identity (고3), and the in-law titles that fork by the speaker's gender (언니의 남편은 형부, 누나의 남편은 매형).
