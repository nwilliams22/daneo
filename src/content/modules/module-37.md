# Korean, Word-First — Module 37: Making-Do

Module 36 was the party where things happened TO you. This is its mirror twin: the verbs for what you MAKE happen — feeding, waking, dressing, entrusting, running the washing machine. Same little suffixes, opposite direction: **이 · 히 · 리 · 기**, plus a new recruit, **우**. And the theme writes itself: causatives are the grammar of *taking care* — of babies, pets, plants, and everyone who can't (or won't) do it themselves.

**How to use this:** like M36, this is vocabulary wearing a pattern, not conjugation. Learn the triangles — 늘다 (it grows) / 늘리다 (you grow it); 깨다 (you wake) / 깨우다 (you wake THEM) — and Band 4's whole engine room clicks into one diagram: happen-verb, do-verb, make-do verb.

---

## Part 1 — The mirror party

Three things to know before the guest list:

1. **The person you act on is usually a plain object** — 동생을 깨웠어요 — but when you route something *to* them, 한테 returns: 아기**한테** 밥을 먹여요. Compare M36's 모기**한테** 물렸어요: same particle marking the culprit there, the mouth here. One 한테, both directions of luck.
2. **Adjectives get causatives too.** 높이다/낮추다 (raise/lower), 늘리다/줄이다 (grow/shrink) — the volume knobs of Korean. M35 gave adjectives -아/어지다 to change on their own; this module lets you turn the dial yourself.
3. **Some words are double agents.** 보이다 = be seen (M36) *and* show (보여 주세요 — you've said it since M9!). 읽히다, 씻기다 — same shape, both parties. Context checks the invitation.

**Masks off, again:** 키우다 (M35) = 크다 + 이우. 세우다 (M12's park-the-car) = 서다 + 이우 — parking is making the car stand. 끓이다 (M18's 라면 verb) = 끓다 + 이. The decode debt keeps clearing.

## Part 3 — The glue: three make-do machines

### 1. The suffix party: 이 · 히 · 리 · 기 · 우 (· 추)

| Suffix | Parent → Causative | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| **이** | 먹다 → 먹이다 · 죽다 → 죽이다 · 붙다 → 붙이다 · 높다 → 높이다 · 끓다 → 끓이다 | feed, kill, attach |
| **히** | 앉다 → 앉히다 · 입다 → 입히다 · 눕다 → 눕히다 | seat, dress, lay down |
| **리** | 살다 → 살리다 · 알다 → 알리다 · 울다 → 울리다 · 돌다 → 돌리다 · 늘다 → 늘리다 | save, inform, spin |
| **기** | 맡다 → 맡기다 · 남다 → 남기다 · 숨다 → 숨기다 · 웃다 → 웃기다 | entrust, leave, hide |
| **우** | 깨다 → 깨우다 · 자다 → 재우다 · 타다 → 태우다 · 서다 → 세우다 · 비다 → 비우다 · 크다 → 키우다 | wake, ride, empty |
| **추** | 맞다 → 맞추다 · 낮다 → 낮추다 | tune, lower |

Membership, not rules — same as M36. Learn the pairs; the suffixes become recognition, not production.

### 2. -게 하다 — make/let (takes walk-ins)

For everything the party doesn't cover: M33's -게 (third career) + 하다 — *make it so that.* 이 노래는 저를 **웃게 해요** (makes me smile) · 아이를 일찍 **자게 했어요** (had the kid sleep early). The suffix party is exclusive; -게 하다 serves everyone.

### 3. [noun]을 시키다 — the order machine

M3 taught 시키다 as "to order food" — the mask comes off: ordering was always its entire job. 심부름을 시키다 (send on an errand), 공부를 시키다 (make them study), 목욕을 시키다 (bathe the dog). Attach it to any 하다-noun and someone else is doing the work. The waiter and the errand-kid hear the same verb.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** M36's 깨다 gains a victim, M13's 세탁기 finally spins (돌리다 + M31's 마다), M20's 알람 meets 맞추다, and M3's 시키다 confesses everything.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** emptying the heart on purpose (마음을 비우다), the emergency scream 사람 살려!, and affection measured in trips (손이 많이 가다).
