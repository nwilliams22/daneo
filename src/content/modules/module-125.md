# Korean, Word-First — Module 125: Evidence, Venues and Motion

A report names evidence; a venue gathers people; movement changes what they can see. The slice crosses these shelves, so the word notes keep sense and register more precise than a single theme could.

**How to use this:** learn the exact word senses first. The eight sentences recycle Ring 3 patterns, with no new glue introduced here.

---

## Part 1 — Reports and places

The report shelf holds 제시, 국적, 유형, and 실현되다. The venue shelf pairs M48's 공연 with 공연장 and sets a public 회관 beside it. 시야 and 망원경 focus on what a viewer can actually see.

**A result can shift focus:** M56's 나누다 divides or shares; 나뉘다 describes what ends up divided. M121's 방해 becomes the action 방해하다. M114's 실현하다 makes a plan real, while 실현되다 puts the result in subject position.

::vocab::

**Register radar:** 내외 is a married couple in the listed sense. 찍히다 is a stamped impression here. 상류 can be a river's upper reach or, figuratively, high social status. 자살 is a grave term and needs careful context.

**Side shelf:** 늑대, 왕자, 으레, and 의외로 stay in the ranked slice without pretending to be the same family.
