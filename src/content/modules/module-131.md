# Korean, Word-First — Module 131: Change, Certainty and Careful Work

An answer appears after an interview; a count rises as a season passes. This slice gives the verbs and measure words for those scenes, plus the smaller actions that make ordinary work feel precise.

**How to use this:** Give the word notes first pass, then read each Korean sentence before the English layer. This module adds no grammar; it gives older glue new work to do.

---

## Part 1 — From uncertainty to an answer

**Decision shelf.** 해답 is the answer that resolves a question; 해소하다 removes a problem or tension. 허락하다 gives permission, while 확신하다 expresses strong confidence. 확립하다 establishes a principle or system firmly. A person can have an answer without yet having permission, or permission without certainty.

**Rate and direction.** 급증하다 says a quantity rises sharply; 급속히 gives the pace. 낮아지다 makes a level fall, and 발달되다 puts the resulting development in the subject position. 하반기 is the second half of a year; M128 already taught 상반기 for the first. 멎다 simply says something stops. These words need a measured subject when the sentence claims a trend.

**Careful hands.** 꼼꼼하다 checks small details; 깔끔하다 describes a tidy result. 거들다 helps with someone else's work rather than claiming all of it. 면담 is a purposeful face-to-face talk, 모집 a search for participants or applicants. 모퉁이, 바구니 and 부딪히다 make the physical scene in the sentence deck.

**Sense radar.** The listed 매 is corporal punishment (매를 맞다), not the hawk or “each.” 비상 is 非常, an emergency, not the homograph 飛上, flight. 단순 is a noun or stem that usually becomes 단순하다 or 단순히. 보름 is a fifteen-day lunar span and can also evoke a full moon. 흥분 is a noun; 흥분하다 supplies the verb.

**Gap deck:** 비상이 걸리다 literally puts an emergency “on”; it means an alert is raised. 해답이 보이다 can mean that a solution becomes clear without a written answer in sight.
