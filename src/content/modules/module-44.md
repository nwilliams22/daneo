# Korean, Word-First — Module 44: In Order to Pass

Ring 1's M15 walked you into the classroom — 교실, 교과서, 시험, and the ladder from 초등학교 to 대학교. This module climbs past it into the academy register: the subjects shelf (과목 frames what 수학, 과학, 역사 started), the degree ladder (대학원 → 석사 → 박사), the research bench (논문, 자료, 조사), and the exam-culture words Korea actually runs on — 합격, 정답, 복습, and the 바보 you call yourself when the answers surface. The glue is the purpose machine: **-기 위해(서)** — in order to. M32 taught you to dedicate things to PEOPLE (가족을 위해서); today, to goals.

**How to use this:** this shelf is almost pure hanja arithmetic, and you already own most of the parts — read every decode before you drill, and let 學, 術, and 力 do the memorizing for you. The exam nouns (합격, 정답, 실패) come with their Ring 1 verbs pre-attached; recall them as pairs.

## Part 1 — One hanja runs the whole ladder

This module is the 學-economy's annual report:

- **The 學-reunion:** M15's 수학 note said the -학 suffix 'names every field — watch for it.' Watched, and collected: 문학 joins 수학 and 과학; then 學 runs the other direction through 학기, 학습, 입학, 유학, 장학금, 대학원. One character, eleven jobs.
- **The 術-trio:** 미술 makes beauty, 예술 makes meaning, 기술 makes things work — all skill-術, and none of them M9's drinkable 술.
- **The 力-family:** 힘 (M14) in a suit — M26's 실력 (built skill), M39's 노력 (spent strength), today's 능력 (raw capability).
- **The pass/fail machine:** M35's 붙다 sticks you to the list, M36's 떨어지다 drops you off it — 합격 and 실패 are those verdicts, nouned and framed (합's fit-合, 격's frame-格).
- **The nouns ride home:** Ring 1 and Band 4 taught the verbs — 발표하다 (M16), 성공하다/실패하다 (M35), 졸업하다 (M15); today their nouns land, the same completion M42 ran for 긴장.

## Part 3 — The glue: doing things on purpose

### 1. -기 위해(서) — in order to VERB

M32's 위해서 finally meets M13's -기, and the noun frame becomes a verb frame: 합격**하기 위해서** 공부해요 = I study in order to pass. Any verb nominalizes in, any goal comes out: 유학을 가**기 위해서** 돈을 모아요, 건강**을 위해서**는 the old noun frame still serves. The 서 is droppable (위해/위해서 — same machine, shorter belt).

### 2. 위한 + noun — the badge form

Wear M24's badge and the purpose attaches to a THING instead of an action: 학생**을 위한** 책 = a book for students, 유학생을 위한 장학금. Dedication as an adjective.

**Calibration:** 위해서 states purpose with intent behind it — plans, sacrifices, study regimens. For casual reasons Korean still reaches for -(으)러 (M12's errand-form) or -아/어서; 위해서 is the one that sounds like it belongs on a vision board. Korea's study culture conjugates its whole ethos through it: 미래를 위해서, 합격하기 위해서, 가족을 위해서.
