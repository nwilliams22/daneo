# Korean, Word-First — Module 87: Seeming and Worth

Module 34 taught you the hedge — 것 같다, a noun (것) plus "same" — and said the hedge was politeness, not doubt. Here is the rest of that machine. **Korean measures certainty with nouns:** a look (듯), a reason (리), a law (법), a count (셈), a provision (마련), a verge (지경), a way-of-one's-own (나름) — each one a dependent noun that takes M24's badge in front and a closer behind, and each one says how sure you are, how much a thing is worth, or how far it has gone. The more formal the noun, the more the sentence is written rather than said: 것 같아요 is the mouth, 듯하다 is the page. Around the hedges sit the words the page uses to judge — the verbs of reckoning (여기다, 판단하다, 평가하다, 인식하다, 의식하다), eleven nouns of judgment from 관점 to 가치, and four adjectives of worth. Thirty-three Grade-C words, two glue shelves, and the sentence Korea says after every dropped plate.

**How to use this:** read the socket table in Part 2 first — every hedge on this shelf is the can-machine from M10 with a different noun in the socket — then drill the two shelves as frames (badge + noun + closer), not as words. The judgment nouns are almost pure hanja arithmetic on characters you own; read the four-view sort (의견/견해/관점/시각) and the three-truth sort (사실/진실/진리) as sets.

## Part 1 — The bricks

Three drawers, and the first one is grammar wearing a noun's clothes:

- **The hedges:** 듯 is the bare form M55's 듯이 note promised ("Headlines clip it to bare 듯: 비 올 듯."); 셈 is the count-세다 M82's 꽃잎 note left frozen, through the -ㅁ factory (M33); 마련 is the noun under M56's 마련하다, and M74's 거짓 note left 진실 riding frozen — all four rides end here. 바 and 아니하다 are formal fossils: the official's 아는 바가 없습니다 and the statute's -지 아니하다, which M34's 괜히 note traced ("both from 관계하지 아니하다, 'not related'").
- **The verbs of reckoning:** 여기다 holds a thing AS something (소중하게 여기다); M34's 판단 dismounts to 판단하다; 평가 puts a price (價 — M21's 가격, read at last) on a thing; 인식하다 and 의식 share the knowing-識 of M44's 지식; 뜻하다 is M10's 뜻 verbed, the native twin of M72's 의미하다.
- **The nouns of judgment, sorted by hanja:** the see-觀 of M23's 관광 in 관점 and 관념; the watch-視 of M48's 시청자 in 시각; the view-見 of M56's 발견하다 in 견해; the argument-論 of M44's 논문 in 논리; the 理 of M45's 이성 in 논리, 진리, 이념; the constant-常 of M38's 일상 in 상식; the doubt-疑 of M34's 의심 in 의문; the 眞 of M74's 가짜 in 진실 and 진리; and 價 + 値 in 가치, where the shelf ends.
- **The adjectives of worth:** 소중하다 is place-所 + heavy-重 (the place where the weight is); 올바르다 is M63's 바르다 made whole; 바람직하다 is M34's 바라다 nouned and made worthy; 적절하다 is the fit-適 of M64's 적용하다 with the 切 of M81's 친절 — the memo's 적당하다.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 여기다 (to regard) is a verb and M6's 여기 (here) is a place — a homograph, not a family · 가치 is spelled the way M9's 같이 is SAID ("Pronounced 가치 — a sound-change bonus") — 같이 가요 and 가치가 있어요 are homophones · the 만 of 만하다 is about-that-much, not the only-만 from M30 and not M9's 만 (ten thousand) · 바 the formal 'what' is not the 바 of M4's 바쁘다 or M28's 바로 · 지경 is a boundary-境, not M46's 지 (time since) · 시각 the angle (視角) and 시각 the clock-time (時刻) are both NIKL rows, one spelling · 의식 the awareness (意識) and 의식 the ceremony (儀式) likewise · and 나름 is one word, not M1's 나 with something after it.

## Part 2 — The glue: the seeming shelf

M10 built the can-machine — "Same hook as the future (-(으)ㄹ), then 수 (a way) + 있다/없다" — and called it "Ability as existence — the 있다 factory's grandest product". Keep the hook and the closer, change the noun in the socket, and you get the whole shelf:

| Socket noun | The noun means | Frame | Says |
|---|---|---|---|
| 수 (M10) | a way | -(으)ㄹ 수 있다/없다 | can / cannot |
| 줄 (M55) | the trick | -(으)ㄹ 줄 알다/모르다 | knows how / doesn't |
| 리 (a reason — frozen) | a reason in the order of things | -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 | there's no way that |
| 법 (M39) | a law | -(으)ㄹ 법하다 | it stands to reason that |
| 듯 | a look | -(으)ㄹ/-는 듯하다 | it seems that |

### 1. -(으)ㄹ 듯하다 / -는 듯하다 — the written 것 같다

Badge + 듯하다: 비가 **올 듯해요** (looks like rain), 아는 **듯했어요** (seemed to know), 벌써 끝난 **듯해요** (seems already over). Same meaning as 것 같다 from M34, and only the register moves — 것 같다 is said, 듯하다 is written; a news anchor reads 듯하다, a friend says 것 같아요. The family is now five: 것 같다 from M34 ("[badge] + 것 같다 — I think / it seems"), -나 보다 from M34 ("-나 보다 wants a clue you can point at"), M34's 모양 ("~모양이에요 can also mean 'seems'"), -(으)ㄹ 수도 있다 from M84, and 듯하다. Radar: M55's 듯이 is the adverb ("듯이 rides a claim") — 아는 듯이 말해요 describes HOW he talks; 아는 듯해요 is a verdict on whether he knows.

### 2. -(으)ㄹ 법하다 and -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 — the law and the reason

M39's 법 (law, and the way of doing) as a socket noun: **그럴 법해요** = that figures, there's a law for it; 있을 **법한** 이야기 (M24's 이야기) = a story that could well be true — the badge form. And its mirror: 리 (理, the ordering-理 M67's 심리 note traced — "the ordering-理 of 정리 (M54)" — riding frozen here as a noun) with 가 and 없다: **그럴 리가 없어요** = that can't be, no reason exists for it; 그 사람이 거짓말을 **했을 리가 없어요** (M19's 거짓말) = there's no way he lied. 법하다 grants plausibility, 리가 없다 denies it — the same socket, opposite verdicts.

**Calibration:** 올 것 같아요 (I feel it) · 올 듯해요 (the report feels it) · 올 법해요 (it would figure) · 올 리가 없어요 (impossible) · 올 수도 있어요 (nobody has ruled it out — M84). Five nouns, five degrees of sure.

## Part 3 — The glue: the reckoning shelf

**-(으)ㄹ 만하다 — worth the doing.** Prospective badge + 만하다: **볼 만해요** = worth seeing, **믿을 만한** 사람 (M19's 믿다) = someone you can trust, **먹을 만해요** = decent — the diner's faint praise. M63's 그만하다 pre-ran the 만 (about that much); M47's 만큼 (as much as, to the extent of) is the same measure as a particle. Enough of what it takes, and no more: 만하다 is praise at exactly the right temperature.

**-(으)ㄴ/는 셈이다 and -기 마련이다 — the count and the provision.** Past or present badge + 셈이다 = it amounts to: 다 끝난 **셈이에요** (as good as done), 그런 **셈이에요** (that's about the size of it — M24's 그런), 결국 이긴 **셈이에요** (we effectively won). And the -기 nominalizer from M13 + 마련이다 = bound to: 사람은 누구나 실수하기 **마련이에요** (M16's 실수하다), 겨울에는 춥기 **마련이에요** — the world has already arranged it. 셈 counts what has happened; 마련 says what was always going to.

**-(으)ㄹ 지경이다 and 나름 — the verge and the ruler.** Badge + 지경이다 = at the point of, and always bad: 죽을 **지경이에요** (M14's 죽다 — gap deck), 울 **지경이에요** (M7's 울다), 이 지경이 됐어요 (M10's 되다) = it has come to this. And 나름, three settings: **나름대로** (대로 from M54) = in one's own way; 사람 **나름이에요** = it depends on the person (gap deck); bare **나름** 괜찮아요 (M7's 괜찮다) = decent, actually — the 2010s' shrug.

**Calibration:** 볼 만해요 (worth it) · 본 셈이에요 (as good as seen) · 보기 마련이에요 (you were always going to) · 볼 지경이에요 (it has come to watching it) · 보기 나름이에요 (depends how you look). One verb, five nouns, five verdicts.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** -(으)니까 from M10 carries two reasons, 마다 from M31 puts an angle on every person, -(으)면 돼요 from M23 grants permission to judge, 대로 from M54 rides inside 나름대로, -지만 from M7 concedes before the doubt, the -게 adverbializer from M33 hands 소중하다 to 여기다, the honorific -(으)세요 from M4 dresses the parents, and the double subject M24 taught (한식은 반찬이 많아요) returns twice — a book with a fresh 관점, a paper with sound 논리.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the national reflex of minding other people's eyes (남을 의식하다), the everyday death by overwork (죽을 지경이다), and the polite refusal to generalize (사람 나름이다).
