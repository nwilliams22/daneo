# Korean, Word-First — Module 137: Renewal, Work and Fairness

A device takes input, an office revises an arrangement, and a tree shades a front door. This pack moves between systems and ordinary life, asking what changes and who benefits.

**How to use this:** Work through each shelf before the sentence cards. Follow the chunk IDs across all three layers; the grammar is already familiar.

## Part 1 — What changes, what stays

**Tools and offices.** 입력 is data put into a system; 도움말 explains what to do. 최신 points to the newest version, not necessarily the best. 본사 is a head office, while 본 before a noun is a formal “this.” A 금고 protects valuables. 배치 is an arrangement or placement, and 조정하다 adjusts it.

**Change with a direction.** 가능해지다 marks becoming possible; 활발해지다 becoming more active. 회복되다 is recovery or restoration. 되살리다 makes something live again, literally or figuratively. 그전 points back to an earlier time; 새로이 starts again. -아지다 and -되다 signal different ways change reaches its result.

**People and fairness.** 정직하다 describes an honest person, 능동적 one who takes initiative. 불평등하다 names an unequal state, and 희생하다 gives something up for another person or cause. 물질적 concerns tangible support; 국제적 crosses national borders. 백인 and 서양인 are different kinds of labels, one racial and one regional; use either only when that distinction matters.

**Everyday anchors.** Count a standing tree with 그루. 끼 counts a meal, not a dish. 반죽 is dough before cooking. A 주름 may form in cloth or skin; a 현관문 is the entrance door. 공연히 often says an action had no good reason.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** M136's 로봇, 보안, 신제품 and 신중하다 return in technical and human settings. -고서 from M85 keeps actions in order; -다 보니 from M93 lets a result emerge through experience.

## Practice

1. Contrast 입력 with 도움말 in an app.
2. Explain why 최신 does not guarantee quality.
3. Count two trees with 그루.
4. Make a new 보안 sentence using M136's 로봇 and this pack's 입력.
5. Contrast 정직하다 and 신중하다 in a decision.
6. Give an example of a 배치 that might be 불평등하다.
7. Tell what became possible with 가능해지다, then what recovered with 회복되다.
8. Use 그전 to compare a workplace before and after a change.

**Gap deck:** 일손이 부족하다 means a task lacks enough working hands; 회복되다 can describe health, order or a system returning to working condition.
