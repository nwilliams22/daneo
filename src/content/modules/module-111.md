# Korean, Word-First — Module 111: Native Verbs V, and the Adverbs of the Moment

M94 opened the native verb shelf with motion and mishap, M102 welded old verbs into new ones, M104 pulled the two parties apart at the seams, and M107 closed the run with the adjectives. This is the fifth shelf, and it comes with the words that time it. The thesis: **the moment has its own adverbs.** Korean does not leave "soon" and "gradually" to context the way English does — it keeps a small set of words whose entire job is to say how a thing sat in time: 이내 (soon after), 금세 (in no time), 차츰 (bit by bit), 한창 (at the height), 하도 (so very that…). Thirty-six Grade-C words, no new grammar, and twelve verbs that mostly happen inside one of those moments — you hesitate, you grab, you swallow, you wake.

**How to use this:** learn the seven adverbs against the twins you already hold, because not one of them is new in meaning — only in register and in aim. The table below is that whole lesson. Then take the four drawers in order and decode before you drill: six of today's verbs are two words welded (앞 + 세우다, 펴다 + 내다, 붙다 + 잡다, 내다 + 주다, 깨다 + 나다, 되- + 풀이 + 하다), and eight of the adjectives are two hanja you mostly own already — 類, 强, 密, 素, 誠, 難, 明, 接 have all been through here before, while 似, 烈, 朴, 透, 困, 瞳 and 敢 are firsts. Five earlier notes wrote IOUs on this shelf and all five clear today. Read the Radar patrol slowly: this module's traps are homographs, not meanings.

## Part 1 — Native verbs V, and the adverbs of the moment

The adverbs first, because each one already has a twin in your head:

| Today | The twin you own | The split |
|---|---|---|
| 이내 · 금세 | 금방 (M38) | 금방 points both ways, forward and back; 이내 needs something to be soon AFTER; 금세 only measures how little time it took |
| 차츰 | 점점 (M35) · 서서히 (M85) | 점점 is the mouth's and glues onto -아/어지다; 서서히 is 徐徐히 on the page; 차츰 sits between them |
| 홀로 | 혼자 (M10) | 혼자 살아요 is a fact about a flat; 홀로 살다 is a line in a novel about the same flat |
| 하도 | 너무 (M11) | 너무 is happy alone; 하도 always drags a consequence in behind it |
| 한창 | — | the peak of a thing while it is still going: 한창 바쁠 때, and 한창때 for a person's own |
| 감히 | — | daring, and nearly always an accusation about rank |

Then the four drawers:

- **THE VERBS (망설이다 앞세우다 펴내다 되풀이하다 붙잡다 내주다 물러나다 뜯다 빼앗다 삼키다 기대다 깨어나다):** hesitate, put in front, bring out, do over, grab hold, hand over, step back, tear open, snatch, swallow, lean, wake. Six are welds on words you own, and two of the welds need a second look: 앞세우다 wants the make-stand sense of M12's 세우다 that M37's 주차장 finally explained — "making the car STAND" — and 내주다 is 내다 + 주다, NOT the 끝내주다 M62's 끝내 winked at. The rest run on famous objects: 자리를 내주다 and 자리에서 물러나다 (M12's 자리), 봉투를 뜯다 (M43's 봉투), 마음을 빼앗다 (M19's 마음), 침을 삼키다 (M104's 침), 벽에 기대다 (M13's 벽).
- **THE ADJECTIVES (별다르다 뻔하다 유사하다 강렬하다 밀접하다 소박하다 성실하다 잦다 투명하다 고통스럽다 곤란하다 귀찮다):** two native adjectives at each end and eight Sino ones in the middle, every one decodable. 유사하다 is the 類 of M57's 종류 ("種類 seed-sort") + resemble-似; 강렬하다 is the force-强 of M64's 강제 + fierce-烈; 밀접하다 is dense-密 + the touch-接 of M43's 면접; 소박하다 is the plain-素 of M38's 평소 + unworked-朴; 성실하다 is the 誠 of M103's 정성 + the real-實 of M34's 확실하다; 투명하다 is through-透 + the bright-明 of M34's 분명하다; 곤란하다 is boxed-in-困 + the hard-難 of M100's 비난. 고통스럽다 is M53's 고통 through -스럽다 from M45, and 뻔하다 has two NIKL rows to its name.
- **THE NOUNS (두려움 빛깔 몸짓 먹이 눈동자):** the fear you write down, the colour a thing gives off, the gesture without the curled lip, the feed in the bowl, and the dark centre of an eye. Four of the five pay a note that carried them frozen.
- **THE ADVERBS OF THE MOMENT (이내 차츰 홀로 감히 금세 한창 하도):** the table above. Two of them are two words wearing one spelling: 이내 is also the noun 以內 (within — the 以 of M32's 이상 and the 內 of M31's 국내), and 한창 is an adverb and a noun both.

**Receipts come due.** Five earlier notes wrote an IOU on this shelf: M107's 두렵다 built **두려움** and left it standing ("Its noun 두려움 (riding frozen) comes off the -(으)ㅁ freezer of M67, the same machine that softened the ㅂ in M67's 어려움."), M93's 극복하다 had already spent it ("두려움을 극복하다 (두려움 — fear, riding frozen) = to get over a fear"), M68's 짓 filed **몸짓** as a neutral cousin riding free ("몸짓 (body gesture — M14's 몸)"), M37's 먹이다 named **먹이** on the way past ("the noun 먹이 = animal feed/prey"), and M104's 밥상 served **소박하다** one module ago ("소박한 밥상 (소박하다 — plain, riding frozen) = a simple spread"). All five dismount today.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 기대다 leans on a wall and is native; M34's 기대 and M42's 기대하다 are 期待 and expect a result — a homograph trap and the module's worst · 뻔하다 is an adjective (뻔한 이야기 — predictable) and a dependent noun inside -(으)ㄹ 뻔하다 from M94 (죽을 뻔했어요 — almost), one syllable doing two grammars · the 별 of 별다르다 is separate-別 from M57's 특별, not M22's 별, the star · 삼키다 is one native verb, not M2's 삼 plus M27's 키 · 금세 carries the now-今 of M38's 금방, not M60's 금, the gold — and it is spelled 금세, never 금새 · 하도 has nothing of M2's 도 in it and nothing of M83's 지하도, whose 도 is the way-道 · 감히 is dare-敢, not M80's 감, the persimmon, and not the feeling-感 that note filed beside it · 귀찮다 contains no M14's 귀 · 홀로 already has M6's 로 fossilized on the end, so 홀로로 does not exist · 먹이 is the noun and 먹이다 is the causative party of M37 — one syllable, two parts of speech · 내주다 gives a thing up; 끝내주다 is M16's 끝내다 in a far better mood · 밀접하다's 密 has been inside M19's 비밀 (秘密) since Ring 1 without ever being decoded there · and 잦다 is an adjective about the event where M8's 자주 is an adverb about you.

**Calibration, two ladders.** The speed: 금방 올게요 (M38's 금방 — I'll be right there, and nobody believes it) · 이내 돌아왔어요 (today — he was back before long, after something) · 금세 끝났어요 (today — it was over in no time, and that is all it says). The slope: 점점 추워져요 (M35's 점점 — the mouth's creep) · 차츰 좋아지고 있어요 (today — the page's) · 서서히 어두워져요 (M85's 서서히 — the slowest of the three). Same clock, one register per rung.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 pattern and names it in its note — -(으)ㄹ 뻔하다 from M94 turns 하도 망설여서 into a chance nearly lost; -는 바람에 from M86 blows the 먹이 out of a child's hands and scatters the birds 금세; -기 마련이다 from M87 rules that a person whose 실수 is 잦은 will 되풀이하다 it; -더라고요 from M88 reports a 기타 being 뜯다-ed more 강렬하게 than expected; -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 grows a 두려움 차츰 out of being 홀로; -(으)ㄴ/는 데다가 from M92 piles a 성실한 주인 on top of a 소박한 밥상; -(으)ㄹ까 봐 from M93 has a parent 눈물을 삼키고 before the light goes out 이내; and -(으)며 from M85 lets a 회장 물러나다 and 내주다 in one printed breath. Underneath sit the frames you have held since Ring 1: the badges -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24 on 잦은, 뜯는, 같은 and 소박한, the -아/어지다 machine from M35 closing 커져요, -아/어서 from M10 carrying four of the eight, verb--고 from M10 joining 삼키고, and the 합니다체 of M4 on the one printed sentence.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the refusal nobody argues with (귀찮아요), the outburst about rank (감히 어떻게), and the theft you report as the victim (마음을 빼앗기다).

## What's next

The native verb shelf now has five modules on it and one method under all of them: read a long native verb as two short ones, run the party check on anything ending in 이/히/리/기/우, decode a two-character adjective before you memorize it, and put every new adverb next to the twin you already own. Band 8 carries on down the Grade-C list — more of the page's vocabulary, and the words the newspaper cannot do without. 하도 많이 읽어서 이제 금세 읽히죠; 계속 읽으세요.
