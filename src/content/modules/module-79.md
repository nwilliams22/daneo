# Korean, Word-First — Module 79: The Converter's Back Shelf

The Konglish interlude opened the adaptation machine — Rule 1 the sound converter, Rule 2 the clip machine, Rule 3 made-in-Korea, Rule 4 the false friends — and then handed you loanwords one at a time. This module runs the machine on thirty-one at once: the whole converter shelf the frequency list still owed, from 그룹 (rank 879) down to 껌. Four of them have been riding frozen in shipped notes for a dozen modules (패션, 벨트, 버튼, 테스트) and land today with their receipts; the rest decode on sight. The organizing idea is the rule the interlude did not state: **every loanword in Korean lives next to a Korean word it did NOT replace.** 박스 beside 상자, 테스트 beside 시험, 카운터 beside 계산대, 메일 beside 편지, 미디어 beside 언론, 그룹 beside 팀 and 단체 and 모임 — the loan took ONE job and the older word kept the rest. The interlude's own verdict: 'When a loanword surprises you, assume specialization, not error'.

**How to use this:** do not drill the spellings — the converter gives you those for free. Spend your reps on the NEIGHBOR of each word: read every note for the Korean word standing next to the loan, and learn the pair as a split (시험 is sat, 테스트 is tried; 상자 in the dictionary, 박스 on the sign). The sports and kitchen words come with their Ring 1 verbs pre-attached (치다, 타다, 굽다, 씹다); recall them as pairs.

## Part 1 — The machine, run on thirty-one words

Nothing here is a stranger; the interlude already taught you to hear it:

- **Rule 1, the cushions:** 테스트 was the interlude's own specimen ('test → 테스트 (one syllable becomes three)'), and M51's 콘서트 named the rule after it. Count the cushions today: 벨트, 필름, 박스, 골프, 오븐, 스케이트 — and 액세서리, five syllables from four, the longest proof that 'Konglish is often LONGER than its source'. The converter's substitutions run on schedule: f → ㅍ (패션, 필름, 팬티), v → ㅂ (오븐), z → ㅈ (렌즈), l/r → ㄹ (라이터, 렌즈, 레스토랑). One outlier: 껌 tensed to ㄲ, the way M2's 빵 did.
- **Rule 2, the clips:** 콘택트렌즈 → 렌즈; 피시 is the mouth's spelling of PC, and 피시방 the room M8's 방 note filed as PC방.
- **Rule 4, the false friends — the module's core:** 팬티 is not trousers, 홈페이지 is the whole site, 카운터 never counts, 메모 is a jotted note, 박스 counts apples, 필름 blacks out. Each one kept exactly one English sense and specialized.
- **The frozen four pay their debts:** 패션 clears three notes (M48's 잡지, M72's 모델, M78's 디자이너); 벨트 clears three (M36's 안전하다, M60's 매다, M78's 챔피언); 버튼 clears M75's 취소; 테스트 clears M51's 콘서트 and M67's 심리.
- **The -장 rail extends:** 골프장 and 스키장 join 주차장 (M37), 정류장 (M12), and 경기장 (M51) — the place-場 M37's 주차장 note paved.

## Part 3 — No new grammar: the neighbor rule

### 1. One loan, one job

Korean does not borrow a word to replace one; it borrows a word to SPLIT one. 상자 was doing the whole job of box; 박스 arrived and took the shop sign, the delivery slip, and the counting (사과 한 박스), and 상자 kept the dictionary and the children's story. 시험 was the whole of test; 테스트 took the product trial and the personality quiz, and left the exam hall alone. 편지 was the whole of mail; 메일 took the inbox. When you meet a loan, ask which job it took — the Korean word standing beside it tells you what it did not.

### 2. The verbs come from Ring 1

Every loan on this shelf takes a verb you already own: 벨트를 매다 (M60), 버튼을 누르다 (M20), 골프를 치다 and 배드민턴을 치다 (M17), 스케이트를 타다 (M6), 오븐에 굽다 (M18), 껌을 씹다 (M41), 렌즈를 끼다 (M21), 포스터를 붙이다 (M37), 파일을 저장하다 (M20), 액세서리를 하다 (the 넥타이 rule from M21). The nouns are new; the grammar around them is not.

**Calibration:** register decides the pair. Print and officialdom lean Sino (계산대, 상자, 매체, 언론); shop signs, apps, and speech lean the loan (카운터, 박스, 미디어, 홈페이지). Use the loan where you see it written and the Korean word where the form has a box for it — and never correct a Korean speaker's loanword back to the English sense: the specialization is the word.
