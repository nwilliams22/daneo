# Korean, Word-First — Module 105: The Office, and the Adverbs of the Page

Band 8 keeps walking down the Grade-C list, and this slice hands you a machine you have watched other people run for twenty modules. M70's 마련되다 wrote the licence in one line — "되다 doesn't check pedigree, only the 하다 it replaces" — and the M97 economy page ran it six times without slowing down. Today you run it yourself: eight of these thirty-five words are 하다-verbs you already own with 되다 bolted on in place of 하다, and not one of them needs a new rule. Around that machine sit the nouns of the desk and the clinic — THE OFFICE (원고 문서 계좌 매장 명칭 상업 실습 자율 향상 휴식) and THE CLINIC (진단 질병 체력 인체 대합실) — and, running above all of them, twelve adverbs the page steers an argument with (미처 얼핏 웬 무려 줄곧 말없이 일찍이 하긴 고작 그토록 각기 최대한). The thesis is two sentences long. The flip is not a new grammar, it is a decision about who gets named. And the adverbs are the M85 shelf, second floor: every one of them has a spoken twin you have owned since Ring 1.

**How to use this:** run the M85 method on the adverbs — that md's instruction was "read each adverb's note for its TWIN — the mouth's word it replaces", and the twin table below gives you all twelve pairs at once; drill the pair, never the word. Then take the eight flips as one exercise, not eight: say each 하다 verb, say the 되다 form, and ask who disappeared. The nouns decode on sight — you already hold 原 文 書 計 座 賣 場 名 稱 商 業 實 習 自 律 向 上 休 息 斷 病 體 力 人 待 合 室 — and the firsts are few: 稿, 其, 疾, and the 診 of 진단.

## Part 1 — The office, and the adverbs of the page

Four drawers, and one machine running under two of them.

- **THE ADVERBS (미처 얼핏 웬 무려 줄곧 말없이 일찍이 하긴 고작 그토록 각기 최대한):** M85 opened this shelf with 따라서, 다만 and 결코; these are the next twelve up. Four of them are assembled from parts you own — 줄곧 is M55's 줄 plus M10's 곧 (in a straight line, all along), 말없이 is M4's 말 plus M32's 없이, 일찍이 is M8's 일찍 with the adverb-factory -이, and 그토록 is M29's 그 with a frozen -토록. Three run only with a negative or a number: 미처 needs a 못했어요 behind it, and 무려 and 고작 both need a number in front of the verb — the same slot, the same figure, opposite verdicts. And 하긴 is a whole conversational turn in two syllables, which is why the gap deck takes it.
- **THE 되다 FLIPS (설치 설치되다 발표되다 확인되다 성립되다 집중되다 해결되다 생산되다):** every root here is already yours — M91's 설치하다, M16's 발표하다, M20's 확인하다, M35's 성공하다 and M81's 국립 lending 成 and 立 to 성립되다, M44's 집중, M56's 해결하다, M97's 생산하다. The noun 설치 joins them because two shipped notes had already borrowed it frozen and owed it back. the M97 md said what the flip is FOR: "The 하다 side names the company; the 되다 side is what the newspaper prints, because the newspaper is not the company."
- **THE OFFICE (원고 문서 계좌 매장 명칭 상업 실습 자율 향상 휴식):** the desk's Sino shelf, and most of it is register work against words you already have. 문서 against M16's 서류, 계좌 against M43's 통장, 매장 against M1's 가게, 명칭 against M1's 이름, 상업 against M43's 사업 and 장사, 자율 against M23's 자유, 향상 against M93's 개선, 휴식 against M8's 쉬다 and M11's 휴가. Two of them pay debts outright: 휴식 lands the noun M61's 취하다 carried frozen in 휴식을 취하다, and 향상 lands the one M101's 생산력 carried in 생산력 향상.
- **THE CLINIC (진단 질병 체력 인체 대합실):** the doctor's register, one rung above the patient's. M14's 병 is what you have and 질병 is what a ministry counts; M14's 몸 aches, M53's 신체 gets measured, and 인체 appears in the diagram; M44's 검사 is the test and 진단 is the name that follows it. 대합실 closes the drawer with three hanja you own from 기대, 합격 and 욕실.

**The flip, run eight times.** 설치하다 → 설치되다 · 발표하다 → 발표되다 · 확인하다 → 확인되다 · 성립하다 → 성립되다 · 집중하다 → 집중되다 · 해결하다 → 해결되다 · 생산하다 → 생산되다. Nothing changes but the ending, and everything changes about the sentence: the 하다 side takes an object and needs a subject, the 되다 side takes a subject and needs nobody. 문제를 해결했어요 says who untied it; 문제가 해결됐어요 says only that the knot is gone. Korean officialdom lives in the second column, and now so can you.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 웬 is a determiner and 왠지 an adverb — if a noun follows, it is 웬, and that one test settles the spelling Koreans get wrong daily · 원고 is 原稿, the manuscript, but also 原告, the plaintiff — NIKL lists the writer's sense, the courtroom keeps the other · 매장 is 賣場, the sales floor, and also 埋葬, burial — no mall directory has ever meant the second · 계좌 is the number, M43's 통장 the booklet that prints it · 자율 is holding yourself, M23's 자유 is nobody holding you · 문서 is the file, M16's 서류 the pile you hand over · 진단 shares its cut-斷 with M34's 판단: one cuts a medical question, the other cuts a decision · 대합실 has nothing to do with the counter 대 of M55 — its 대 is 待, await · and 설치 the noun is not 설치되다 the verb: the button says the first, the notice says the second.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** eight sentences, eight Band 7 patterns, one apiece and named in every note — -(으)므로 from M85 posts the notice about an unconfirmed 원고; -(으)로 인해 from M86 puts a 질병 on file behind a lost 체력; -던 from M88 looks back at the 일 you 그토록 wanted before it was 말없이 해결됐어요; -(으)ㄹ 정도로 from M89 measures a 대합실 holding 무려 천 명; -(으)ㄴ 결과 from M90 hinges a 진단 into a 휴식; -는 김에 from M91 checks a 계좌 번호 on the way to the bank; -기는 하다 from M92 concedes an 원고 that came to 고작 세 문장; and -(으)ㄹ걸요 from M93 guesses that a camera will be 설치될걸요 in that 매장 too. Underneath run the frames you have held since Ring 1: the long negations -지 않다 and -지 못하다 from M28, the past badge -(으)ㄴ and the prospective from M24 and M25, -아/어지다 and -게 되다 from M35, -는데 from M53, 도 from M2, and the 합니다체 from M4 on the one printed line.

Four of the eight put a 되다 flip in the closing slot, and no sentence in the module names the person who installed, confirmed, announced or solved anything. That is not a coincidence; it is the register.
