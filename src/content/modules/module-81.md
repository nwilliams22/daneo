# Korean, Word-First — Module 81: The Notice Board

M75 taught the hinge — any 하다-verb is a noun waiting — and dismounted three; this module dismounts eleven more and reads them where they live: on signs, forms, timetables, and front pages. 안전, 유명, 친절, 중요, 적극, 고속, 사무, 이동, 보호, 공동, 정식 — roots you have only met inside a -하다 verb, a -적 stamp, or a longer compound, now standing bare the way print writes them. Around them, thirteen welds of parts you already own (글자, 우리말, 각국, 대중문화, 등록금, 신문사, 야외, 시외…), several paying IOUs to the letter, and eight radar-heavy singles (종, 질, 고급, 예금, 주택, 동기, 부작용, 국립). The organizing thesis: **the notice board speaks in bare Sino roots; speech puts 하다 or 적 back on** — 안전 운전 on the sign, 안전하게 운전하세요 in the mouth. Thirty-two nouns, no new grammar, a great deal of new reading.

**How to use this:** read every decode before you drill — almost nothing on this shelf is a stranger, and eight firsts (立, 副, 住, 宅, 級, 預, 共, 護) do the rest. Spend your reps on the sense collapses the NIKL list forced (종 seed or bell, 동기 motive or cohort, 정식 official or the set meal) and on the register pairs: 각국 / 나라마다, 이동 / 옮기다, 주택 / 집 — the same meaning at two altitudes.

## Part 1 — Dismount II: the roots print writes

M76's 계산 note named the move — the M75 move, 'Dismount — verb → bare noun, the way print writes' — and this shelf runs it eleven times:

- **Off the adjectives:** 안전하다 (M36), 유명하다 (M17), 친절하다 (M4), 중요하다 (M16) each drop their 하다 and become determiners: 안전 운전, 유명 배우, 친절 교육, 중요 서류. The mouth never says these bare; the wall never says them any other way.
- **Off the -적 stamp:** M45's 적극적 gives up its root — 적극 추천 on the shelf sticker, 적극적으로 in the interview.
- **Out of the compounds:** 고속 walks out of M50's 고속도로, 사무 out of M16's 사무실 — four shipped notes had already called it office-work — and the 계산 precedent holds for all of them.
- **Fresh, but decodable:** 이동 from M54's 이사 + M57's 활동; 보호 from M43's 보험 + a guard-護 you have carried since 간호사; 공동 from a together-共 that is NOT 공원's public-公, + M38's 동시; 정식 from M63's 정확하다 + M55's 식. Each stacks on a sign: 이동 중, 환경 보호, 공동 구매, 정식 직원.
- **The welds pay their debts:** M32's 글 promised 글자; M57's 가능성 promised 중요성; M48's 대중 spelled out 대중문화; M27's 독일 listed 독일어 first; M44's 장학금 was aimed at 등록금 — and M77's 일상생활 showed how four-syllable print welds (생활환경) are built.

## Part 3 — Two altitudes, no new grammar

### 1. Bare root on the wall

Strip the 하다, drop the 적, and the root stacks with no particle: **안전 제일**, **이동 중**, **사무 중**, **정식 직원**, **환경 보호**, **고속 충전**. Signs, forms, and headlines run on this — nouns pressed together, verbs implied — the register M75 named and the S2 sign kit (중, 금지) already read for you. Two learner rules: a bare root before a noun is a determiner (중요 서류 = important documents), and a bare root before 중 is a state (이동 중 = in transit).

### 2. 하다 and 적 back on in the mouth

The same roots in speech re-mount: 안전해요, 유명하잖아요, 직원이 친절해서, 이동합니다, 예금하고 있어요 — and the -적 roots take their stamp back (적극적으로, 한국적인). The -성 factory M57 named ships 중요성 the same way: root in print, suffix in the sentence.

**Calibration:** read the bare root, hear the mounted verb. 안전 운전 on the sign is 안전하게 운전하세요 in a driving lesson; 이동 중 on the bus wall is 지금 이동하고 있어요 on the phone; 유명 배우 in a headline is 유명한 배우 across a table. When you write for a wall, dismount; when you speak to a face, mount. Getting the altitude wrong is not a grammar error — it is sounding like a sign.

## What's next

The long tail keeps unspooling, root by root. You can now read a notice board the way print meant it — bare roots, verbs implied — and say the same thing across a table with the 하다 put back. 안전 제일; 복습은 매일.
