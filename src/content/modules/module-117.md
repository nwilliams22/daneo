# Korean, Word-First — Module 117: The Small Words and the Places

The Grade-C long tail ends in words that cannot stand up by themselves. Six of today's thirty-six — 전, 리, 한, 년생, 수천, 듯싶다 — never appear alone: each is the middle of a frame, and a note that teaches the word without the frame has taught nothing, which is why the frame table comes first and the vocab list second. Around them sit the places the -소 suffix names (사무소, 파출소), the last car of a compass rail M98 laid nineteen modules ago (동부), the sports page's four loanwords and near-loanwords (리그, 결승, 시즌, 달리기), ten verbs that are every one of them a noun with 하다 or 되다 bolted on, and ten things the news and the kitchen keep (마이크, 헬기, 시멘트, 음식물, 혈액, 화살, 제비, 잠자리, 승객, 외모). Sixteen of the thirty-six were already riding frozen inside an older note, and each of those notes quotes the line that promised it. No new grammar: every sentence recycles one Band 7 ending and names it.

**How to use this:** read the frame table before anything else, and never rehearse one of the six small words on its own — say 전 국민, 수천 명, 팔오 년생, 올 듯싶어요, 할 수 있는 한, 읍/면/리, because none of the six has a sentence of its own to be met in. Then read the ten verbs as subtraction: 고집하다 is M45's 고집 with 하다 on it, 주의하다 is S2's 주의 with the same, 신청하다 is M64's 신청, 공격하다 is M98's 공격, 기록되다 is M96's 기록 through the flip — five of the ten cost you nothing but the ending, and the other five bring four new characters between them. Last, check the receipts. NIKL files three of today's words as two rows under one sound (전, 한, 잠자리) and two of them under a sound you already own for something else (리, 전) — the radar patrol below is not decoration, it is the module.

## Part 1 — The small words and the places

**The frames.** Read the right-hand column, not the left:

| word | the frame it lives in | said |
|---|---|---|
| 전 (全) | 전 + noun | 전 국민 · 전 세계 · 전 직원 |
| 수천 (數千) | 수천 + counter | 수천 명 · 수천 개 |
| 년생 (年生) | year (Sino) + 년생 | 팔오 년생 · 구공 년생 |
| 듯싶다 | badge + 듯싶다 | 비가 올 듯싶어요 |
| 리 (里) | number + 리 · address + 리 | 십 리 · 읍/면/리 |
| 한 (限) | badge -는 + 한 | 할 수 있는 한 · 제가 아는 한 |
| 한 (恨) | 한이 + 맺히다 · 한을 + 풀다 | 한이 맺혔어요 |
| 그놈 | 이놈 · 그놈 · 저놈 | 그놈이 또 왔어요 |

Three of these pay debts outright. 한 is the 限 M95 built -는 한 on and left frozen — "살아 있는 한 (M4's 살다) = as long as I live" — and the same character M39's 한계 read as "限界 limit-boundary". 수천 is the car M77's 수년 left sitting on its rail, "수십/수백/수천 — several tens, hundreds, thousands — riding frozen". 듯싶다 is the twin M87's 듯하다 named and then let ride, "its twin 듯싶다 (riding frozen) is the same word in a softer voice". And 리 extends a rail rather than closing one: M99's 면 ran the address down to "the township — 읍/면/동, the rural rung below the county", and 리 is the rung under 면, the village the postbox actually stands in.

**The -소 rail** is M37's, and that note laid it in one line: "The -소 place-suffix files alongside 주유소 (gas station) and 파출소 (police box); small shops, essential errands." 파출소 arrives named. Beside it 사무소 — 事務所, the 事 of M69's 행사, the 務 of M57's 업무, and 所 — which is not M16's 사무실: a 실 is a room inside a building, a 소 is the premises with its own sign, and the M90 gap deck has been holding one of these frozen since it explained the ID card, "The 동사무소, the neighbourhood office (riding frozen), issues it". The other places are read by their second halves: 항구 is 港 plus the 口 M64's 인구 decoded, 영화제 is M2's 영화 plus the 祭 M25's 축제 stopped short of, and 리그 · 결승 · 시즌 · 달리기 are the four words a broadcast needs, two borrowed and two built.

**The compass rail closes.** M98's 남부 laid the whole thing and left three cars frozen — "the rail runs 북부, 동부, 서부 (frozen — the north, east and west parts) and 중부 (frozen) for the middle" — and it has been collected one car per module ever since: M103's 중부 took the middle, M106's 북부 the north, M110's 서부 the west. 동부 is the last one standing, and with it 북부, 남부, 중부, 동부, 서부 have all landed. Nineteen modules, five words, one 部.

**The verbs and the things.** Ten verbs, and the interesting halves are few: 注 in 주의하다, 拂 in 지불하다, 蒐 in 수집하다, 派 in 파출소, 港 in 항구, 血 and 液 in 혈액, 貌 in 외모 — seven new characters in a thirty-six-word module. The things are a different kind of work. 헬기 is filed by NIKL as "helicopter機", a clipped English loan welded to a hanja, which is a word shape you have not met before; 음식물 exists in daily life for exactly one compound, 음식물 쓰레기, and both M13's 쓰레기 and the M114 gap deck had already promised it; 시멘트 pays M104's 굳다 and 승객 pays the wink M50's 승용차 has been owing since "the 승 is boarding-乘, the one that also lifts 승객 (passengers) and elevators — winks only"; and 외모 is one of the few words where the register lesson is about your listener rather than your grammar.

**Radar patrol:** 전 is 全 before a noun (전 국민) and 前 before a verb or inside 오전 — two characters, one syllable, and the slot decides · 리 is 里 in an address and 理 in 그럴 리가 없어요, which the M87 glue table files as "리 (a reason — frozen)" · 한 is three tenants: 恨 the grief, 限 the fence inside -는 한, and the counter-contraction 한 that M5 taught and never gave an entry to · 잠자리 is the bed here and a dragonfly elsewhere, a genuine pitch pair — the dragonfly sits far down NIKL's list and is not today's word · 화살표's 표 is 標, a mark, not the ticket-票 of M12's 표 · 혈액형's 형 is 型, a type, not M4's 형, the older brother · 사무소 is a firm's premises, 파출소 the one-room police box, 경찰서 the station above it · 수집하다's 수 is 蒐 (gather), not the 數 of 수천 two shelves along; and its 집 is 集, not M1's 집 · 고집하다's 집 is 執, a grip, and also not M1's 집 · 대처하다 takes 에 and never 를 · and 지불하다 is the customer paying over, M114's 지급하다 the institution paying out, M28's 내다 what the mouth says at the counter.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** eight sentences, eight Band 7 patterns, one apiece and named in every note — 에 따르면 from M90 sources a 헬기 into an 동부 항구; -는 김에 from M91 hitches a 지불하다 onto a trip to the bank; -(으)ㄴ/는 셈이다 from M87 reckons a 시즌 a success for having reached the 결승; -는 바람에 from M86 strands 수천 명의 승객 at an airport; -(으)며 from M85 writes the notice in which a 신청 is taken at the 사무소 and a 결과 기록됩니다; -더라고요 from M88 reports a 잠자리 that turned out 불편하더라고요; -길래 from M93 watches 제비 fly low and guesses 비가 올 듯싶었어요; and -는 한 from M95 draws a fence out of the very 한 this module teaches, and finds that a 계약 does not 성립하다 inside it. Underneath run the frames you have held since Ring 1: the badges -(으)ㄴ/-는 and the prospective from M24 and M25, -(으)니까 from M10, the long negation -지 않다 from M28, -게 from M33, the 합니다체 from M4, and the double-subject shape from M24.

**Gap deck:** the untranslatable that is 정's opposite number (한), the letter every Korean knows you by (혈액형), and the fair way to settle anything (제비를 뽑다).

## What's next

The Grade-C list keeps unspooling, and the shape of it is now plain: a long tail is not rarer words, it is words that need more scaffolding — a frame, a second row, a compound that is the only place they live. You have closed the compass rail, the -소 rail is running, and five earlier notes stopped owing you something today. No new grammar is coming; the endings closed at M96, and from here it is words, registers and machines you already own. 할 수 있는 한 계속 읽으세요.
