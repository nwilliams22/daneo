# Korean, Word-First — Module 119: Verbs of Doing and Undoing

A third of this pack is already written in older notes. Eleven verbs were named, glossed and left frozen there; today they leave the margins and become entries. The rest show machinery the learner already owns: a passive, a causative, a 되다 flip, a 받다 weld, or 하다 attached to an owned noun. Two words, 거품 and 끝없다, hold the edge of the shelf.

**How to use this:** start with the receipt table and say what the older note could already tell you. Next, sort the active and receiving sides of each verb. Only then read the sentences; their Band 7 endings are recycled, not new glue.

## Part 1 — Verbs of doing and undoing

**The receipts.** Each left a promise in an earlier note:

| arriving verb | owner | the promised frame |
|---|---|---|
| 끼어들다 | M107 끼우다 | traffic and conversation cut-ins |
| 인정받다 | M93 인정하다 | 인정 + 받다, recognition received |
| 어찌하다 | M61 어쩌다 · M85 어찌 | the long how-do form |
| 소유하다 | M98 소유 | ownership made a verb |
| 알아내다 | M74 찾아내다 | knowledge brought out |
| 부러지다 | M82 나뭇가지 | a branch snaps |
| 예방하다 | M108 예방 | prevention made an act |
| 전환하다 | M88 전환 | change with a hand on the switch |
| 감소하다 | M89 줄어들다 | a reported fall |
| 괴롭히다 | M42 괴롭다 | suffering caused to someone |
| 잘리다 | M40 자르다 | a passive cut, or dismissal |

**The receiving side** matters more than an English passive label. M93's 인정하다 has an agent who recognizes; 인정받다 has the person receiving that recognition. M40's 자르다 cuts; 잘리다 is cut. M75's 반복하다 repeats deliberately; 반복되다 reports that repetition happened. M115's 허용 becomes 허용되다 when the rule, not the rule-maker, is foregrounded.

**The 되다 and 받다 forms** extend known nouns and verbs rather than teaching new grammar. 예정되다 schedules what M57's 예정 names; 이해되다 lets understanding happen or fail; 행해지다 reports an action carried out without an actor. The old forms remain useful; this is a change of camera angle.

**The Sino verbs** fill the official page. 동의하다 agrees, 적응하다 adapts, 의논하다 confers, 반성하다 reflects on a wrong, 대표하다 represents, 유의하다 asks for attention. 행하다 performs where 하다 simply does; 도망치다 flees and 토하다 vomits or pours out words. 용서하다 asks for forgiveness without an office at all.

**The native verbs** make pictures. 마음먹다 eats a decision; 불어오다 blows toward us; 타고나다 brings a trait into birth; 나르다 hauls loads; 놀리다 teases; 늘어놓다 lays out things or excuses; 두려워하다 describes another person's fear; 쓸다 sweeps a floor. Keep the full verbs together rather than practising their syllables apart.

::vocab::

**The two edge words.** 거품 is foam on beer or a bubble in an economy. 끝없다 has M28's 끝 and M1's 없다; M120 will land the adverb 끝없이. Neither adds a new grammar part to this module.

**Radar patrol:** 부러지다 snaps in two, M116's 부서지다 breaks to pieces · 놀리다 usually teases, despite its make-play form · 도망치다's 치다 adds force rather than striking · 토하다's 吐 is not the 討 of M101's 검토 · 쓸다 sweeps, M10's 쓰다 writes or uses · 나르다 hauls, M28's 날다 flies · 두렵다 names my fear, 두려워하다 describes someone else's.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** 에 따르면 from M90 reports the decline; -는 바람에 from M86 makes a cut-in spoil a story; -(으)ㄹ 만하다 from M87 judges a problem worth discussing; -더라고요 from M88 reports witnessed bullying; -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 links practice to recognition; -고도 from M85 concedes a dismissal before starting again; -는 김에 from M91 uses a search to find the cause; -더라도 from M95 keeps an instruction in force despite repeated mistakes.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** 마음먹기에 달렸다 makes resolve the hinge; 이해가 안 돼요 is the ordinary way to ask for an explanation; 회사에서 잘리다 is the blunt office meaning of a passive cut.
