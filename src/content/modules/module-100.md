# Korean, Word-First — Module 100: The Second Front Page

The news is made of nouns that were once verbs. Ring 1 taught you the verb the mouth uses — 연락해!, 화났어?, 이겼어요 — and the headline uses the noun instead: 연락, 분노, 승리. This slice is the second front page, the one about the fight and about the press itself: thirty-four Grade-C words in five drawers — THE PRESS (연락 회견 성명 사설 홍보), THE FIGHT (비난 논쟁 지지 경고 위협 차별 구분 구분하다 분노 의무 승리 좌우 흔적), THE STATE II (정치권 정치인 총리 수도권 민주), THE LAW II (처벌 살인 범인 손해) and THE QUANTIFIERS (소위 총 수십 대량 특수 동일하다 지속적). M32 turned on the television; M48 gave you 언론 and 기사; M90 gave you the state and its law; M97 the economy page. This module is the page where those institutions argue.

**How to use this:** decode first — every word is Sino-Korean and you own a half of nearly every one (連 會 見 明 社 說 報 非 論 爭 支 持 告 威 差 別 區 分 義 務 勝 利 政 治 權 人 總 理 首 都 民 主 所 數 十 大 量 特 同 一 的 處 犯 害); the firsts are 絡 聲 弘 難 警 脅 憤 怒 左 右 痕 跡 圈 謂 銃 殊 續 罰 殺 損. Then read the shelf as one machine: the 하다-noun with its 하다 removed. 비난 논쟁 지지 경고 위협 차별 분노 all take 하다 to become verbs — most of those verbs ride frozen here, on purpose, because the page prints the noun and hands it to 받다, 벌어지다, 보내다 instead. The sentence deck is eight headlines, each riding one Band 7 ending — read the note to find which.

## Part 1 — The second front page

Five drawers, and one habit that opens all of them: where the mouth would say a verb, the page says a noun and gives it a verb of its own.

- **THE PRESS (연락 회견 성명 사설 홍보):** the noun inside M19's 연락하다, dismounted at last (연락 — three earlier notes rode it frozen); the room where the statement is read (회견 — only ever 기자 회견); the statement itself (성명 — 聲明, and on a form the same syllables are your name, 姓名); the paper speaking in its own voice (사설 — the 社 of 사회 in its company job, against M48's 기사 that only records); and the company speaking about itself (홍보 — against M32's 광고, which pays for the space).
- **THE FIGHT (비난 논쟁 지지 경고 위협 차별 구분 구분하다 분노 의무 승리 좌우 흔적):** what the page prints about the argument. Attack and dispute (비난 — attacks where M93's 비판 argues; 논쟁 — a 토론 without rules), the pollster's noun (지지, paid to M81's 적극), the card and the menace (경고, 위협 — the 威 of M90's 시위 turned on you), the difference someone paid for (차별, against M57's 차이) and the sorting that costs nothing (구분, with its 하다), the editorial's anger (분노, against M19's 화나다), the civics-test twin (의무, paid to M90's 권리), the sports page's noun (승리, paid twice — M93's 이끌다 and M94's 거두다), the two sides at once (좌우), and what stays behind (흔적 — 跡 the footprint, not the -적 stamp).
- **THE STATE II (정치권 정치인 총리 수도권 민주):** the political class as one body (정치권 — 權), the person in the trade (정치인 — the word M32's 정치 note built in passing), Korea's own head of cabinet (총리 — paid to M78's 수상, which pointed abroad), the ring around the head-city (수도권 — 圈, paid to M50's 전철), and the bare root of M90's 민주주의 (민주, as a prefix on 국가 and 사회).
- **THE LAW II (처벌 살인 범인 손해):** what the court hands out (처벌 — a 벌 that needed a judge), the report's word for M37's 죽이다 (살인), the one who did it (범인 — the 犯 of M90's 범죄), and the red column (손해 — the shadow M43's 이익 note promised).
- **THE QUANTIFIERS (소위 총 수십 대량 특수 동일하다 지속적):** the tongs (소위), the invoice's last line and the crime page's weapon (총 — 總 and 銃, one sound), the first rung of the rail M77's 수년 set (수십), the factory's scale (대량 — paid to M97's 생산), the ministry's category (특수 — against M57's 특별), the contract's same (동일하다 — against M24's 같다) and the report's continuous (지속적 — against M28's 계속).

**Receipts come due.** Band 8 thaws the riders of earlier shelves, and this one cashes fourteen:

- M19's 연락하다, M70's 끊어지다 and M83's 연락처 all rode the noun frozen — **연락** lands it.
- M81's 적극 wrote 적극 지지 with the noun frozen — **지지** is the pollster's word.
- M93's 이끌다 and M94's 거두다 both filed 승리 as a rider — **승리** pays two notes at once.
- M90's 권리 named its twin on every civics test — **의무** arrives, and the test can be taken.
- M78's 수상 and s_m78_pm_visit both said Korea's own head of cabinet is the 총리, frozen — **총리** is paid.
- M50's 전철 ran the capital-region network — **수도권** is the region.
- M90's 민주주의 read the root as the people as masters, and M90's 민주화 built on it — **민주** stands bare.
- M43's 이익 said its shadow waits on a later shelf — **손해** is the shelf.
- M77's 수년 set the rail 수십/수백/수천 frozen — **수십** is the first rung; the other two keep riding.
- M97's 생산 wrote 대량 생산 frozen one module ago, and M98's 부대 wrote 특수 부대 frozen — **대량** and **특수** land together.
- M58's 총장 left 總 frozen — **총** is that character standing bare, with a second 총 (銃) beside it.
- M90's 범죄 listed the 범죄자 — **범인** is the one who did this particular crime.

**The noun rule, one shelf later:** the M97 md ran the economy page on 하다/되다 twins; this page runs on the noun ALONE. 비난을 받다, 경고를 보내다, 지지를 얻다, 처벌을 받다, 손해를 보다 — the news hands the noun to a plain verb it already owns (M9's 받다 above all), and keeps the 하다 for the party being accused of doing it. Read a headline and count: the nouns are today's, the verbs are Ring 1's.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 총 before a number is 總 (총 열 명), 총 with 을 is 銃 (총을 쏘다) — two NIKL rows, one sound · 성명 you 발표하다 is 聲明, a statement; 성명 on a form is 姓名, your name (M33's 성함 holds the 姓) · three 권: 정치권 is 權 (power), 수도권 is 圈 (a ring), and M5's 권 counts books · 살인 is murder, but the 살인 of s_m53_momsal_guess (몸살인 것 같아요) is 몸살 + the copula — nobody died · 흔적 ends in 跡, a footprint, not the -적 of M45 · 지지 is support; the 지지 of 만지지 마세요 is two grammar pieces, and the 지지 said to a toddler is dirt · 손해 is 損害, not M8's 손 + M11's 해 · 좌우 is left and right; its 우 is 右, not the 優 of M51's 우승 · 민주 has no week in it — not M8's 주.

**Calibration, three ladders.** The anger: 화났어요 (M19's 화나다 — across a table) · 분노를 느꼈어요 (today — the editorial's noun) · 분노가 폭발했어요 (the headline). The criticism: 지적 (M93 — one finger on one flaw) · 비판 (M93 — an argument with reasons) · 비난 (today — an attack). The winning: 이겼어요 (M17's 이기다 — the mouth) · 승리를 거두었어요 (today's 승리 on M94's 거두다 — the sports page) · 우승했어요 (M51's 우승 — the whole tournament). Same event, one register per rung, and the second front page speaks from the top one.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 ending, named in its note — -(으)며 from M85 lets the 총리 issue a 성명 and send an 경고 in one written breath; -(으)ㄴ 채 from M94 lets the 범인 vanish with no 흔적 left; -(으)ㄴ/는 셈이다 from M87 adds up the 소위 전문가 and gets 홍보; -더라고요 from M88 reports the 지지 the 수도권 showed on election night; -(으)ㄹ 정도로 from M89 measures a 분노 by the 총리 it removed; 에 의해 from M90 puts a 정치인 under the law's 처벌; -기는커녕 from M92 watches the 정치권 skip ending the 논쟁 and go straight to 비난; and 반면에 from M95 weighs 수도권 growth against the provinces. Underneath, the frames you have held since Ring 1: the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24, the -지 않다 long negation from M28, -는데 from M53, the -게 adverbializer from M33, the -고 있다 progressive from M10, and the 합니다체 from M4 on the six printed sentences.

Six of the eight are printed and two are said; every note names the one ending that made the difference.
