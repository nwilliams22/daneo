# Korean, Word-First — Module 110: The Town and the Old Ways

Thirty-six words for a town that keeps two vocabularies and never mixes them up. **The offices name themselves with two-hanja verbs turned into nouns** — 허가, 상담, 심사, 승진, 투표 and 실현 are every one of them a thing you could do with 하다, standing perfectly still as a noun on a form, because that is the register a form is written in. The old ways keep the native words the offices never came for: 살림 is the running of a house and 호박 is the squash in it, and neither has a hanja anywhere. In between sit the words that map a place — 사방, 해안, 서부, 인근, 시중 — and the words that name what a body and a kitchen do: 화장, 주방, 부품, 분량, 의욕, 절망, 공해. Nine of today's entries were already riding frozen inside an older note, and each one quotes the line that promised it. No new grammar: every sentence recycles one Band 7 ending and names it.

**How to use this:** read the office drawer as a list of VERBS with the 하다 taken off — if 심사하다 and 승진하다 are obvious to you, fifteen words cost you almost nothing — and then spend your reps on the pairs this shelf sorts, because the pairs are the whole difficulty: 풍습 vs 관습, 경치 vs 풍경 vs 광경, 바다 vs 바닷가 vs 해안, 근처 vs 부근 vs 인근, 근로 vs 노동, 대다수 vs 대부분, 주방 vs 부엌, 오염 vs 공해, 욕심 vs 의욕, 실망 vs 절망.

## Part 1 — The town and the old ways, in three drawers

**The old ways (13).** 풍습 관습 청춘 곡 곡식 살림 호박 광경 사방 해안 서부 인근 시중 — the customs, the songs, the harvest, the house, and the map the town reads itself on. Two of them are native and proud of it (살림, 호박); the rest are Sino compounds built from halves you already own, and four of them arrive paying a debt: 청춘 was promised by M49's 푸르다, 곡 by M96's 희곡, 곡식 by M94's 거두다, and 서부 by M98's 남부, which laid the compass rail and left this car sitting on it.

**The office II (15).** 허가 상담 심사 정기 제의 승진 경력 근로 명의 부회장 대다수 일반인 흑인 연예인 투표. This is the drawer the thesis is about. 許可 is allow plus possible, 相談 is mutual plus talk, 審査 is examine plus examine, 昇進 is rise plus advance — four verbs, every one of which Korean files as a NOUN and hands 하다 whenever it wants the verb back. Around them stand the people the office counts: the 부회장 at the top, the 일반인 and the 연예인 on either side of a camera, the 대다수 that carries a vote, and the 근로자 the statute calls by its own name.

**The body and the kitchen (8).** 화장 주방 부품 분량 의욕 실현 절망 공해 — makeup, the professional kitchen, the component, the running length, the drive, the realization, the despair, and the harm pollution does to everybody. Seven of the eight are two owned halves and nothing else; only 주방 brings a genuinely new character, the kitchen-廚.

**Read the drawers by their registers.** Almost every word in the old-ways drawer is the third member of a set you already own, and the set is always the same shape: a word you SAY, a word a report WRITES, and today's, which is the one that goes on a map, a form or a chart. Learn the row, not the word:

| what it names | the one you say | the one in print | today's |
|---|---|---|---|
| nearby | 근처 (M12) | 부근 (M31) | 인근 |
| the sea's edge | 바다 (M11) | 바닷가 (M23) | 해안 |
| something seen | 경치 (M22) | 풍경 (M49) | 광경 |
| labour | 일 (M2) | 노동 (M97) | 근로 |
| a kitchen | 부엌 (M13) | — | 주방 |
| most of them | 대부분 (M32) | 다수 (M76) | 대다수 |

The office drawer has no such rows, and that is the point of it: those fifteen words have no spoken twin at all. Nobody says 심사 to a friend — they say 봐 주세요, and the form says 심사.

**Radar patrol:** 곡 is 曲, the tune — the 곡 of M92's 계곡 is 谷, a valley, and nothing in it is musical · 화장 is 化粧, makeup, and M6's 화장실 is literally that room; a second 화장 is 火葬, cremation, which is not on the list and rides frozen · the 제의 in 문제의 본질 (M91's 본질) and 화제의 인물 (M96's 화제) is a noun plus 의, not today's 提議 · the 명의 in 생명의 위협 (M100's 위협) is 생명 plus 의, not today's 名義 — two office words and two false welds, the same trick twice · 사방 is 四方, four directions — not M8's 방, the room, exactly as M31's 방향 warned · 시중 is 市中 with 中 welded in FRONT, where the busy-sign machine of S2 hangs it behind (영업 중) · 해안's 안 is 岸, a shore — neither M3's 안 nor M6's 안, and nothing is inside anything · 주방's 주 is 廚, not M8's 주 (the week) · 분량's 분 is 分, not M5's 분 (the minute counter), and it is SAID 불량 · 부회장's 부 is 副, secondary, while M58's 부장 wears the section-部 of M32's 부분 — two 부s on one org chart · 상담's 상 is 相, not M40's 상 (the dinner table) · 정기 is 定 plus 期 — not M26's 정 and not M99's 기 · 공해 is 公 plus 害, not M17's 공 (the ball) and not M11's 해 (the sun).

**The IOUs, paid.** Nine entries were already in the corpus, frozen inside somebody else's note: 청춘 ("청춘 — 'blue spring', youth itself" — M49), 곡 ("한 곡 (one song, riding frozen) is what the 노래방 (M17) charges by" — M96), 곡식 ("곡식을 거두다 (곡식 — grain, riding frozen) = bring in the grain" — M94), 서부 ("the rail runs 북부, 동부, 서부 (frozen — the north, east and west parts)" — M98), 호박 ("호박죽 for comfort" — M37), 명의 ("공동 명의 (frozen) on the deed" — M81), 일반인 ("일반인 = an ordinary person" — M57), 화장 ("진한 화장 (makeup, riding frozen) took an hour" — M60), and 공해 ("소음 공해 (공해 — pollution, riding frozen; M99's 오염 is its cousin) = noise pollution" — M103). Read each entry for its receipt before its meaning.

**Calibration, four pairs.** Custom: 풍습 is what a village does at M25's 설날, 관습 is what an institution treats as binding with nothing written down. Scene: M22's 경치 is the view you exclaim at, M49's 풍경 the landscape you photograph, 광경 the thing that was going on when you turned around. Nearby: M12's 근처 is what you say, M31's 부근 what a report says of a landmark, 인근 what a notice says of a whole area. Labour: M97's 노동 sorted it for you — 근로 is the ministry's word, 노동 the union's.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 ending, named in its note — -(으)므로 from M85 lets an office explain on paper why a 허가 waits on a 심사; 에 비해 from M86 puts 관습 and 풍습 on the two pans of one comparison; -더라고요 from M88 reports what a 곡 did to somebody's 청춘; -다 보니 from M94 gets 살림 done long enough that the 주방 stops being hard; -(으)ㄹ 뿐만 아니라 from M89 brings in 곡식 and 호박 with M94's 거두다, paying the grain's IOU in a sentence; 에 따라 from M90 traces a 대다수 and a 제의 back to a 투표; -(으)ㄹ 겸 from M91 sends somebody to M16's 부장님 partly about 승진; and -기는 하다 from M92 grants that a 꿈 got its 실현 and notes that the 의욕 had already gone. Underneath, the frames you have held since Ring 1: -(으)ㄹ 때 from M25 with 마다 from M31, the long negation from M28, the can-machine -(으)ㄹ 수 있다 from M10, 도 from M2, and the 되다 flip from M10.

## What's next

Band 8 keeps unspooling the Grade-C list by rank and root. Several of today's nouns are already booked as somebody else's parents — 실현 and 투표 take 되다 and 하다, 승진 and 화장 want their verbs, and 절망 is waiting on its 실망 — so today's frozen riders are a later module's entries, the way 청춘 and 곡식 were somebody else's riders until this morning. No new grammar; the endings are finished and the words are the work. 한 곡 더, 다음 모듈입니다.
