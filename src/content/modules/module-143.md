# Korean, Word-First — Module 143: Night Work, Family and Small Things

The night brings grading, visitors and a late return home. Small household objects sit beside strong judgments: what is shoddy, what is precious, and what deserves care.

**How to use this:** Read the scene shelves, then learn the vocabulary before opening the sentences. Follow each chunk through Korean, literal gloss and natural English.

> **Warm-up — transfer from Module 142.** Say: (1) The return home is required tonight. (2) The space behind the shop is dark. (3) The lowest price still feels expensive. Build fresh combinations, not a sentence from the previous page.

## Part 1 — A busy night and a valued home

**The public scene.** 제출 hands work in; 채점 evaluates it. 관람객 watches, 전문직 names a trained occupation, and 귀가 records the trip home.

**The physical scene.** 수도꼭지 controls water. 습기 is dampness; 캄캄하다 describes darkness. 반짝이다 gives a glint, and 잔디밭 names the grassy area.

**A sense to guard.** 바가지 is a bowl and the image in an overcharging idiom. 음력 is lunar time. 싸구려 judges poor quality, while 소중히 describes caring for something valued.
