# Korean, Word-First — Module 29: The 그렇다 Machine and the Little Words

The sweep's grammar-adjacent module. One humble adjective — **그렇다**, *to be that way* — turns out to be the factory behind half of Korean's connectives, including two you've used since Module 7 without meeting their maker. Around it: the bare pointers 이/그/저, the everyone-words, and the tiny adverbs that run real speech.

> **Warm-up (Module 28):** build these out loud — Module 28 blocks, combinations you've never seen written: *Put the wallet in the bag* (지갑을 가방 속에 넣으세요) · *Suddenly a bird flew up* (갑자기 새가 날았어요) · *I held the baby for a moment* (잠깐 아기를 안았어요). If the blocks don't snap together, visit Review first.

**How to use this:** nothing here is long or hard — these are the two-syllable words that appear in every third sentence of real Korean. Small words, enormous mileage.

---

## Part 1 — One adjective, six connectives

그렇다 = to be that way (ㅎ-family, like M24's 어떻다): 그래요 — that's right / I see. 그랬어요? — was it so? Now watch it manufacture the words between sentences:

- 그러면 (→ **그럼**) — if-that's-so: *then*
- 그러니까 — because-that's-so: *so, as I said*
- 그렇지만 — that's-so-BUT: *however* (M7's 하지만's formal cousin)
- **그러나** — the written *but*: books and news only; say it aloud and you sound like an editorial
- And the cold cases close: M7's 그런데 (그렇+ㄴ데) and 그래서 (그렇+아서) were this machine's earliest shipments, and M24's 그런 its badge form.

One import joins the family: **왜냐하면** — "if you ask why…" — the *because* that opens an answer, usually landing on -기 때문이에요 (meet 때문 below).

## Part 2 — The bricks

The pointers, the every-words, and speech's smallest workhorses.

::vocab::

**The pointer row, finally bare:** 이/그/저 — this/that/that-yonder — have hidden inside 이거 (M16), 이런 (M24), and 이쪽 (M23) all along. Naked, they clip onto any noun: 이 책, 그 사람, 저 건물. And 그 does double duty as Korean's *the*: 그 영화 — the movie (we both know which).

---

## Part 3 — The glue

### 1. 때문에 — because of

[noun] + **때문에**: 눈 때문에 — because of the snow; 너 때문에 — because of YOU (credit or blame, tone decides). The formal engine -기 때문이에요 ("it's because…") answers 왜냐하면 — recognize it now, produce it in Ring 2.

### 2. 아무 + negative — nobody, nothing

**아무** is the any-pointer, and with a negative it empties the room: 아무**도** 없어요 — nobody's here. 아무**것도** 몰라요 — I know nothing. 아무 때나 — anytime at all (the positive side). The indefinite system's front door.

### 3. The degree ladder, completed

M11 gave you 아주/너무/진짜/정말; M22 added 제일. The formal rungs arrive: **매우** (very — the textbook's favorite), **가장** (most — 제일's written twin: 가장 좋아하는 노래), and 참 (truly — the warm one grandmothers use). Same ladder, dressier suits.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** 겨울이 왔어요. 그러나 춥지 않았어요 narrates with M28's long negation in the written register, 나쁜 꿈 때문에 잠을 못 잤어요 blames the night on M28's 잠, and 아이고, 벌써 끝이네요 sighs over M28's 끝 with M26's noticing ending.

::sentences::

**Sigh check (gap deck):** 아이고 — the all-purpose Korean exhalation — has its own entry. Learn its four jobs before your knees learn them for you.

## Part 5 — Practice

Cover the right column and translate; then flip and build the Korean.

1. Really? I didn't know! → 그래요? 몰랐어요!
2. Well then, see you tomorrow! → 그럼, 내일 봐요!
3. Korean is hard. But it's fun. → 한국어는 어려워요. 그렇지만 재미있어요.
4. Nobody is here. → 아무도 없어요.
5. Don't worry! → 걱정하지 마세요!
6. Everyone knows that story. → 모든 사람이 그 이야기를 알아요.
7. Because of a bad dream, I couldn't sleep. → 나쁜 꿈 때문에 잠을 못 잤어요.
8. Which season do you like most? → 어느 계절을 가장 좋아해요?

Connective drill: tell yesterday as three sentences, joined once each by 그래서, 그런데, and 그러니까 — then swap all three for their 그렇다-siblings and feel the register shift.
