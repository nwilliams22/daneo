# Korean, Word-First — Module 134: Portraits, Resolve and Helping Hands

A portrait looks different under new lighting. A plausible account draws you in. A helping hand makes a small act matter. The words here connect what you see to how people act.

**How to use this:** Learn one shelf at a time. Then read the Korean and English layers together, tracing the same chunk across both. This is a vocabulary pack; the grammar is already yours.

> **Warm-up — transfer from Module 133.** Say: (1) “The farm is elegant.” (2) “The instructor proposed a pause.” (3) “The storyline changed.” Make each scene more specific with one word from an earlier module.

## Part 1 — Words that change the scene

**Art and accounts.** 초상화 is a painted portrait, 극작가 writes for the stage, and 국사 studies national history. 경제학 is a field of study; 관심사 is a subject that interests someone. Something can be 흥미롭다 without being your own area of expertise.

**Firmness and first impressions.** 각오 prepares you for a challenge; 굳히다 makes that resolve firm. 그럴듯하다 only says that an account seems plausible. 끌리다 adds the attraction you feel, but neither word proves the account true. 경고하다 can be the useful interruption that makes you check.

**Movement and touch.** A 담 marks a property boundary; an object can 기울다 or 미끄러지다. 들여놓다 brings something inside and leaves it there; 내보내다 sends something out. 손길 moves from a literal touch to help and care. 따스하다 and 상쾌하다 describe different pleasant sensations: gentle warmth and freshness.

**Sense radar.** 동서 here joins east and west. 기적 is a miracle, not a whistle. 명령어 belongs to computing. 부친 is a formal way to refer to a father; it is not the normal call across the kitchen.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** M133's 조명, 줄거리, 서늘하다 and 농장 all return in new sentences. The patterns are familiar: -더라도 keeps resolve despite obstacles, -길래 gives an observed reason, and 조차 includes even a small or unlikely case. No new grammar is needed.

## Practice

1. Change the light in the portrait scene and predict the result.
2. Explain what a playwright may leave out of a 줄거리.
3. Contrast 서늘하다 with 따스하다 using two places.
4. Use 각오 with a different familiar verb.
5. Why does 그럴듯하다 fall short of proving something true?
6. Make a fresh example with M133’s 차선 and 미끄러지다.
7. Name the difference between a literal 손길 and help.
8. Explain why 국사 and 경제학 are fields rather than people.

**Gap deck:** 마음을 굳히다 settles one's resolve. 손길이 필요하다 can mean that help is needed, even when nobody needs a literal touch.
