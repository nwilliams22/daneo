# Korean, Word-First — Module 114: Sino Verbs IV, and the Adverbs of Degree

By now the verb is arithmetic and the adverb is not. Thirty-six Grade-C words, and twenty-five of them are a sum: eight are a noun you already own plus 하다, six are a noun or a verb you already own plus 되다, and eleven more are Sino verbs whose characters mostly fell out of compounds you have been reading since Ring 1. M70's 마련되다 wrote the licence for the 되다 column in one line — "되다 doesn't check pedigree, only the 하다 it replaces" — M105 ran that row eight times and M109 ran it ten more, and today's six close it out. Ten of today's entries arrive on a promise an earlier note made — most of them riding frozen inside it — so their notes are receipts, and each one quotes the line that owed them. The eleven adverbs get the ink, because there is no machine that builds them, nothing to decode in most of them, and nothing to do but meet each one in the company it keeps.

**How to use this:** take each table below as a single exercise rather than a list of rows. For the 하다 column, say the noun, say the verb, and ask what the 하다 added — usually an object slot and nothing else. For the 되다 column, say the 하다 side, say the 되다 side, and ask who disappeared. Then spend the reps on the adverbs, and spend them on the COLLOCATION: 벌떡 has exactly one verb, 불과 needs a quantity behind it, 막상 promises a reversal, and 재빨리 is a reflex rather than a speed. An adverb learned alone is an adverb you will never use.

## Part 1 — Sino verbs IV, and the adverbs of degree

**The 하다 on nouns you own.** Eight verbs, eight receipts — and two of the eight sit on a character rather than a noun, which is the more interesting half: neither the argument-論 nor 請 can stand alone as a word, but you have read both inside 논문, 토론, 언론, 신청, 요청 and 초청장, and with 하다 behind them they need no lookup at all.

**The new Sino verbs.** Eleven, and seven decode entirely from characters you already hold: 실현하다, 구별하다, 완성하다, 선정하다, 적합하다, 조절하다, 지급하다. Only four bring a genuinely new character — 描 in 묘사하다, 測 in 예측하다, 宣 in 선언하다, 誕 in 탄생하다 — and each of those four stands beside a character you own, so even the firsts arrive with a neighbour to lean on. 적합하다 is the group's one adjective, and it is the third ruler on a shelf M73 and M63 had already half-built.

**The 되다 flips, part two.** Same machine, same question, sixth outing — and the last row is why the pair was kept together: 완성하다 needs the architect, 완성되다 needs only the building, and M108's 완성 had promised both, calling them "one module away from being yours outright".

**The adverbs.** Eleven, and only five decode at all — 수없이 is the 수 of M63's 수많다 with 없이 behind it, 자연히 is M22's 자연 on the -히 rack, and 지극히, 간혹 and 종종 are Sino pairs whose halves you own. The other six are shapes rather than sums: 불과 leans on a quantity, 갈수록 is an ending frozen onto M1's 가다, 막상 promises a reversal, 오로지 is M73's 오직 leaning harder, 벌떡 is a mimetic with one verb, and 재빨리 is M12's 빨리 with a prefix. **Radar patrol:** 논하다 is 論 and argues, while M102's 논 is native and grows M59's 쌀 — same syllable, opposite worlds · M34's 예상 guesses from the gut, 예측하다 puts an instrument on it · three rulers, not one: M73's 적당하다 is good enough, M63's 알맞다 fits just right, 적합하다 meets a requirement on paper · M37's 맞추다 sets a thing to a target and lets go, 조절하다 is the hand still on the dial · people 태어나다 (M25), records and companies 탄생하다 — ask what has a mother · the 막 inside 막상 is none of the three careers M62's 막 counted · M103's 극히 is drier, 지극히 warmer, and both belong to the page · M89's 불과하다 is a verb that wants 에 in front of it, today's 불과 a bare adverb that wants a number behind · M100's 구분하다 sorts a set into groups, 구별하다 holds two things up side by side · and M12's 빨리 is a speed you can ask for, 재빨리 a reflex nobody can order.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** eight sentences, eight Band 7 patterns, one apiece and named in every note — -(으)ㄴ 결과 from M90 hinges a 시장 that a government 개방하다-ed into a fiercer 경쟁; -(으)며 from M85 lets one 부장님 보고하다 and 논하다 in a single written breath; 에 비해 from M86 sets a 안정된 생활 against last year's and sends the speaker 종종 travelling; -았/었더니 from M88 finds a head dizzy after a 벌떡 일어나다; -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 arrives already fossilized as 갈수록 and pushes a crowd until somebody 재빨리 이동하다-s; -기 마련이다 from M87 states the law this whole app runs on, that 수없이 반복하면 실력이 자연히 늘어요; -다시피 from M91 opens the line in which 자유 is 보장되다 by 법 and by nobody; and -(으)ㄹ걸요 from M93 lets 막상 do what it always does, overturn the expectation, on a 지극히 간단한 문제. Underneath run the frames you have held since Ring 1: -아/어서 from M10, the badge -(으)ㄴ from M24, the try-machine -아/어 보다 from M16, -아/어지다 from M35, the honorific -(으)시- from M4, and 께 from M27 pointing one report up a ladder.

**Gap deck:** the adverb that owns exactly one verb (벌떡 일어나다), the household ritual the whole country schedules its evenings around (분리수거), and the warm goodbye with no date in it (종종 놀러 오세요).

## What's next

Band 8 keeps unspooling the Grade-C list, and the two columns above are close to exhausted: after six modules of running the 하다/되다 machine, what remains on the C-list is mostly what no machine will assemble for you — more adverbs, the mimetics, the one-syllable dependent nouns. No new grammar is coming; the endings closed at M96, and from here the work is collocation. Read a Korean editorial tonight and watch the adverbs rather than the verbs — 갈수록, 불과, 지극히, 오로지 — because the verbs you can now build, and the adverbs you can only meet. 계속 읽으세요.
