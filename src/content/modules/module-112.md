# Korean, Word-First — Module 112: The Promises Come Due

M97 opened Band 8 by promising that the frozen riders of forty modules would thaw here, and M103 turned the thaw into a method: find the half you own, learn the half you don't. This module turns it into a LEDGER. Thirty-six Grade-C nouns, and nine of them were named out loud in an earlier note and then sent away to ride frozen — 효율적 in M99's 효과적, 소수 in M76's 다수, 축소 in M72's 확대하다, 금액 in M101's 액수, 임무 in M93's 수행하다, 조정 in M91's 구조, 염려 in M67's 염려하다, 불행 in M36's 다행, and 순수 in M45's 순수하다. Each of those nine notes opens with its receipt, quoted. Around them sit three drawers: the -적 shelf's third run, the office, and the longer view. No new grammar — every sentence recycles one Band 7 pattern and names it.

**How to use this:** read the receipts table first and go back to the note that wrote each IOU, because a word you were shown frozen and then taught is the cheapest vocabulary in the course. Then read the -적 shelf IN ORDER, because it ends in a trap: 면적 looks like the sixth product of the M45 factory and is nothing of the kind. 유적, in the last drawer, is the same trap wearing a different character. Three 적s today, one of them a suffix six times over and two of them not a suffix at all — and the only thing that tells you which is the word, never the syllable.

## Part 1 — The promises come due

Nine receipts. Left is today's word, middle is the note that wrote the IOU, right is the line it wrote.

|---|---|---|
| 효율적 | M99's 효과적 | "효과적 WORKS, 효율적 works cheaply" |
| 소수 | M76's 다수 | "Its pair rides frozen: 소수 (the minority, 少數)" |
| 축소 | M72's 확대하다 | "Its shrink-twin 축소하다 rides frozen for now." |
| 금액 | M101's 액수 | "Its twin is 금액 (金額 — riding frozen)" |

| 순수 | M45's 순수하다 | "순수한 마음 (M19's 마음), 순수한 사람 = guileless" |

Then the four drawers:

- **THE -적 SHELF III (보편적 이상적 부분적 실질적 효율적 면적):** the third run off the factory M45 built, and the md's rule still holds — "Sino noun in, quality-word out — and the product is still a noun", which is why these words need -적이다, -적인 or -적으로 before they can act. 보편적 is two new characters (普 widespread, 遍 all over) and sorts against M45's 일반적; 이상적 is the 理 of M34's 이해 on the 想 of M34's 예상; 부분적 is M32's 부분 and nothing else; 실질적 is the 實 of M34's 확실하다 on the 質 of M81's 질; 효율적 pays M99's 효과적. And then **면적**, which is the trap the five above it exist to set: its 적 is piled-積, the character of M43's 적금 and M45's 적극적, and 면적 is a plain noun meaning floor area. The proof sits in M92's 표면, which shows the real suffix on this very syllable — "표면적으로는 (-적 from M45 plus 으로 plus 는)".
- **THE PROMISES (순수 소수 축소 금액 임무 조정 염려 불행):** the table above, dismounting one by one. Note what each one was doing while it waited: 소수 was inside 다수결, 조정 inside 구조 조정, 임무 inside 임무를 수행하다, 금액 on a receipt beside M101's 액수 — Korean parked them in compounds, and the compounds taught you the words before the words arrived.
- **THE OFFICE IV (일체 전용 확정 가상 동의 반성 보상 국산 서민 연간 용도 확장 창작 취향 정보화):** fifteen words off the forms, the signage and the news page, and you own a half of nearly every one. 일체 is 一 plus the 切 of M81's 친절; 전용 is the 專 of M57's 전문 on the 用 of M43's 비용; 확정 is M64's 확인 plus the 定 of M33's 결정하다; 가상 is the 假 of M74's 가짜 on the 想 of M34's 예상; 동의 is the 同 of M38's 동시 on the 意 of M32's 의미; 반성 is the 反 of M64's 위반 plus a new 省; 보상 is the 補 of M90's 후보 plus a new 償; 국산 is the 國 of M32's 국민 on the 産 of M43's 부동산; 서민 is a new 庶 on the 民 of M50's 시민; 연간 is the 年 of M38's 매년 on the 間 of M38's 기간; 용도 is the 用 again on the 途 of M52's 도중; 확장 is the 擴 of M72's 확대하다 on the 張 of M42's 긴장; 창작 is the 創 of M101's 창조 on the 作 of M48's 작가; 취향 is a new 趣 on the 向 of M31's 방향; and 정보화 is M33's 정보 through the -화 factory M32's 변화 decoded.
- **THE LONGER VIEW (도덕 유적 일생 일자 연설 비명 마무리):** the page's words for a whole life. 도덕 is the way-道 of M38's 도로 under the virtue-德 that M103's 덕 let stand alone, and in Korea it is a school subject with a textbook. 유적 is the day's second trap. 일생 is one life counted once, against M46's 평생. 일자 is the date as a form field, and is NOT 일자리. 연설 is the 演 of M48's 공연 on the 說 of M100's 사설. 비명 is the 悲 of M96's 비극 on a new 鳴. And 마무리 is the one native word on the shelf, with no hanja at all.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 면적's 적 is piled-積 and 유적's 적 is footprint-跡 — neither is the suffix, and M100's 흔적 ran this radar for you first, "this 적 is 跡, a footprint, NOT the -적 stamp of M45" · 이상적 is 理想的; M32's 이상 is 以上, twenty and up — same two syllables, no shared character · 일체 read 일절 means not at all and wants a negative behind it; the gap deck takes the pair · 취향's 향 is facing-向, not the incense-香 of M103's 향 ("three 향s, and only this one burns") and not the home-鄕 of M27's 고향 · 일자 is a date on a form; 일자리 is 일 plus 자리, the job M43's 구하다 went looking for ("일자리를 구하다") · 비명's 명 only sounds — it is not M5's 명, the people-counter · 반성's 반 is turn-back-反 from M64's 위반, not M15's 반, the homeroom class — that note counted the syllable out already, "same syllable, three jobs" · 조정's 정 is 整, order, not the 定 of M33's 결정하다 · 소수 here is 少數, the minority; the decimal 소수 is 小數 and NIKL does not list it · and 확장 is not 확대: 확대 makes the same thing bigger, 확장 makes it reach further.

**Calibration, two ladders.** Worry: M29's 걱정 in the kitchen · 염려 on letterhead · M86's 우려 in the newspaper. Finishing: M16's 끝내다 ends it · M56's 마치다 declares it concluded · 마무리 is the last stretch itself, a noun you can hold.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 pattern and names it, and no two come from the same module — -(으)ㄴ/는 데다가 from M92 stacks a wide 면적 and a 전용 주차장 into one office; 에 따라 from M90 moves a 금액 by its 용도, which is 조정 doing its honest job; -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 grows the 임무 as the 사업 keeps 확장-ing; -더라고요 from M88 reports a friend's 취향 as 독특한 rather than wrong; -기 마련이다 from M87 rules that whoever 피해를 입다-s will want 보상; -(으)ㄴ 탓에 from M86 blames a 축소된 예산 for an 일자 that is still not 확정; 반면에 from M95 weighs a 국산 price against a 실질적 difference; and -(으)며 from M85 lets a 대통령 speak of 도덕 while 연설을 마무리하다, in print. Underneath sit the frames you have held since Ring 1: the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24 on 넓은, 높은, 입은 and 실질적인, the -아/어지다 machine from M35 closing 많아져요, the long negation from M28, 도 from M2 sitting where the case particle sat, the -기 nominalizer from M13 under 마련, and the 합니다체 of M4 on the one printed line.

**Gap deck:** the three-syllable shrug that ends an argument about taste (취향 존중), the sign that means the exact opposite of the word you just learned (일절 없습니다), and the page every Korean child has had to write (반성문).

## What's next

The ledger is settled: nine words that rode frozen through other people's notes now have cards of their own, and you have seen the whole trick from both ends — a compound teaches a word long before the word is taught. Keep the habit for the rest of the ring. When a note tells you something is riding frozen, that is not a footnote, it is a receipt. Band 8 carries on down the Grade-C list, and the traps stay the same shape: a syllable is not a character, and only the word tells you which one you are holding. 부분적으로는 다 아는 단어였죠; 이제 마무리합시다.
