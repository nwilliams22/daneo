## Part 3 — The glue: as the noun commands

### 1. Noun + 대로 — according to

Stick it straight on: 약속**대로** (M5 — as promised), 계획**대로** (M8 — as planned), 순서**대로** (M33 — in order, the note that wrote today's IOU). M39's 법 note already ran 법대로 하세요 — by the book — on credit; the machine behind it is now official. The noun states the standard; 대로 pledges conformity.

### 2. Verb badge + 대로 — as-it-happens, as-it-was

M24's badges plug in. Present -는: 도착하**는 대로** 연락하세요 = the moment you arrive. **Radar vs M38's -자마자:** -자마자 clocks the instant two events touched; -는 대로 makes a promise about the follow-through — which is why requests love it. Past -(으)ㄴ: 들**은 대로** 말했어요 = I said it as I heard it — testimony's favorite ending.

### 3. The fossils — 그대로 · 마음대로 · 제대로

Three 대로s fused solid and shipped in the vocab: 그대로 (as-is: 그대로 두세요), 마음대로 (as-you-please: blessing or threat, tone decides), 제대로 (properly — the 제 is one's-proper, not M17's humble my). Each one is [word] + 대로, frozen mid-pledge.

**Boulevard radar:** the -대로 in 강남대로 is 大路 — a GREAT road. M31's 종로 note stamped street names with road-路; this 대로 takes orders, that one takes traffic.
