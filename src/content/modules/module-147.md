# Korean, Word-First — Module 147: Shared Ground, Small Details and Change

A form asks about education. A host prepares an event. A walk reveals the first raindrops. This pack moves between the categories institutions use and the details a person actually notices.

**How to use this:** Learn the checklist before the sentences. Use the notes to separate a broad label from a precise meaning, then trace each sentence through its three layers.

---

## Part 1 — A familiar block changes its job

**The ending is doing work.** 철학 → 철학적, 기초 → 기초적, 보수 → 보수적. The -적 family from M45 turns a topic into a description. It still needs context: 보수적 can describe a political view or an attitude toward change.

**A result can be the subject.** 향상 is improvement; 향상되다 says the improvement happens. 실력이 향상되었어요 puts the skills in the subject slot. 판매되다 likewise puts the goods, rather than the seller, in front.

**Names on a form.** 학력 asks about educational background. 대상자 names a person included by a program's criteria. 한정하다 sets those criteria's boundary. These are useful public words, but they do not describe everything about a person.

**Zoom in.** 주름살 narrows 주름 to skin. 귓속 places you inside the ear. 빗방울 reduces rain to drops. The familiar parts help, but the whole compound still deserves its own meaning.

**Earlier vocabulary:** 하나님 → M69; 가톨릭 → M69; 사회자 → M69. These words return in the examples as review; the 33 entries below are this pack’s new checklist.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

No new glue. The sentence frames are familiar; the new work is choosing precise words for them.

**Spot the recycling:** M69's 참석 helps define eligible attendees; 재활용 supplies the topic of reference material; 입사 precedes improved skills. 유적지 also returns as the setting of an accidental meeting.

- **-더라도 from M95:** a restriction or uncertainty does not remove the next action.
- **-(으)ㄴ 결과 from M90:** show what changed after consulting information.
- **-다 보니 from M94:** let an observation arise during an ongoing activity.
- **-(으)ㄹ 듯하다 from M87:** present an expectation without announcing it as a fact.

::sentences::

공통점 from M144 is now easy to unpack: 공통 is what is shared, and the whole noun identifies a shared point. A shared point is not the same as saying two things are identical.

**Gap deck:** 동그라미를 치다 asks for a circle mark, not a blow. 온종일 gives the already complete day of 종일 extra emphasis.
