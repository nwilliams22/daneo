# Korean, Word-First — Module 91: Process and Structure

M90 gave the report its nouns for the state; this module gives it the nouns for how anything is BUILT and how anything is DONE. Thirty-four Grade-C words in three drawers: the SHAPE of a thing (구조 형태 형식 방식 구성 형성 기능 틀 토대), its CORE (핵심 초점 본질 근본 근본적 기본적 영역 부문 분야 작업), and the CARRYING-OUT (절차 실시 처리 접근 전개 통합 도입 설치하다). The organizing thesis is a pair: Korean names structure with 하다/되다 twins — 구성하다/구성되다, 형성하다/형성되다, 실시하다/실시되다 — the flip M70's 마련되다 note described ("되다 doesn't check pedigree, only the 하다 it replaces"), and the report writes the 되다 side. Four glue parts fold what the reader already knows into the sentence: **-(으)ㄴ/는 바** (as stated, that which — the IOU M87's 바 note wrote), **-다시피** (as you know, as you see), **-(으)ㄹ 겸** (partly to), and **-는 김에** (while you're at it).

**How to use this:** decode first — thirty of these words are two-hanja compounds and you own a half of nearly every one (形 式 方 成 機 能 節 次 心 點 本 質 根 領 域 部 門 分 野 作 業 實 施 處 理 接 近 展 開 統 合 入 設 置); the firsts are 造 土 核 焦 導, and 構 is the one M90's 기구 note filed. Then read the three 되다-verbs as one lesson and the four glue parts as one idea: a fact the listener already has, hung on the front of a sentence. The sentence deck is a report with a presenter standing in front of it.

## Part 1 — Three drawers, one thesis

- **SHAPE (구조 형태 형식 방식 구성 구성하다 구성되다 형성 형성하다 형성되다 기능 틀 토대):** 구조 is how the parts hold together, 형태 the shape they show, 형식 the shape they are required to show, 방식 the style a thing is habitually done in (M32's 방법 is the how that works; 방식 is the manner). 구성 is what a thing is made of and 형성 the process of its taking shape — each with its 하다 (someone builds) and 되다 (it stands built). 기능 is what a machine can do (機能) or a hand has learned (技能) — NIKL lists both and the note covers both. 틀 is the native mould; 토대 the platform the whole thing stands on.
- **CORE (핵심 초점 본질 근본 근본적 기본적 영역 부문 분야 작업):** the kernel (핵심), the burning-point a lens makes (초점), what a thing is underneath (본질) and where it comes from (근본); two -적 words from the factory of M45 — 기본적 the floor, 근본적 the footing; three fields — 분야 is what you work in, 부문 a division with a gate on it, 영역 a territory with a border; and 작업, one piece of the work that M2's 일하다 does in general.
- **CARRYING-OUT (절차 실시 실시하다 실시되다 처리 접근 접근하다 전개 통합 도입 설치하다):** the required order (절차), the ministry's verb of implementation (실시, with its 하다 and 되다), dealing with a thing in its place (처리), coming at it (접근, on 에), the unrolling of a plot (전개), the merger that keeps its parts (통합), the leading-in of a system (도입), and the bolting-to-the-wall of a thing (설치하다).

::vocab::

**The pair rule:** every 하다 on this shelf has a 되다, and the two are one word seen from two ends — 위원회를 구성했어요 names the builder, 위원회가 구성됐어요 does not (위원회 — the committee, riding frozen). M70's 마련되다 opened the door ("되다 doesn't check pedigree, only the 하다 it replaces"); today the door is a hallway: 구성·형성·실시 each walk through it, and the state form 되어 있다 that M70's 포함되다 note preferred (a composition is a standing condition) closes the report's sentence. Read a 되다 and ask who was dropped; the answer is usually the state.

**Radar patrol:** 구조 is 構造 (structure) — the 구조대 that pulls you from a river is 救助, riding frozen · 기능 is 機能 on a phone and 技能 in a welder's hands, and the ledger counts both · 형태, 형식, 형성 all wear 形 (shape), and none of them is M4's 형, the older brother · 구성's 성 is the 成 of M35's 성공하다, not M23's 성, the castle · 틀 the frame, M15's 틀리다 the wrong answer, M51's 틀다 the tap — three native 틀s, sort by sound · 작업 is one job of work; the 일 of M2's 일하다 is work itself · and 초점 burns (焦) where M25's 초 melts.

## Part 2 — The glue: -(으)ㄴ/는 바 — the formal connective

M87's 바 note ended on a promissory line — "Today the NOUN only — the connective -(으)ㄴ/는 바 that opens a formal clause belongs to Module 91" — and this is the module. The noun was 아는 바가 없습니다 = I have no knowledge of it; the connective hangs a clause on 바 and then a particle on the 바, so that a known fact becomes the frame of a new sentence. Three frames own the register: **앞서 말씀드린 바와 같이** (M85's 앞서, M71's 말씀드리다, M9's 같이 on 와) = as I mentioned earlier — the speech's hinge; **조사한 바에 따르면** (M56's 조사하다, 에 따르면 from M90) = according to what was found — the report's source line, with a clause where M90 hung a noun; and **-(으)ㄴ 바 있다** — 발표한 바 있다 (M16's 발표하다) = has previously announced — the news's perfect, a fact on file. The badge is the M24 choice: -(으)ㄴ for a finished clause, -는 for a standing one (생각하는 바 = what one thinks, M10's 생각하다). RADAR: -는 것 from M17 is the same clause-noun with its tie off — 조사한 것에 따르면 is the same sentence at the kitchen table, and nobody says 바 there. **Calibration:** 아까 말했잖아요 (I told you, and you were there — -잖아요 from M42) · 아까 말한 것처럼 (as I said — M17's 것 with 처럼 from M41) · 앞서 말씀드린 바와 같이 (as stated above — the podium). Same fact, three distances from the listener.

## Part 3 — The glue: -다시피 — as you know, as you see

Verb stem + 다시피, no vowel/consonant fuss: the clause names something the listener can already see or already knows, and the sentence proceeds from it. Two words do most of the work — **알다시피** (M4's 알다) = as you know, and **보시다시피** (M1's 보다 with the honorific -(으)시- from M4) = as you can see — the presenter's two openers, one for the fact and one for the slide. Then a second job, the near-miss: verb + 다시피 하다 = to all but do it — 굶다시피 했어요 (굶다 — to starve, riding frozen) = practically starved, 회사에서 살다시피 했어요 (M4's 살다, M2's 회사) = practically lived at the office. Sort it against -잖아요 from M42, whose md heading was "-잖아요 — you know this already": -잖아요 nudges ONE person who shares the memory; -다시피 addresses a ROOM that shares the evidence. **Calibration:** 아시죠? (you know, right? — -죠 from M51) · 아시잖아요 (you know this already — a nudge) · 아시다시피 (as you know — the podium) · 아는 바와 같이 (as is known — the document). Four ways to say the listener already has it, from a wink to a footnote.

## Part 4 — The glue: -(으)ㄹ 겸 — partly to, killing two birds

겸 (兼, both-at-once — a first; the 겸 of 겸손, humble, is a different word and rides frozen) takes the future badge -(으)ㄹ from the 때-machine of M25 and names a SECOND purpose for the act in the main clause: **운동도 할 겸 회사까지 걸어갔어요** (M8's 운동하다 dismounted, M2's 도, M28's 걸어가다) = I walked to the office, partly for the exercise. The 도 is nearly always there — the second purpose is an ALSO — and two purposes can stack: 친구도 만날 겸 영화도 볼 겸 (M1's 친구, M2's 영화, M2's 만나다, M12's 서울) 서울에 갔어요. Nouns take 겸 bare: 아침 겸 점심 (M2's 아침, M3's 점심) = brunch, 거실 겸 침실 (M13's 거실; 침실 — the bedroom, riding frozen) = a living room that is also the bedroom. **Calibration:** 걸으면서 운동했어요 (-(으)면서 from M38 — two things at the SAME TIME) · 운동하려고 걸었어요 (-(으)려고 from M50 — ONE purpose) · 운동도 할 겸 걸었어요 (TWO purposes, one act). 겸사겸사 in the gap deck is the same character doubled into an adverb.

## Part 5 — The glue: -는 김에 — while you're at it

김 — the steam, the momentum — is a dependent noun here (riding frozen), and RADAR before anything else: not M18's 김, the seaweed, not the 김 of M3's 김밥, not the surname. Verb + the present badge -는 from M24 + 김에 rides an occasion that ALREADY exists: **가는 김에 우유도 사 오세요** (M1's 가다, M2's 우유, M2's 사다 with M1's 오다 welded on) = since you're going anyway, get milk too; **말이 나온 김에** (M4's 말, M28's 나오다 in the past badge) = now that it's come up — the gap deck has it. The past badge -(으)ㄴ for an occasion already begun (온 김에 = while you're here), the present -는 for one under way. The 도 rides along here too, because the second act is an extra. Sort it from its two neighbours: -(으)면서 from M38 is two acts at one time; -(으)ㄹ 겸 is one act with two purposes; -는 김에 is a second act hitched to an occasion that was going to happen anyway. **Calibration:** 서울에 가면서 친구를 만났어요 (met a friend on the way — -(으)면서) · 친구도 만날 겸 서울에 갔어요 (went partly to meet a friend — 겸) · 서울에 가는 김에 친구를 만났어요 (was going anyway, so met a friend — 김에). Three trips, three reasons the friend got seen.

## Part 6 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** M85's 앞서 and M71's 말씀드리다 open the 바 frame that M87 promised; 에 따르면 from M90 takes a clause instead of a noun; the honorific -(으)시- from M4 sits inside 보시다시피; the double-subject frame M24 first showed (한식은 반찬이 많아요) carries both 기능이 많아요 and 시간이 걸려요; M12's 걸리다 keeps its take-time job; the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24 carries 말씀드린, 조사한, and 사는; the 되어 있다 state that M70's 포함되다 note preferred closes the report; M35's 바뀌다 changes a lifestyle on its own; and M37's 맞추다 tunes a lens.
