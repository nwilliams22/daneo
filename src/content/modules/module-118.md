# Korean, Word-First — Module 118: Collection Day

Earlier notes kept naming sets and leaving their members for later. Today those receipts come due: M58 already taught all three forces; M89 contrasted size classes, M76 and M99 pointed at routes, and M108 named the money form of punishment. The thirty-one words here complete those shelves while the office nouns give them a place to be used. No new grammar enters; each sentence borrows one Band 7 pattern.

**How to use this:** begin with the receipts below. Say the older word, then the new one; a set member is easier to keep when you know which empty place it fills. The -인 people and the office words come next. Read the radar before treating an identical sound as an identical meaning.

## Part 1 — Collection day

**The receipts.** These are promises the course made in earlier notes:

**The three forces return as earlier vocabulary:** 육군, 해군 and 공군 were taught with 군 in M58. Their land, sea and air roots now support the new report and office nouns; they do not add three new checklist entries here.

**The people on the -인 rail** divide by setting. M58’s 상인 sells and 신인 marks a debut; today 죄인 carries guilt, 본인 identifies the person on a form, 현대인 stands for people today, and 국왕 is a ruler in an official report. 인사 is a trap at the start of the row: the greeting inside 인사하다 is already yours, but 人事 personnel and 人士 notable person are separate dictionary words.

**The receipts at the desk** complete earlier collocations. M69's 빌다 waited for 소원, M78's 부족 for 수면, M72's 발생 for 화재, M23's 안내소 for 안내, M51's 참가하다 for 참가, M103's 정면 for 충돌, M72's 연결하다 for 연결. Learn them as pairs: 소원을 빌다, 수면 부족, 화재 발생, 참가 신청, 정면 충돌, 연결이 끊기다.

**The office drawer** has the paper and the people around it. 한문 is classical Chinese writing; 간격 leaves a space; 통로 is a passage; 봉사 fills logged hours; 부서 is a company department; 악몽 can describe a terrible day; 열정 fills a résumé; 제공, 해소 and 혜택 belong on official notices; 연관, 교체, 목록 and 참조 belong in reports and apps. Their notes give the root and a phrase each.

::vocab::

**Listen for the register.** A bus rider asks which 노선 to take; a map labels the numbered 호선. An office notice announces 인사 이동 and 선수 교체; a friend simply says someone moved or changed.

**Read the sets both ways.** Start with the old note and predict its missing word; then start with the new word and name the earlier promise. This checks whether the connection is useful rather than merely familiar.

**Radar patrol:** 인사 the greeting ≠ 人事 HR ≠ 人士 notable person · 왕 in a story ≠ 국왕 in a bulletin · 해소 disperses pressure, 해결 solves a problem · 연관 and 관련 nearly overlap, but related articles usually say 관련 기사 · 교체 is the announced changeover, 바꾸다 the action · 참가 enters an event, 참석 attends, 참여 takes part · 노선 is any route, 호선 the numbered subway line.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** 에 따르면 from M90 reports the small plane; -는 바람에 from M86 makes a changed route cause lateness; -(으)ㄴ/는 셈이다 from M87 reckons a member's benefit; -더라고요 from M88 records the merchant's passion; -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 compares narrow intervals and collisions; -(으)며 from M85 joins the two forces' actions; -는 김에 from M91 turns a list into a reference-reading occasion; -더라도 from M95 keeps the benefit stable despite a department change.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** 봉사 활동 is volunteer work when a school counts hours, and 인사 이동 is a personnel reshuffle, not a greeting on the move.
