# Korean, Word-First — Module 75: Mount and Dismount

M72 taught the lab's -하다/-되다 process verbs; M56 built the news engine; this module is the everyday hinge — hospitals, schools, subways, kitchens, translation apps — run in both directions. Thirteen nouns you already own get their verb (행동 → 행동하다, 치료 → 치료하다, 입학 → 입학하다), three verbs you own get their bare noun (취소하다 → 취소, 귀국하다 → 귀국, 복사하다 → 복사), and fifteen fresh pairs arrive with one half riding free. The organizing thesis: **any 하다-noun is a verb waiting, and any 하다-verb is a noun** — and the one radar that governs it: 하다 mounts ACTION nouns; state nouns stay on 이다/있다. No new grammar; a lot of new mileage.

**How to use this:** read the receipts before you drill — over half this shelf quotes a note you have already learned, and the X-rays do the memorizing. Spend your reps on the pairs the hinge splits: 수입하다 (ship-in only) vs the income 수입, 방문 the visit vs 방문 the door, 감상 the hobby vs 감상 the impressions, and 반하다 counter-to vs 반하다 fall-for. Band 6 words earn their rank by refusing to be interchangeable.

## Part 1 — The hinge runs both ways

This shelf is mostly receipts, paid in both directions:

- **Mount — the promises cash:** M53's 치료 note ruled 'The verb 치료하다 belongs to the one in the white coat'; M53's 입원 ran '입원하다 = to be admitted'; M51's 우승 said '우승하다 = to take the title'; M52's 외출 ended '외출하다 to verb it'; M47's 이혼 filed '이혼하다 = to divorce'; M43's 수출 already sent 'the semiconductors, cars, and dramas abroad'. Thirteen nouns, each with its verb pre-announced — today the verbs get seats.
- **Dismount — the M72 move, repeated:** M72 dismounted 이용, 해결, and 발견 from the M56 verbs, and its 발달 note named the precedent — 'dismounting the way M57's 결정 did from 결정하다'. 취소, 귀국, and 복사 stand bare the same way, and stack the way print stacks: 예약 취소, 귀국 날짜, 복사 한 장.
- **Fresh pairs, decoded from receipts:** 반복 is 반대's flip-反 (M24) + 복습's again-復 (M44); 보관 is 보험's keep-保 (M43) + 관리's pipe-管 (M57); 무시 is 무료's nothing-無 (M20) + the see-視 that 시청 (M31) surfaced; 절약 pays the IOU M72's 에너지 note wrote ('에너지 절약 (절약 — saving, riding frozen)').
- **Two firsts:** the ask-問 inside M10's 질문 (decoded for 방문) and the write-寫 inside M17's 사진 (decoded for 복사) — both notes had left their characters unread.
- **The one refusal:** 반하다 is the shelf's only 하다 that will not dismount — there is no bare 반 to stand alone — which is why it closes the list, and why the gap deck files both of its senses.

## Part 3 — Two directions, no new grammar

### 1. Mount — noun + 하다, and the particle it brings

Every action noun you own is one 하다 from a verb — the move M44 made with 합격하다 and M56 with 조사하다, now run thirteen times. What changes is the particle: enter-verbs take 에 (**대학교에 입학하다**, **병원에 입원하다** — you go INTO the institution), the tournament takes 에서 (**대회에서 우승하다** — you win IN it), and the rest take a plain object (**환자를 치료하다**, **커피를 수입하다**, **자리를 양보하다**). The noun stays in the sentence's other seats: 치료를 받다, 계획을 세우다, 복습 시간.

### 2. Dismount — verb → bare noun, the way print writes

Strip the 하다 and the noun stacks with no particle at all: **취소 문자**, **귀국 후에**, **복사 한 장**, **냉장 보관**, **반복 연습**, **해외 진출**. Headlines and signs run on this: nouns pressed together, verbs implied. The 되다-row (M36) waits at the same door for each of them — 취소되다, 번역되다, 표시되다 — the swap M72 taught as a pair.

**Calibration:** 하다 mounts ACTIONS. State nouns ride 이다 or 있다 instead: 관심이 있어요 — M24's 관심 was 'Measured on the 있다-chassis' — 시간이 있어요, 문제가 있어요 — never 관심하다. Some nouns do both because they are both: 계획이 있어요 (a plan exists) / 계획하다 (the planning happens). When in doubt, ask whether someone is DOING it; if nobody is, leave the 하다 off.
