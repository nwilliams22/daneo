# Korean, Word-First — Module 104: Native Verbs III, and the House

M94 opened the native verb shelf with motion and mishap; M102 welded old verbs into new ones. This is the third shelf, and its thesis is the one the parties have been building toward since Band 4: **the native verb shelf is where the passive party of M36 shows its seams.** You have been meeting party products for seventy modules — 걸리다, 막히다, 쫓기다, 깔리다, 박히다 — always from the far end, the end where the thing happens and nobody is named. Today the near ends arrive. 묻히다 is 묻다 buried; 말리다 is M36's 마르다 pushed through the causative party of M37; 굳어지다 is today's 굳다 on the -지다 wing from M35; and 깔다 is the active M102's 깔리다 was waiting for — that note said so outright: "깔다 itself stays frozen". Thirty-six Grade-C words in four drawers, no new grammar, and nine of them are the nouns of an old Korean house.

**How to use this:** learn the seam verbs as PAIRS, not as entries — read the table in Part 1 before you touch the vocab, and every one of the five lines is a sentence you can already say from one end. Then decode: 되찾다 is 되- on M6's 찾다, 가라앉다 is a frozen 가라 on M9's 앉다, 감싸다 is M41's 감다 plus the wrap-싸다 the M94 md sorted out for you, 물결 is M1's 물 plus a grain, 밥상 is M1's 밥 plus M40's 상. The seven Sino verbs each stand on a root you have already decoded — 加 from M51's 참가하다, 到 from M89's 도저히, 達 from M56's 발달하다, 保 from M43's 보험, 接 from M43's 면접, 外 from M55's 외, 面 from the same 면접 — and five of them are verbs an earlier note explicitly left riding frozen. Read the Radar patrol slowly: this shelf has more homographs on it than any since M99.

## Part 1 — Native verbs III, and the house

Four drawers, and one machine running under the first two.

- **THE HAND AND THE GROUND (꺾다 뚫다 파다 깔다 감싸다 품다 쫓다 묻히다 말리다 되찾다 헤아리다 다짐하다):** what a hand does to the world — snap it off, bore through it, dig down into it, spread it flat, wrap it, hold it to the chest, chase it, bury it, dry it, get it back, count it, swear to it. Three of the twelve are party products (묻히다 passive, 말리다 causative, and 깔다's partner 깔리다 already shipped in M102), and two are welds on verbs you own (되찾다 on M6's 찾다, 감싸다 on M41's 감다).
- **THE FALL AND THE RISE (가라앉다 솟다 굳다 굳어지다 굶다 망하다 접하다 외면하다):** what happens to a thing, or to a life. Down: 가라앉다 sinks, 굶다 goes without, 망하다 vanishes as a business, 외면하다 turns the face away. Up and out: 솟다 shoots from below, 접하다 makes contact. And in the middle, the pair that is the module's own seam — 굳다 (hard) and 굳어지다 (turning hard), the -아/어지다 wing from M35 run on the shelf's own adjective.
- **THE SINO VERBS (가하다 도달하다 보장하다 예상하다 제작하다 기초하다 개선하다):** seven verbs that print uses and the mouth mostly does not, and five of them cash a receipt. M86's 압력 ran "압력을 가하다 (가하다 — to apply, riding frozen)"; M93's 예상되다 filed its twin as "riding free on M34's 예상"; M96's 제작 ruled that "The verb 제작하다 rides frozen; print uses the noun"; M93's 개선 left the pair where "개선하다 / 개선되다 ride free". Today they all dismount.
- **THE HOUSE (품 침 마디 엉덩이 저고리 무덤 물결 밥상 천장):** the body, the clothes and the rooms of an older Korea — the bosom you are held in, the spit, the joint, the seat, the hanbok's jacket, the grave, the water's grain, the meal table, and the ceiling over all of it. Four of the nine pay a note that carried them frozen: M88's 한마디 on 마디, M82's 원숭이 on 엉덩이, M39's 핑계 on 무덤, M40's 상 on 밥상.

**Receipts come due.** Ten earlier notes wrote an IOU on this shelf, and Band 8 is where they clear:

- M83's 신문지 spread the newsprint with 깔다 riding frozen, and M102's 깔리다 shipped the far end two modules ago — **깔다** lands, and the pair now closes from both directions.
- M73's 굳이 decoded itself on a verb it did not own — **굳다** is that verb, and **굳어지다** is it caught in the act of changing.
- The M91 md practically starved (굶다시피 했어요) and the M95 md would sooner starve than lie (굶을망정) — **굶다** arrives paid twice.
- M86's 압력 applied its pressure with 가하다 frozen, M96's 제작 kept its verb off the shelf, and M93 left both 예상하다 and 개선하다 riding free — **가하다**, **제작하다**, **예상하다** and **개선하다** all dismount today.
- M88's 한마디 counted in **마디**, M82's 원숭이 rhymed on **엉덩이**, M39's 핑계 hid its excuse in a **무덤**, and M40's 상 had already laid the **밥상**.

**The seams, five lines.** Each row is one verb from both ends; say the left, then the right, and the party stops being forty separate words.

| Active — somebody does it | Happen-form — it is done | The machine |
|---|---|---|
| 묻다 (bury — frozen) | **묻히다** — is buried, gets dirt on it | the passive party of M36, 히 |
| 마르다 (M36 — dries by itself) | **말리다** — dries it, hangs it out | the causative party of M37, 리 |
| **굳다** — is hard | **굳어지다** — is turning hard | the -아/어지다 wing from M35 |
| **쫓다** — chases, drives off | 쫓기다 (the M36 md's table: "쫓다 → 쫓기다") | the passive party of M36, 기 |
| **깔다** — spreads it on the floor | 깔리다 (M102) — lies spread, is run over | the passive party of M36, 리 |

**Radar patrol:** 묻히다 is two NIKL rows — 땅에 묻히다 buries a body, 옷에 흙을 묻히다 gets dirt on a coat — and the 묻다 under the first is the homograph M28's 묻다 (ask) logged in its own note · 말리다 is two rows as well, 빨래를 말리다 and 싸움을 말리다, and neither is M4's 말 · 파다 digs; M40's 파 is the scallion, M49's 파도 the wave — that note already ruled the two apart · the 싸다 inside 감싸다 wraps, where M7's 싸다 is cheap, the split the M94 md made for 둘러싸다 · 품 is native and stands alone (the bosom); the 품 of M23's 기념품 and M101's 품질 is Sino 品, goods — that note's "품 (goods" is the radar · 침 is spit and an acupuncture needle; the 침 of M6's 침대 is sleep-寢, and M2's 아침 has neither in it · 천장 is 天障, heaven's barrier, not M9's 천 (thousand) plus M30's 장 — and the 障 is the one in today's 보장하다 · 굳다 is the hardening, M60's 딱딱하다 the complaint about the result · 굶다 is having no food, M3's 배고프다 the feeling about it · and 저고리 is one word: not M1's 저 with a ring on the end.

**Calibration, two ladders.** The table: M40's 상 is any low table · 밥상 is the one with dinner on it · 밥상을 차리다 is the whole meal, laid. The water: M49's 파도 crashes and you ride it · 물결 ripples and you watch it · and when it is people, 사람들의 물결 is a crowd, not a sea.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 pattern, named in its note — -(으)ㄴ 채 from M94 freezes the baby at the 품 while the 밥상 gets laid; -더니 from M88 watches a 표정 harden the moment the 소식 is 접하다-ed; -(으)ㄹ 정도로 from M89 measures a 물결 by the 천장 it reaches; -는 바람에 from M86 makes the shop's 망하다 the wind that leaves a family 굶다-ing; -(으)ㄹ까 봐 from M93 dries the 빨래 indoors for fear of rain; -는 김에 from M91 hitches 깔다 to a tidy-up; -(으)며 from M85 lets a company 보장하다 and 제작하다 in one printed breath; and -기 마련이다 from M87 says old stories are bound to be 묻히다-ed. Underneath, the frames you have held since Ring 1: the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24 on 안은, 오래된, 망하는 and 치우는, the -아/어지다 machine from M35 closing 굳어졌어요, the 합니다체 from M4 on the one printed sentence, and M2's 도 hiding an object particle on 이불도.

## What's next

The grammar is finished; the words are the work. Band 8 goes on down the Grade-C list — the office and its paper, the adverbs the page runs on, and the last of the abstract shelf — and every module from here is decode, radar, receipt. You can now say both ends of a Korean event: 아이가 이불을 깔았어요 and 이불이 깔려 있어요, 누가 묻었어요 and 역사에 묻혔어요. Same event, two grammars, one shelf. 계속 읽으세요.
