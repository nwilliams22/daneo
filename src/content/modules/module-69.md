# Korean, Word-First — Module 69: Gather and Believe

Band 6 climbs the belief shelf and walks the gathering circuit. Ring 1 already opened the doors — M26's 절 taught the temple (and the bow), M27's 교회 glowed under its red cross, M25 set the holiday table. This module stocks what's inside: the religions as words (불교, 유교, 종교), the people who keep them (부처님, 스님, 목사님), the things done there (기도, 빌다) — then follows Korea OUT of the buildings and onto the circuit its social life actually runs: 모임, 동아리, 동창회, 행사, the 환갑 잔치, and the thank-you (덕분) that keeps the whole machine turning. The words are descriptive, not devotional — this is the vocabulary Korea uses about itself.

**How to use this:** the shelf is hanja arithmetic and you own most of the parts — read the decodes before you drill. Then spend your reps where the money is: the 빌다/빌리다 radar pair (one syllable between praying and borrowing) and the two 환s (환갑 returns, 환영 rejoices).

---

## Part 1 — The teach-교 factory

- **The 敎-reunion:** 교회 and 교수 (M27) and 교육 (M32) banked the teach-敎. Now the religions arrive AS teachings: 불교 (the Buddha's), 유교 (the scholars'), and 종교 — religion itself, lineage-teaching. Radar: M32's 교육 note flagged it first — 학교's 교 is the school-building 校, a different character in the same disguise.
- **The 行事-economy:** 행사 = 행동's go-行 (M57) + 사건/사업's affair-事 (M36/M43): an affair set going.
- **The 회-annex:** the gathering-회 that M27's 교회 note decoded builds this module's after-parties in the notes — 동창회, 환영회.
- **The 님-clause:** 부처님, 목사님, and 스님 (where the -님 is factory-installed) all ride M16's 님-ladder. On this shelf, reverence is grammar.
- **The -ㅁ receipt:** 모임 is M25's 모이다 through the factory M33 named (웃음, 걸음) — and the payoff of a promise M25's note made outright.

## Part 2 — The bricks

Almost every noun here is either a decode or a debt collected — 모임 was promised by M25's 모이다, 환갑 by M30's 예순, 어린이날 by M35's 어린이, and 축하 completes M19's 축하하다 the way M44 completed its verbs. Read each note before drilling; the shelf remembers where you've been.

::vocab::

**Radar patrol:** 빌다 wishes and begs, 빌리다 borrows — the extra 리 is the entire difference. 신 (神) is a god; 신이 나다 is native fun; 신다 (M21) and 신발 (M9) are just shoes. 부처 is the Buddha until the news says 정부 부처 (ministries). 미팅 is a group blind date, not a 회의 (M10). And 초청장's -장 is document-狀, not M30's counter — though the card it names is still 한 장.

## Part 3 — The two circuits

### 1. The belief circuit

Korea's religious landscape in nouns: the 절 (M26) with its 탑 and 스님, the 교회 (M27) with its 목사님, and beneath both the oldest layer — 조상, the ancestors, greeted at every 명절 (M25) through M46's 차례. 유교 hums under everything like an operating system: 웃어른 first, 존댓말 (M19) always. The circuit's shared verb is 빌다 — palms together, wish upward — whatever the address.

### 2. The gathering circuit

The social calendar, word by word: the recurring 모임 (M25's 모이다, nouned at last), the campus 동아리, the 동창회 that finds you decades later, the official 행사 with banners, and the life-stage peaks — the 환갑 잔치, 어린이날, the 결혼식 you write a 축하 인사말 for. The circuit's currency is 덕분: whatever went well, someone else's virtue takes the credit.

**Calibration:** register runs this shelf. 초대 (M19) invites friends; 초청 invites dignitaries. 파티 (M19) is one night; a 모임 recurs; a 행사 has a budget. And 덕분에 only ever credits — blame still belongs to M29's 때문에.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** M47's 께서 honors the wishing grandmother while M25's -(으)ㄹ 때 and M31's 마다 schedule her, M46's -(으)ㄴ 지 counts the years since the 동창 scattered and M48's -기로 하다 commits to the reunion, M32's 위해서 and M40's -아/어 놓다 book the 환갑 feast, M44's 위한 badge aims the welcome event, M43's (이)나 counts the borrowed books, and M40's -아/어 두다 plus M52's -아/어 버리다 prepare — and then lose — the wedding toast.

::sentences::

**Gap deck:** the reply that gives the credit away (덕분에요) and the day the lanterns go up (부처님 오신 날).
