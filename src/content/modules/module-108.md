# Korean, Word-First — Module 108: The Workshop

Thirty-six words for a room where things get measured, checked, limited and paid for — and almost none of them ask you to learn a new character. **The workshop's tools are counters made of hanja you already own.** 機器 is the machine-機 of M33's 기회 on the 器 of M43's 계산기; 統計 is the unite-統 M31's 통일 isolated on the same 計; 觀察 is M72's 관찰하다 with the verb taken off; 想像力 is M67's 상상 with the strength-力 of M39's 노력 bolted on. Around them sit the checks (예방 완성 증명하다 지적되다 해석하다 신고하다 철저히 연속 유리하다), the limits (보장 방지 한정되다 제한하다 분리 상당 대가 반발 욕 벌 감옥) and the stance a person takes toward them (의지하다 존중하다 책임지다 귀신 유물 판). Twelve of today's words were already riding frozen inside an older note, and each entry quotes the line that promised it. No new grammar: every sentence recycles one Band 7 ending and names it.

**How to use this:** read the two one-syllable words BY THE ROW — 판 has four NIKL rows and 벌 has three, and the table below is the M99 lesson repeated, not a new one. Then spend your reps on the pairs this shelf sorts, because they are the whole difficulty: 기계 vs 기기, 예방 vs 방지, 제한하다 vs 한정되다, 존중하다 vs 존경하다, 의지하다 vs 의지, 정비 vs 정리, 목소리 vs 음성. Each pair is two words the dictionary translates the same way and Korean never confuses.

## Part 1 — The workshop, four drawers

**The instruments (10).** 기기 the appliance, 나침반 the compass, 통계 the number, 관찰 the looking, 상상력 the faculty, 두뇌 the intellect, 물리학 the subject, 음성 the signal, 장식 the trim, 정비 the servicing. Eight of the ten are two owned halves and nothing else; 나침반 adds a net-羅 and 장식 adds an adorn-飾, and those are the only genuinely new characters in the drawer.

**The checks (9).** What you do to a thing before you trust it: 예방 (block it early), 완성 (finish it), 증명하다 (prove it), 지적되다 (get flagged), 해석하다 (construe it), 신고하다 (report it), 철저히 (do it to the bottom), 연속 (do it again with no gap), 유리하다 (come out ahead). Four of them cash IOUs written two to thirty modules ago.

**The limits (11).** 보장 방지 한정되다 제한하다 분리 상당 대가 반발 욕 벌 감옥 — the fences, the pushback, and what happens when you go over. 예방 and 방지 share the defend-防 and split the work; 제한하다 and 한정되다 share the limit-限 and split it again.

**The stance (6).** 의지하다 존중하다 책임지다 귀신 유물 판 — leaning, respecting, bearing, and the three nouns of what a culture keeps.

**The two syllables with rows.** Read them the way a dictionary does:

| syllable | row 1 | row 2 | row 3 | row 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 벌 | 罰 punishment (벌을 받다) | the counter for suits (양복 한 벌) | 곤충 a bee (꿀벌) | — |
| 판 | 板 a board (칠판, 간판, 자판) | a round, counted (한 판 더) | 版 an edition (초판) | a scene (판이 벌어지다) |

Both tables are NIKL's, not ours — and the words they are NOT are the other half of the lesson: M16's 벌다 earns money and is a verb, while the 盤 of today's 나침반 and M46's 초반 is a round dial where 판 is a flat plank.

**The IOUs, paid.** Twelve of today's entries were already in the corpus, frozen inside somebody else's note: 관찰 ("The noun 관찰 rides free" — M72), 상상력 ("상상력 straps on the 력 M44's 능력 filed (M14's 힘 in a suit) and becomes imagination-as-capacity, the job-posting word" — M67), 정비 ("하천 정비 (정비 — maintenance, riding frozen)" — M92), 해석하다 ("해석하다 rides free" — M75), 신고하다 ("신고하다 rides free" — M75), 철저히 ("철저하게 (the -게 adverbializer from M33) or 철저히 (the -히 adverb, riding frozen) = thoroughly" — M89), 유리하다 ("유리하게/불리하게 작용하다 (유리 and 불리 — favourable and unfavourable, riding frozen; -게 from M33)" — M86), 보장 ("The noun 보장 rides frozen: 사회 보장 = social security" — M104), 분리 ("Korea's real civics exam is 분리수거 — separated recycling, frozen here as a concept" — M54), 대가 ("대가를 치르다 (대가 — the cost of a thing, riding frozen) = pay for it, in the sense the drama means" — M94), 존중하다 ("인격을 존중하다 (존중 — respect, riding frozen) = to respect someone's dignity" — M101), and 욕, which M75's 비판하다 set aside in one clause — "비판 (argued) is not 욕 (abuse — riding frozen, glossed)". Read each entry for its receipt before its meaning.

**Radar patrol:** 유리하다 is 有利, the edge; M36's 유리 is 琉璃, glass — no shared hanja, and 유리하다 never means glassy · 의지하다 is 依支, leaning; M39's 의지 is 意志, the will — and the 지하 inside the spelling is not M12's 지하 · 대가 is 代價 on the list, the price paid, and 大家 on the page, a master of an art · 벌 is 罰, a suit, and a bee — and M16's 벌다 is none of them · 판 is 板, a round, 版 and a scene, four rows in one syllable · 예방 blocks the disease, 방지 blocks the repeat — 예방 주사 but 사고 방지 · 귀신's 귀 is 鬼, a ghost, not M14's 귀 (the ear) · 나침반 is 羅針盤, and not M1's 나 plus M104's 침 plus M5's 반 · 기기 is 機器 with a plug, M72's 기계 is 機械 with a motor, and M83's 국기 already ruled on the file — "four hanja, one syllable, and the compound tells you which" · 물리학's 물 is 物, the thing-物 of M35's 식물, not M1's 물 (water) · 한정되다's 한 is 限, a ceiling, not the 韓 of the 한정식 M81's 정식 left frozen · 장식's 식 is 飾, not M55's 식 ("式 the way-hanja") and not the eat-식 · 연속's 속 is 續, not M28's 속 (the inside) and not the speed-速 of M57's 속도 · 감옥's 감 is 監, the overseeing of M96's 감독, not M80's 감 (the persimmon) · 반발 is 反 plus 撥 — not M5's 반 and not M14's 발.

**Calibration, four pairs.** Respect: M26's 존경하다 looks up at a person, 존중하다 holds level with an opinion. Order: M54's 정리 tidies what is there, 정비 repairs what has worn out. Limit: M64's 제한 caps how much, 한정 confines to whom. Voice: M19's 목소리 is whose voice it is, 음성 is the signal a machine reads.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence rides one Band 7 ending, named in its note — 에 따르면 from M90 opens a report with 통계 and lets 상당 stamp M32's 부분; -(으)며 from M85 lets one 공장 service 기계 and finish 기기 in one printed breath; -기 마련이다 from M87 makes 반발 the necessary answer to 제한하다; -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 ties 철저히 관찰 to M34's 분명하다; -았/었더니 from M88 files a 신고 and finds a man in 감옥; -(으)ㄴ/는 데다가 from M92 stacks 욕 on M19's 거짓말 until a 벌 lands; 반면에 from M95 weighs 유리하다 against 한정되다; and -는 김에 from M91 gets 분리 done while the 쓰레기 is already going out. Underneath, the frames you have held since Ring 1: the badge -(으)ㄴ/-는 from M24, -(으)면 from M20, -아/어서 from M10, -(으)ㄹ 수 있다 from M10, -기 위해 from M44, and -아/어지다 from M35.
