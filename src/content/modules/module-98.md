# Korean, Word-First — Module 98: Ground and Rank

Band 8 opens the Ring 3 long tail: no new grammar, thirty-three Grade-C nouns, and every one of them a sum on characters you already own. The theme is where a thing stands — on the ground and in the ranks. THE GROUND (입장 현장 중앙 내부 외부 남부 구역 지대 경계 서구) is the map's vocabulary a register above M31: the position you take, the scene where it happened, the zone, the belt, the border. THE RANKS (관계자 지도자 간부 부대 군사 무기 공격 훈련 지시) is the newsroom's chain of command — from the persons concerned up to the leader, and the drill, the weapon and the order between them. THE LAW (소유 사례 법칙 부정 불법 구속 증거 형사 행위 수단) is the courthouse of M90 one floor down, where the detective, the evidence and the warrant live. THE SCHOOL (교사 교장 입시 자세) is four words the staff room uses about itself. The organizing thesis: **the newspaper places people with 장 and 부** — the place-場 of 입장 and 현장 and the section-部 of 내부, 외부, 남부, 간부 and 부대 are two rails that run through half this shelf, and reading the rail reads the word.

**How to use this:** read the two rails first — 場 puts a thing on a spot, 部 cuts a whole into sections — and then the four drawers open themselves. Spend your reps on the homograph pairs the Radar patrol lists: this is the most collapsed shelf in Ring 3 so far (입장, 부정, 불법, 군사 and 경계 each carry two hanja), and the sentence, not the spelling, tells you which. Every sentence in Part 2 recycles one Band 7 ending by name; if a name is unfamiliar, that module's md is one tap away.

## Part 1 — Ground and rank

Five characters do most of the work on this shelf. Read them once and the drawers sort themselves:

| Rail | Character | On this shelf | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| place | 場 (M37's 주차장) | 입장 · 현장 | a spot a thing stands on or appears at |
| section | 部 (M32's 부분) | 내부 · 외부 · 남부 · 간부 · 부대 | one section of a whole; the first syllable says which |
| point | 指 (M93's 지적하다) | 지도자 · 지시 | a finger — at the way, at the job |
| matter | 事 (M36's 사고) | 군사 · 사례 · 형사 | an affair, a case, a matter someone handles |
| law | 法 (M39's 법) | 법칙 · 불법 | the rule, kept or broken |

Two rails and four drawers, in more detail:

- **The 場 rail — a spot on the ground:** the 場 of M37's 주차장 ("駐車場 park-car-place") puts a thing on a place. 입장 (立場) is the place you STAND — your position — and, by a second hanja, the place you ENTER (入場): M47's 신부 note's "신랑 신부 입장!" is that one. 현장 (現場) is the place where a thing APPEARED — the scene, the site. Behind them M27's 장소 and M12's 정류장 already ran the same character.
- **The 部 rail — a section of the whole:** the 部 of M32's 부분 ("部分 section-portion") cuts a thing into sections, and the first syllable says which: 내부 inside, 외부 outside, 남부 the south (with 북부, 동부, 서부 riding frozen behind it), 간부 the trunk-section of an organization — its executives — and 부대 a section of M58's 군대 — a unit. M76's 부 counted copies with the same character; M58's 부장 runs one section of it.
- **The ground beyond the rails:** 중앙 is the geometric centre (against M50's 중심, the heart of a matter), 구역 the zone an official drew, 지대 the belt the land itself makes — M60's 회색 지대 lands — 경계 the line where one ends and the next begins, and 서구 the European West against M31's 서양, the cultural one.
- **The ranks beyond the rails:** 관계자 pays the sign M55's 외 read, 지도자 points the way on the 指 of M93's 지적하다, 군사 is military affairs (and, by NIKL's second row, an old soldier), 무기 pays the 핵무기 of M92's 핵, 공격 reads M44's 전공 backwards, 훈련 pays M78's 강도, and 지시 points at the job with the 示 of M75's 표시하다.
- **The law drawer:** 소유 is the owning, 사례 the case that happened, 법칙 the law nobody voted on, 부정 foul play (and, by 否, denial — the 부정적 that M45's 긍정적 held lands), 불법 against the statute (and, by 佛, the Dharma), 구속 pays M86's 불구하다, 증거 is proof on the 據 of M86's 근거, 형사 the detective and the criminal case, 행위 the act with a legal name, 수단 the means in the hand against M32's 방법.
- **The school drawer:** 교사 is the teacher on the payroll (M4's 선생님 is the address), 교장 the principal on the school-校 M32's 교육 note kept apart, 입시 the exam that decides the door, and 자세 the posture M63's 바르다 and M73's 자세히 both left frozen.

::vocab::

**The sign register, one more time:** half of this shelf is read off walls before it is ever said aloud — 금연 구역, 주차 구역, 관계자 외 출입 금지, 공사 현장, 군사 시설 — four nouns and no verb, the register S2 taught you to read. When a word here feels bookish, that is why: it was built for a sign or a headline, and the mouth reaches for M6's 안 and 밖 or M4's 선생님 instead.

**Radar patrol:** 입장 is a standpoint (立場) or an entrance (入場) — the press release has the first, the wedding the second · 부정 is foul play (不正) or negation (否定) — 부정 선거 rigs, 부정적 merely frowns · 불법 is illegal (不法) or the Buddha's law (佛法) — the parking ticket and the sutra · 군사 is military affairs (軍事) as a determiner and an old soldier (軍士) with a particle · 지도자 leads on 指導, and M12's 지도 (the map) is 地圖 — a different 지도 · 무기 is martial-武, not M40's 무 (the radish) · 자세 (posture, attitude — 姿勢) is not M73's 자세히 (in detail — 仔細) · 경계 is a boundary (境界) or vigilance (警戒) — the guard does the second at the first · and 서구 is Western Europe (西歐) and also the West District of several cities (西區), which is 구역's 區, never M2's 구.

## Part 2 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** every sentence recycles one Band 7 ending and names it — -는 한 from M95 fences the arrest behind the evidence, -(으)ㄴ 결과 from M90 turns the officials' positions into a changed plan, -다가 from M94 interrupts the detective's search with a weapon, -(으)ㄹ수록 from M89 tightens the principal's speech as the exams near, -(으)ㄴ/는 데다가 from M92 piles the missing weapons on top of the drill, -(으)ㄹ까 봐 from M93 explains the extra military training, -기 마련이다 from M87 promises that the executives' wrongdoing will surface, and -(으)며 from M85 writes the statute that recognizes ownership and forbids the unlawful act. Under them ride the can-machine from M10, -아/어지다 from M35, the badge of M24 and the long negation from M28.

**Gap deck:** the sign that guards half the doors in Korea (관계자 외 출입 금지), the hell a teenager spends three years in (입시 지옥), and the verb that stops sorting means from methods (수단과 방법을 가리지 않다).
