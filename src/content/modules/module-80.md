# Korean, Word-First — Module 80: The Name Is the Recipe

M3's 김밥 note said it in one line — "김 (seaweed) + 밥 (rice) — the name is the recipe" — and M18 ran it across a menu (떡볶이, 삼겹살, 만두). This module runs the same machine at scale, then opens the drawer the food comes home in. Twenty-four food words assemble from parts you own: X+밥 (볶음밥, 초밥), X+찌개 (된장찌개, 김치찌개), X+탕 (설렁탕, 갈비탕 — the suffix M3's 국 note promised as "Fancier stews are 찌개 and 탕 — later"), X+차 (녹차, 홍차, 인삼차, 찻잔), 술+X (술잔, 술병, 술자리, 술집), and five loans the kitchen took (레몬, 와인, 카레, 소시지, 플라스틱). Then ten packaging words — 봉지, 비닐, 비닐봉지, 종이컵, 자판기, 도시락, 포장, 일회용, 일회용품 — and the argument Korea is having about them. The organizing thesis: **Korean names food by assembly and packaging by verdict** — you can read a menu you have never seen, and the bag has an opinion about you.

**How to use this:** read every compound as arithmetic before you drill it — almost every word here is two taught words and a rule. Spend your reps on the radar pairs the shelf hides: 봉투 vs 봉지, 비닐 vs 플라스틱, 포도주 vs 와인, the four 초s, and the three 회s inside 일회용. No new grammar today; the sentences recycle M20, M23, M25, M38, M39 and M52 in fresh forms.

## Part 1 — Assembly names the food

Every food word on this shelf is a receipt:

- **The 밥 shelf:** 볶다 (M18) + the -(으)ㅁ freezer (M33's 웃음, M40's 얼음) + 밥 = 볶음밥; the vinegar-초 M40's 식초 decoded + 밥 = 초밥, sushi as recipe; 밥 + M18's 그릇 = 밥그릇, the bowl and the livelihood; the food-食 of M18's 한식 + M2's 빵 = 식빵, the loaf.
- **The pot shelf, and a register ladder:** M18's 찌개 takes its engine in front — 된장찌개, 김치찌개 — and M54's 목욕탕 already read 탕 as hot-water-湯: 설렁탕, 갈비탕 — M30's 갈비, boiled. 국 (M3) is the everyday bowl, 찌개 the shared pot, 탕 the restaurant order.
- **The 차 shelf pays two frozen loans:** M60's 녹색 note poured 녹차 on credit; M59's 인삼 note left 홍삼 frozen — 홍차 reads the 紅 for both. 찻잔 wears the linking-ㅅ M46's 오랫동안 filed. 찬물 and 감 sit at the shelf's end: cold water with a proverb, and the persimmon the tiger feared (M49).
- **The 술 shelf:** M9's 술 + 잔/병/자리/집 — glass, bottle, occasion, bar. 포도주 reads the alcohol-酒 that has been inside 맥주 and 소주 since M3; 와인 is the same liquid on the menu.
- **The -집 rule:** 술집, 중국집, and the frozen 빵집/꽃집/고깃집 — a house named by what it sells, the pattern M18's 맛집 started.
- **The converter's kitchen:** 레몬 (l → ㄹ), 카레 (via Japan — 커리 is the other route), 소시지, 와인 (w → 와, the landing M78's 웨이터 mapped), 플라스틱 (five syllables from two — the interlude's law that Konglish is often longer than its source).

## Part 3 — Two machines, no new grammar

### 1. The compound machine, run at scale

Ring 1 taught the rule and one example; this module is the rule's annual report. Method or material in front, category behind: 볶음 + 밥, 된장 + 찌개, 갈비 + 탕, 녹 + 차, 술 + 집. The category word is always one you own (밥, 찌개, 탕, 차, 잔, 병, 집), so a strange menu is a list of front-halves to decode. When the front-half is a loan the machine still runs (레몬차, 카레라이스 — frozen), and when both halves are Sino the hanja does the work: 葡萄酒, 自販機, 一回用品.

### 2. The packaging verdict

Food is named by what it is; packaging is named by what it does to you. 일회용 says one-round-use and means throw it away; 비닐봉지 asks whether you need it and charges you if you do; 포장 is takeout, gift-wrap and the tent M18's 떡볶이 note pitched (포장마차). The bare modifier is the grammar here — 일회용 컵, 자판기 커피, 편의점 도시락 — nouns stacked in front of nouns with no particle, the way s_m78_beginner_app stacked 디자인 앱. Korea sorts its 쓰레기 (M13) strictly; this drawer is the vocabulary of that sorting.

**Calibration:** 포도주 and 와인 are the shelf's register lesson — the Sino compound names the recipe (grape-alcohol) and lives on labels and at church; the loan names the scene and lives on menus and dates. Same split as 자판기 (the machine) vs 커피 자판기 (the lobby ritual): pick the word by where you are standing.

## Part 4 — Building sentences

**Spot the recycling:** -(으)ㄹ까요 from M9 and 시키다 from M3 put the lunch vote to the 중국집, -(으)면 돼요 from M23 declines the 비닐봉지, -(으)면 안 돼요 from M20 posts the café's 일회용 rule, -아/어야 돼요 from M10, decoded by M39, receives the 술잔 with both hands, -자마자 from M38 pours the morning 찬물 with the counter 잔 from M5, -(으)ㄹ 때 from M25 and -아/어 주다 from M9 pack the 도시락, -아/어 버리다 from M52 lets the 자판기 coffee go cold in its 종이컵, and the badge -(으)ㄴ from M24 on M18's 뜨겁다 serves the hangover 설렁탕 by the 그릇.
