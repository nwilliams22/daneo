# Korean, Word-First — Module 56: The Written Verbs

S2 taught you to READ the written register; M32's quote machine taught you to relay it; this pack hands over its engine room — the verbs every article, notice, and textbook runs on, frequency-ranked into one room at last. The organizing thesis: **most of these are machines you already own, running at news altitude.** 밝히다 is M13's 밝다 through M37's causative party; 알려지다 is M37's 알리다 through M35's -아/어지다; 발생하다 is M36's 사고가 났어요 wearing a press badge. Band 4 built the engine; today it gets a byline.

**How to use this:** read the decodes before you drill — over half this shelf is causative/passive machinery plus hanja you've already met, and the X-rays do the memorizing. Spend your reps on the radar pairs (이용/사용, 발달/발전, 마치다/마치, 벌이다/벌다) — the written register splits words where speech got by with one.

## Part 1 — Masks off, one last sweep

The M36/M37 decode debt clears its biggest accounts:

- **밝히다** = M13's 밝다 + M37's causative 히 (the adjective wing that gave you 높이다): make-bright. Lamps 밝히다 rooms; the government 밝히다s facts.
- **일으키다** = M8's 일어나다 (get up / happen), made transitive by the party: raise a body, raise a problem — 문제를 일으키다.
- **나타내다** = M32's 나타나다 + M28's 내다 (the put-it-out verb): put an appearance out = express.
- **알려지다** = M37's 알리다 + M35's -아/어지다: become-known, no publicist named.
- **늘어나다** = M35's 늘다 + M28's 나다: the increase, erupting into view. With M37's 늘리다, the triangle completes: it grows / you grow it / it visibly grows.

## Part 3 — The frames of officialdom

### 1. ~에 의하면 / ~에 의해(서) — leaning on sources, crediting agents

의하다 (依 lean-on) joins M32's frame verbs — like 대하다, 'the dictionary forms are just their ID cards.' **[source]에 의하면** = according to: the 에 따르면 frame (M32) in full hanja dress — 뉴스에 의하면. **[agent]에 의해(서)** = by, for print's passives: M36 dressed the spoken agent in 한테 (경찰한테 잡혔어요); documents promote it — 경찰에 의해 발견되었다. Speech says 한테; the record says 에 의해.

### 2. ~에 관해서 / ~에 관한 — the gated topic

관하다 carries the connecting gate 關 of 관심 (M24) and 관계 (M32), and conjugates only ceremonially: **[topic]에 관해서** (about) and **에 관한 + noun** — the formal sibling of the 에 대해서 frame (M32)/에 대한, built exactly the way M44 framed -기 위해(서): learn the chunk, not the verb. 관련 extends the family to nouns: 관련 기사, ~와 관련해서.

### 3. The connective shelf, extended

S2's stiff connectives (및, 또는, 즉) gain three colleagues: **또한** (M29's 또 in a suit — moreover), **한편** (one-side — the news' scene-change: 한편, …; as a noun, 한편으로는 seesaws arguments), and **혹은** — the 'literary twin' S2's 또는 note promised, completing the or-ladder: (이)나 speaks (M43), 또는 forms, 혹은 haunts older print.

**Calibration:** these frames are register, not grammar — everything they say, Ring 1 particles already said. Reach for them when you write or present; recognize them everywhere else. A text message with 에 의하면 in it is a joke, and Koreans do make it.
