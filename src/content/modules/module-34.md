# Korean, Word-First — Module 34: Thoughts and Seemings

The quoting machine (M32–33) repeats what was said. This module builds what Korean does even more often: **hedging what you think.** Korean serves almost every opinion soft — 맛있어요 files a verdict, 맛있는 것 같아요 files an impression — and the machinery for it is pure assembly from parts you own. The vocabulary is the inner-life shelf: memory, hope, regret, certainty, and the emotion words that give your inferences something to infer from.

**How to use this:** neither glue pattern contains a single new part. Badges (M24/M25) + 것 (M17) + 같다 (M24) is the hedge; 보다 (M1) is the inference. Read the assembly diagrams, then go collect the feelings.

---

## Part 1 — The hedge is politeness, not doubt

When a Korean says 좀 어려운 것 같아요 about something they are certain is hard, they aren't waffling — they're leaving the door open for you to differ. The hedge is the polite default for opinions the way 요 is the polite default for endings. Calibrate: use it for taste, judgments, and disagreement; skip it for facts (your name is not a seeming).

The register works in English too — "I think it's a bit hard" vs "it's hard" — Korean just automated it.

## Part 3 — The glue: seeming machines

### 1. [badge] + 것 같다 — I think / it seems

Take M24/M25's badges, point them at 것, close with 같다: *it's the same as an X-ing thing.*

| Time | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| now (adjective) | **-(으)ㄴ 것 같아요** | 어려운 것 같아요 — seems hard |
| now (verb) | **-는 것 같아요** | 자는 것 같아요 — seems to be sleeping |
| past | **-(으)ㄴ 것 같아요** | 비가 온 것 같아요 — looks like it rained |
| future / guess | **-(으)ㄹ 것 같아요** | 눈이 올 것 같아요 — it'll probably snow |
| noun | **인 것 같아요** | 좋은 사람인 것 같아요 |

Bare nouns skip the badge entirely: 꿈 같아요 (like a dream), 바보 같아요 (like a fool) — with 마치 announcing the simile when you want drama.

### 2. -나 보다 — looks like (I have evidence)

Verbs take **-나 보다** (손님이 왔나 봐요 — shoes everywhere), adjectives take **-(으)ㄴ가 보다** (피곤한가 봐요 — he keeps yawning). The difference from 것 같다: **-나 보다 wants a clue you can point at.** A hunch is 것 같아요; a deduction is -나 봐요.

And count 보다's careers: see (M1), take-a-test (M15), try -아/어 보다 (M16), look-like -아/어 보이다 (M21), than-particle (M22), and now infer -나 보다. **Six jobs.** One verb is doing the work of an English thesaurus page.
