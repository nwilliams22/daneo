**Organizing machines (all owners verified — cite exactly these):**

1. **The -히 rack, at scale.** Lineage to cite: M10's 천천히/열심히
   are the raw examples; M32's 특히 note ("the adverb-히 of 천천히/
   열심히 (M10)"); M33's 조용히 note NAMED it ("the adverb factory
   from M32's 특히, now clearly a factory"); M42's 당연히 ("through
   the -히 factory (M32's 특히 started it; M33 made it official)");
   M62's 분명히 called it "The -히 rack's courtroom member" and 충분히
   "The -히 rack's generous member". Today's members, each hung on
   its adjective:
   - 정확히 — 정확하다 M63, whose note already ran "정확하게 =
     precisely (the -게 adverb, M33)". Teach the pair rule once, here:
     -게 (M33) is the live adverbializer any adjective can wear; -히 is
     the fossilized Sino one, and 하다-adjectives keep BOTH (정확하게/
     정확히, 간단하게/간단히) — same meaning, -히 a shade more written.
   - 간단히 — 간단하다 M63 ("Simple as in SHORT… 간단하게 말하면").
   - 단순히 — 단순하다 M63 ("measures complexity"): 단순히 = simply,
     MERELY — 단순히 돈 문제가 아니에요 (돈 M9, 문제 M7).
   - 특별히 — M57's 특별 note ran the word already: "And the adverb
     comes free: 특별히 = especially, particularly — the -히 of
     천천히/열심히 (M10) on 특별, the aimed version of M32's 특히."
     Receipt it; radar 특히 (spotlights a part) vs 특별히 (does a thing
     specially: 특별히 준비했어요, 준비하다 M8).
   - 대단히 — 대단하다 M24 ("대단해요! — impressive!"): 대단히
     감사합니다 = the formal thank-you (감사합니다 M1).
   - 굉장히 — 宏壯 vast-grand, both characters frozen (first
     appearance); 굉장하다 is untaught (rides frozen, glossed). The
     SPOKEN strong very: 굉장히 좋아요. Calibrate against M11's 아주/
     너무 and M62's 무척/몹시 (its note: "무척 feels; 몹시 suffers…
     print-flavored") — 굉장히 is the one Koreans actually say aloud.
   - 자세히 — 仔細 (frozen) — in detail: 자세히 설명해 주세요 (설명하다
     M24). 자세하다 rides frozen. Radar: 자세 (posture, 姿勢) is a
     different word, untaught — one line.
   - 적당히 — 적당하다 (TODAY, teach adjacent): 適當 suitable-fitting;
     the 當 is the fitting-當 of M34's 당연하다 ("當然 fitting-so"); 適
     frozen. 적당한 가격 (가격 M21) = a reasonable price; 적당히 = in
     moderation, and the national idiom 적당히 해 = take it easy /
     don't overdo it — also a warning to a slacker. 적당히 넣으세요 (넣다
     M28) = add to taste.
   - 꾸준히 — native, no hanja: steadily, without letting up — 꾸준히
     공부하다: the study adverb beside M10's 열심히 (열심히 burns,
     꾸준히 lasts). 꾸준하다 rides frozen.
   - 우연히 — 偶然 by-chance: 偶 frozen + the so-然 of 당연하다 M34 and
     자연 M22 (nature — 自然, the self-so; 자연's note has no decode, so
     decode it here as a first). 우연히 만났어요 (만나다 M2). Radar vs
     M62's 마침 ("Lucky timing… the universe briefly cooperating"):
     우연히 is chance, 마침 is chance that helped. 우연 the noun rides:
     우연이에요 = it's a coincidence.
   - 편히 — 편하다 M12 ("편해요. 편의점 (M6) is literally the 편-store"):
     편히 쉬세요 (쉬다 M8), 편히 앉으세요 (앉다 M9), and the formal
     goodnight 편히 주무세요 — M47's 주무시다, which that note pairs
     with 안녕히 주무세요.
   - 깨끗이 — 깨끗하다 M7 + -이, NOT -히: the spelling rule. After a
     ㅅ-final 하다-stem the adverb is written -이 (깨끗이, 따뜻이),
     because that's what the mouth says. M62's 가까이/멀리 note names
     the native -이 wing (verify wording before quoting) — 깨끗이 is
     the Sino-rack word that defected to it. 깨끗이 치우세요 (치우다 —
     verify owner; else 청소하세요 M13).
   - 굳이 — 굳다 (to harden, untaught, rides frozen) + -이: insistently,
     "if you must" — 굳이 왜? = why bother?; 굳이 안 가도 돼요 (-아/어도
     from M39, -(으)면 돼요 lineage M23). Said 구지: ㄷ+이 → 지, the
     same sound rule M9's 같이 note filed ("Pronounced 가치 — a
     sound-change bonus (ㅌ+이 → 치)"). The one-word retort 굳이? is
     a gap candidate.
2. **The degree dial — LESS and the new highs.**
   - 덜 — the rung M9's 더 ("더 주세요 — more, please. Later it builds
     comparisons") never had: less. 덜 맵게 해 주세요 (맵다 M3, -게 M33,
     -아/어 주세요 M9) = make it less spicy — THE restaurant sentence;
     덜 익었어요 (익다 M40: "고기가 다 익었어요 = the meat's ready") =
     undercooked. Radar: M41's 떨다 note's 덜덜 ("추워서 덜덜 떨었어요")
     is a shiver sound, not this word.
   - 되게 — the spoken really: 되게 맛있어 — 되다 (M10) wearing the -게
     shape of M33, frozen into an adverb meaning "intensely". Register:
     반말-flavored, friends-only; M11's 아주 is the neutral one, M29's
     매우 the textbook's. Do not over-explain the etymology — one
     clause.
   - 제법 — quite, more than expected: 제법 잘하네요 (잘하다 M17, -네요
     M26) = not bad at all! — the raised-eyebrow compliment. Calibrate
     vs M62's 꽤 ("The understatement professional… one honest notch
     below 아주"): 꽤 measures; 제법 is surprised.
   - 그다지 — not that much, negative-polarity: 그다지 안 좋아요. M62's
     그리 note already drew the line ("별로 shrugs, 그리 measures"; M17's
     별로 = "The politest possible shrug") — 그다지 is 그리's fuller,
     more spoken form: 별로 / 그리 / 그다지, three not-thats, calibrate
     in one line without re-teaching the first two.
   - 비교적 — pays M56's wink: its 비교하다 note ended "Wink: 비교적 =
     comparatively (riding frozen — M45's -적 factory, one badge
     early)." Today it lands, and 비교 (TODAY, adjacent) lands with it:
     比較 compare-compare ("both characters mean it"). 비교가 안 돼요 =
     no comparison; 비교적 싸요 (싸다 — verify owner) = relatively
     cheap. Note NIKL files 비교적 as an adverb; it also badges (비교적
     쉬운 문제).
3. **The exactness corner.**
   - 딱 — exactly / just: 딱 맞아요 (맞다 M15) = fits perfectly, 딱 하나
     = just one, 딱 좋아요 = just right. NIKL sense collapse — its
     B-row glosses the SPREAD sense (어깨가 딱 벌어지다 — 어깨 M14,
     벌어지다 M61 whose note has "Second sense, gaps… widen") and a
     C-row the HALT sense (딱 멈추다, 멈추다 M50); cover both in the
     note, the fit sense as headline. Radar: M60's 딱딱하다 ("Hard-and-
     stiff") is the mimetic; 딱 is the click of something fitting.
   - 정확히 (above), 곧바로 — 곧 M10 ("곧 도착해요") + 바로 M28 ("바로
     옆에… 바로 지금") fused: straight away, no detour — 곧바로 집에
     갔어요. Fully decodable; say so.
   - 즉시 — 卽時: the 즉 of S2's restater ("즉, [simple thing]… a
     verbal colon") + M5's o'clock 시 ("The word 시간 you know is this
     시 + 간") = at-that-very-hour: immediately, the SIGN and rulebook
     register — 즉시 연락하세요 (연락하다 M19). Ladder: 바로 (M28,
     spoken) / 곧바로 (spoken, emphatic) / 즉시 (printed).
   - 한꺼번에 — all at once, in one go: 한 (M5's counter contraction)
     + 꺼번 (frozen fossil) + 에. 한꺼번에 하지 마세요. Radar vs M28's
     한번 ("one-time… 한번 해 보세요"): one TIME vs one GO.
   - 오직 — only, solely: the adverb that fronts M30's 만-particle
     ("하나만 — just one; 너만 — only you") and M55's 뿐 ("너뿐이야 = it's
     only you"): 오직 너만, 오직 하나뿐. The ballad's only; also
     signage: 오직 회원만 (회원 — verify owner; frozen if untaught).
   - 내지 — 乃至 (both frozen): the range word on forms, 3 내지 5명 =
     three to five; also "or" in legalese. Written only. Radar: M34's
     짜증 note's "짜증 내지 마세요" is 내다 + -지 — a homograph trap.
   - 각자 — 各自 each-self: M55's 각 ("各 each… the written-notice
     determiner") + the self-自 of M32's 자기 ("自己 self"). 각자
     계산해요 (계산하다 M9) = everyone pays their own — the Dutch-pay
     line, beside M62's 따로따로. NIKL lists noun AND adverb; both
     free.
   - 전부 — 全部 all-part: the all-全 of M31's 전국 that M39's 전혀
     note cited + the part-部 of M32's 부분 ("部分 section-portion").
     전부 얼마예요? — M29's 모두 note ran "모두 얼마예요?"; calibrate
     the trio in one line: 다 (M9, native, before the verb) / 모두
     (M29, counts the crowd) / 전부 (totals the bill). 전부 다 doubled
     is normal speech.
4. **The time rack.**
   - 해마다 — pays M38's 매년 note, which said "매년 = 해마다, same
     promise, different register": 해 (M11: "Also 'year' (새해 — new
     year)") + 마다 (M31). The native every-year; 날마다/달마다 ride
     free on the rail.
   - 내내 — throughout, following its span: 여름 내내 (여름 M11), 하루
     내내 — beside M38's 종일 ("usually as 하루 종일") — 종일 is the
     unit, 내내 is the stretch felt from inside; 내내 기다렸어요.
   - 또다시 — 또 (M29: "once-more-again") + 다시 (M10: "redo-again")
     fused: yet again, the narrator's word — 또다시 실패했어요 (실패
     M44). Fully decodable.
   - 오래도록 — 오래 M10 + -도록 (an ending meaning "until / so that",
     untaught glue — frozen here, glossed in one clause): for a long,
     long time — 오래도록 기억할게요 (기억하다 M19) is a toast and a
     farewell. The 오래 family assembles: 오래 M10 / 오랜만 M19 /
     오랫동안 M46 / 오래도록 / and —
   - 오랜 — the determiner: 오랜 친구 = an old friend (NOT 늙은 — 늙다
     M35 is aging; 오랜 is duration), 오랜 시간. M19's 오랜만 note
     ("M10's 오래 grew a suffix and became a greeting") — 오랜만 was
     오랜 + 만 all along; M46's 오랫동안 note is the sibling. Tag pos
     "noun" like M46's 옛 and M13's 새 (the determiner shelf).
