Read SHARED-BRIEF.md first; follow exactly. Band 7 = the Band 6 pack
contract (garnish-free, full notes, 8 sentences) PLUS glue parts, the
Band 4 shape. NO morpheme-possessive phrasings. Every quote from a

**Theme:** the dependent nouns that let Korean HEDGE and RECKON, and
the nouns of judgment they sit beside. 33 words in three drawers. THE
HEDGES (glue): 듯 / 듯하다 (seems), 만하다 (worth), 셈 (reckoning), 마련
(bound to), 지경 (on the verge), 나름 (in its own way), 바 (what), 아니하다
(the formal not). THE VERBS OF RECKONING: 여기다, 여겨지다, 뜻하다,
판단하다, 평가, 평가하다, 인식하다, 의식, 의식하다. THE NOUNS OF JUDGMENT:
관점 시각 견해 논리 상식 의문 진실 진리 관념 이념 가치, and the four
adjectives of WORTH: 소중하다 올바르다 바람직하다 적절하다. Organizing
thesis: **Korean measures certainty with nouns** — 것 같다 (M34) was a
noun (것) plus "same"; every hedge here is another noun on the same
chassis, and the more formal the noun, the more the sentence is written
rather than said.

4. **The verbs of reckoning (9).**
   - 여기다 — to consider, regard as: 소중하게 여기다 (this module's

   - 뜻하다 — to mean, signify: M10's 뜻 ("meaning") + 하다. Register

   - 판단하다 — to judge. M34's 판단 ("判斷 judge-cut: the call y…" —

   - 평가 (評價) / 평가하다 — evaluation / to evaluate. 評 judge (a
