compound machine M3's 김밥 note named ("김 (seaweed) + 밥 (rice) — the
name is the recipe") run at scale: X+밥, X+찌개, X+탕, X+차, 술+X, and

자판기/도시락 — and the culture that is now arguing about it. Two IOUs
open the module: M3's 국 note promised "Fancier stews are 찌개 and 탕 —
later" (M18 paid 찌개; 탕 lands today as a food suffix on 설렁탕/갈비탕),
and M30's 포도 note pre-poured 포도주. Organizing thesis: **Korean
names food by assembly and packaging by verdict** — you can read a
menu you have never seen, and the bag has an opinion about you.
