# Brief — M79 "The Converter's Back Shelf" (order 88, ring 2, band 6)

Read SHARED-BRIEF.md first; follow exactly. Band 6: garnish-free. NO
morpheme-possessive phrasings ("M14's -지 마세요" breaks the verifier —
write "-지 마세요 from M14"; hang a possessive only on a whole taught
word). Every quote from a shipped note must be VERBATIM — the reviewer
greps each one. Read `src/content/modules/module-konglish.md` (the
Konglish interlude — its four rules are this module's engine) and every
loanword note cited below BEFORE drafting. All 31 words are nouns; ids
are RR of the hangul (w_geurup, w_midieo…), never the English.

**Theme:** 31 loanwords the Konglish interlude's rules predict on sight
— the pool's whole converter shelf, shipped at once. The interlude
taught the machine (Rule 1 the sound converter, Rule 2 the clip
machine, Rule 3 made-in-Korea, Rule 4 the false friends); this module
runs it on thirty-one words, and adds the rule the interlude did not
state: **every loanword in Korean lives next to a Korean word it did
NOT replace.** 박스 beside M54's 상자, 테스트 beside M10's 시험, 카운터
beside the frozen 계산대, 메일 beside M27's 편지, 미디어 beside M48's
언론, 그룹 beside M16's 팀 / M23's 단체 / M69's 모임 — the loan took ONE
job and the older word kept the rest (the interlude's own verdict:
"When a loanword surprises you, assume specialization, not error").
Four of these words have been riding frozen in shipped notes (패션,
벨트, 버튼, 테스트) — they land today and pay those notes.

**Organizing machines (all owners verified — cite exactly these):**

1. **The frozen ones land (4) — teach these FIRST, they pay debts.**
   - 패션 — three IOUs: M48's 잡지 ("패션 잡지, 시사 잡지"), M72's 모델
     ("패션 모델 — 패션 riding frozen"), M78's 디자이너 ("패션 디자이너
     (패션 riding frozen"). Fashion through the converter (f → ㅍ, sh →
     ㅅ+ㅕ): 패션쇼 (쇼 frozen), 패션 감각 (감각 — verify owner; else
     frozen). M21's 스타일 ("제 스타일이에요 — it's my style") is what
     YOU wear; 패션 is the industry.
   - 벨트 — M36's 안전하다 ("안전벨트 = the belt"), M60's 매다 ("안전벨트를
     매세요 (seatbelt, riding frozen)"), M78's 챔피언 ("챔피언 벨트 (벨트
     riding frozen)"). 벨트를 매다 with M60's 매다 (the tie's verb); the
     ㅡ-cushion on the final t (the interlude's 테스트 rule). Native
     허리띠 (frozen — M14's 허리 + a band) is the older word for the
     same object.
   - 버튼 — M75's 취소 ("취소 버튼 (버튼 — riding frozen)"). Pressed with
     M20's 누르다 ("르-family: 눌러요. Buttons, links, elevator floors,
     and the 좋아요 below"): 버튼을 누르다. Radar: the button sewn on a
     shirt is native 단추 (frozen) — 버튼 is pressed, 단추 is buttoned.
   - 테스트 — the interlude's own example ("test → 테스트 (one syllable
     becomes three)"), M51's 콘서트 ("the 테스트 rule: crowded consonants
     get vowels"), M67's 심리 ("심리 테스트 is the personality quiz Korea
     loves"). RADAR MANDATORY vs M10's 시험 ("시험을 보다 — to take
     (literally 'see') an exam"): 시험 is graded and sat; 테스트 is
     tried — 테스트하다 = to test a product, 테스트를 받다 = to be tested.
     M44's 검사 ("檢査 check-examine") is the inspection.
2. **The false friends (Rule 4) — words that kept one job (7).**
   - 박스 — M50's 교통사고 note ("every Korean windshield carries a
     블랙박스 (Konglish: dashcam)"). Register pair with M54's 상자 ("箱子
     box-thing"): 택배 박스 on the shop sign, 택배 상자 in the dictionary
     — both live; 박스 also counts: 사과 한 박스 (M3's 사과).
   - 카운터 — NIKL glosses it 계산대: the checkout M76's 계산 note left
     frozen ("계산대 (the checkout — riding frozen) is where it happens").
     카운터에서 계산하세요 (M9's 계산하다) = pay at the counter; also the
     hotel's front desk and the bar's counter seat. Not a person who
     counts.
   - 메일 — pays M16's 이메일 verbatim: "메일 alone also works — Korean
     rarely means postal mail by it." The postal thing is M27's 편지
     ("편지를 쓰다/받다") and M52's 소포; 메일을 보내다/확인하다 (M9's
     보내다, M20's 확인하다). 메일 주소 (M12's 주소) = email address.
   - 미디어 — the medium/media in academic and news register: 소셜 미디어
     is what the West says where M20's 에스엔에스 note filed Korea's word
     ("Korea says SNS — es-en-es — where English says social media");
     the institution is M48's 언론 ("The press as an institution"), the
     act is M32's 방송; 매체 (frozen, Sino) is the dictionary synonym.
     미디어 = the channels, plural, as a field of study.
   - 팬티 — underwear, NOT trousers: British 'pants' is the trap; M21's
     바지 is pants. Radar on M51's 팬 ("The converter's f → ㅍ rule runs
     twice and lands both English words on one syllable") — 팬티 is a
     third landing, unrelated. The category word 속옷 rides frozen
     (underwear — a later shelf).
   - 홈페이지 — Rule 4 specialization: a 홈페이지 is the WHOLE website,
     not the landing page — M20's 사이트 is the same thing one register
     down; 회사 홈페이지 (M2's 회사), 학교 홈페이지 (M4's 학교). 홈 alone
     rides frozen.
   - 필름 — the roll in M20's 카메라 and the stock M2's 영화 was shot on;
     M48's 촬영 ("撮影 snatch-shadow: filming is catching shadows before
     they move on") is what it caught. GAP CANDIDATE: 필름이 끊기다 =
     to black out drinking — M36's 끊기다 ("modern life is a list of
     things that 끊기다") applied to memory: the film cut, the night
     gone.
3. **The clip machine and the cushions (Rule 1–2) (8).**
   - 그룹 — rank 879, the pool's top word. Three jobs: the K-pop
     아이돌 그룹 (아이돌 frozen) / 걸그룹 / 보이그룹; the conglomerate
     (삼성그룹 — the 대기업 M64 ran; verify 대기업's owner before
     citing); and any group of people. Place it against M16's 팀 ("우리
     팀"), M23's 단체 ("단체 여행 — the guided tour"), M69's 모임 ("any
     recurring get-together") — 그룹 is the loose one.
   - 피시 — NIKL glosses it PC: the sign says PC방, the mouth says
     피시방 — M8's 방 note ("Also in 노래방 (karaoke room) and PC방") and
     g17_bang own the room; 피시 is the computer itself in speech-spelled
     form, M10's 컴퓨터 in initials. Gap ko may carry Latin (KTX, PC방
     precedent); sentence ko must be hangul — write 피시방.
   - 모니터 — the screen as hardware; M20's 화면 ("화 (picture) + 면
     (surface): picture-surface") is what's ON it. 모니터하다 / 모니터링
     (frozen) = to monitor.
   - 테이프 — 붙이다 (M37: "스티커를 붙여요") sticks it; 스카치테이프
     (frozen) is the brand-as-noun; the cassette 녹음 테이프 (M48's 녹음)
     is the sense grandparents mean. 
   - 파일 — pays M49's 날아가다 verbatim: "파일이 날아갔어요 = the unsaved
     file, mourned in every office". 파일을 저장하다 (저장 — verify owner;
     else frozen), 파일을 보내다 (M9). Radar: the paper folder is also a
     파일; M16's 서류 is what goes in it.
   - 액세서리 — five syllables of cushion (accessory → 액세서리): the
     interlude's "Konglish is often LONGER than its source". M21's
     넥타이 note set the verb ("An accessory, so it takes 하다"): 액세서리를
     하다 — over M21's 목걸이, 귀걸이, 반지.
   - 블라우스 — b-l cushioned to 블, the s to 스: four syllables. Beside
     M21's 셔츠 (the shirt) and M21's 원피스 ("Konglish 'one-piece'").
   - 레스토랑 — the French r-tail Korean keeps (레스토랑, not 레스토런):
     the WESTERN or upscale restaurant; M2's 식당 is any restaurant, and
     the family-restaurant chains of the 90s were 패밀리 레스토랑
     (frozen). 
4. **Sports, kitchen, pocket (12).**
   - 골프 / 골프장 (adjacent) — M17's 치다 ("The strike-play verb: 피아노를
     치다, 기타를 치다, 테니스를 치다. If it's hit or plucked, it's 치다"):
     골프를 치다. 골프장 = 골프 + the place-場 M37's 주차장 note paved
     ("The -장 (場, grounds) also paves 정류장 (M12) and 운동장"). 스크린
     골프 (frozen) — the simulator bars where Korea actually golfs.
   - 배드민턴 — 치다 again; the apartment-complex evening sport, nets
     strung in every 공원 (M6). 셔틀콕 rides frozen.
   - 스키장 — pays M11's 스키 ("스키를 타다 — ride skis, like every
     vehicle since M6") + the same 場: the resort. 강원도 (M31) has them.
   - 스케이트 — M6's 타다; 스케이트를 타다 on M40's 얼음. 피겨 스케이팅
     (frozen) and the 김연아 era, one line; 인라인 (frozen) for the
     wheeled kind.
   - 오븐 — M18's 굽다 (its gloss is grill / bake — the note itself is about 삼겹살; don't quote it): 오븐에 굽다. Korean kitchens
     ran on M40's 가스 note's stovetop ("가스레인지 (Konglish 'gas range'
     = the stovetop)") and M72's 전자 note's microwave ("전자레인지 (the
     microwave — riding frozen"); the oven is the immigrant appliance,
     and the 에어프라이어 (frozen) took its job.
   - 라이터 — M14's 담배 and 피우다 ("Literally 'to make bloom'"), M13's
     불 ("One word for fire and the lights"): 라이터 있어요? / 불 좀
     빌려주세요 (빌리다 — verify owner) — the smoker's two asks. Radar:
     the writer is M48's 작가; 라이터 only lights.
   - 렌즈 — M21's 안경 note gave the verbs ("안경을 쓰다 OR 끼다"): 렌즈를
     끼다 — 콘택트렌즈 clipped to 렌즈 (Rule 2); and M20's 카메라 렌즈.
   - 포스터 — on M13's 벽 with M37's 붙이다; M32's 광고 ("廣告 wide-tell")
     is what it does; M48's 공연 poster is where 공연 연기 (that note's
     radar) gets read wrong.
   - 메모 — 메모하다 = jot; 메모지 (frozen) = the pad; M15's 공책 holds
     it, M10's 쓰다 writes it, M34's 기억 ("기억이 안 나요 = it's not
     coming to me") is why. Radar: 메모리 (frozen) is the phone's.
   - 팩 — NIKL 'pack': the carton (우유 팩 — M2's 우유) and the sheet mask
     (마스크팩 — M14's 마스크, K-beauty's export). Radar: 팩스 (fax,
     frozen) is a different machine.
   - 껌 — gum with a tense ㄲ: the converter usually lands English g on
     ㄱ; 껌 tensed like M2's 빵 ("Starts with tense ㅃ"). M41's 씹다 ("To
     chew"): 껌을 씹다. GAP CANDIDATE: 껌값 = chicken feed — 껌값이에요 =
     it's dirt cheap (M7's 싸다 as an idiom); 껌값도 안 돼요.
   - (teach 메일 last of the pack if you want a closer; otherwise 껌.)
