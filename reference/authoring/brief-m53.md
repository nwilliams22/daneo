**Glue:** **-는데/-(으)ㄴ데** — THE spoken connective, and a special case:
grep confirms it has NEVER appeared in the course (zero hits in sentences and
module mds). CURRICULUM.md planned it to "garnish wherever it first fits" in

budget the md accordingly. Frame it at the doctor's office, its natural home:
1. **Background-setting** — state the situation, then make your move:
   머리가 아픈데 약 좀 주세요 (symptom → request); 감기에 걸렸는데 병원에
   가야 돼요? The -는데 clause hands the listener context.
2. **Soft contrast** — 약을 먹었는데 안 나았어요 (I took it, BUT). Gentler
   than -지만 (M7 — verify): -지만 argues, -는데 just notes the mismatch.
3. **Trailing off politely** — 좀 아픈데요… (the sentence that lets the
   pharmacist finish it for you). The -는데요 ending as a soft landing.
Forms: verbs/있다/없다 take -는데, adjectives -(으)ㄴ데, nouns -인데, past
-았/었는데 (works on adjectives too). ㄹ-drop applies (살다 → 사는데).
