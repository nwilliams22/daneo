**Theme:** 26 time words, almost all of them WELDS of parts the
learner owns — the native day-count family closes (나흘/닷새), the
날-welds and 그-welds arrive (이튿날/전날/그다음/그해/어젯밤), the year
ladder gets its ends (재작년/금년), the clock gets its soft spots
(점심때/저녁때/밤중/밤낮), the seasons become periods (여름철/겨울철/
사계절/무더위), and six schedule words close (일상생활/규칙적/스케줄/
임시/본래/만일). Organizing thesis: **Korean tells time by welding —
a pointer or a span onto a day, a night, a year — and the welds obey
sound rules you already hold** (the linking ㅅ, the ㄹ→ㄷ swap, the
ㄹ-drop, the nasal slide). The register pattern M46's 지난해 filed
("anchors say 지난해, the break room says 작년 (M25)") runs the whole
shelf: 저번/어젯밤/땜-style words are spoken, 금년/수년/임시/본래 are
print.

4. **The clock's soft spots (4).**

   - 밤중 — M8's 밤 + S2's 중 ("중국's middle-中, hung after any noun"):

   - 밤낮 — M8's 밤 + M28's 낮 ("The day's missing timeslot: 아침 → 점심

   - 임시 — 臨時: 臨 (to face, approach — frozen) + the time-時 M46's
