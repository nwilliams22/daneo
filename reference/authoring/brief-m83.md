# Brief — M83 "Kit and Carriage" (order 92, ring 2, band 6)

Read SHARED-BRIEF.md first; follow exactly. Band 6: garnish-free. NO
morpheme-possessive phrasings ("M14's -지 마세요" breaks the verifier —
write "-지 마세요 from M14"; hang a possessive only on a whole taught
word). Every quote from a shipped note must be VERBATIM — the reviewer
greps each one. Read M43's md (the 品/器 shelf: 제품, 상품, 계산기),
M51's 악기 note (機 vs 器), M21's md (the wardrobe and its -복/-화
suffixes) and M50's md (the transport fleet) BEFORE drafting. All 34
words are nouns, three of them proper nouns (teach them as real words
with real notes — the app has done it since M31's 인천/동대문).

**Theme:** the last 34 Grade-B words in the pool, and with them the
B-list closes. Four drawers. THE MACHINE SHELF (4): 전화기, 수화기,
사진기, 청소기 — and the 機/器 split M51's 악기 note drew ("tool-器 — a
different hanja from 비행기's machine-機 (M12), same career"). THE SINO
TAILS (10): a suffix each — 학생증 (證), 연락처 (處), 좌석 (座席), 자가용
(用), 준비물 (物), 화장품 (品), 운동복 (服), 국기 (旗), 도자기 (器),
수표 (票). THE KIT (11): 바늘, 빗, 담요, 속옷, 책가방, 유리창, 신문지,
글씨, 받침, 축구공, 권투, 배구 — native and welded things in the bag,
the bed, and the gym. THE CARRIAGE (9): 고속버스, 시내버스, 공항버스,
자가용, 좌석, 지하도, and the three proper nouns 김포공항, 인천공항,
동대문시장. Organizing thesis: **a Sino tail tells you what a thing IS
FOR, a native weld tells you what it is MADE OF** — 화장품 is goods for
makeup, 신문지 is paper that was a newspaper — and the last module of
the B-list ends at the airport, where the learner's next trip starts.
Firsts this module decodes: 證, 座, 服, 旗, 髮-free (that's M82's), 陶/瓷,
排, 鬪, 浦.

**Organizing machines (all owners verified — cite exactly these):**

1. **The machine shelf: 機 vs 器 (4 + 도자기).** M51's 악기 note is the
   law: "The 기 is tool-器 — a different hanja from 비행기's machine-機
   (M12), same career." M43's 계산기 ("計算器 count-machine" … "the machine-기 of 세탁기 (M13)
   and 비행기 (M12)") — note M43 wrote
   器 for the calculator; M12's 비행기 ("비행 (flight) + 기 (machine)")
   and M13's 세탁기 ("세탁 (laundry) + 기 (machine) — the same 기 as
   비행기") are 機. Rule for the shelf: a MOTOR gets 機, a VESSEL or
   HAND-TOOL gets 器.
   - 전화기 — 電話機: M20's 전화 ("M8's 전화하다 was this noun wearing
     하다") + 機: the phone as an OBJECT — 전화기를 바꾸다 (M9's 바꾸다),
     공중전화기 (frozen — the payphone, museum piece). 전화 is the call,
     전화기 is the thing; today's 전화기 is a 스마트폰 (frozen) and nobody
     says 전화기 for it — 핸드폰 (M6) took the job.
   - 수화기 — 受話器: the receive-受 M78's 수상 note decoded (受賞: "受, receive (a FIRST") + the talk-話 M33's 회화 filed ("전화's talk-화") + 器 (a
     vessel for the voice): the handset. 수화기를 들다 (M28's 들다) / 놓다
     (M40's 놓다) — the landline gestures, alive in every office and
     every drama slam-down. Radar: not 수화 (sign language — frozen).
   - 사진기 — 寫眞機: M17's 사진, whose hanja M75's 복사 note finally read
     ("寫眞, write-the-real: a photo is reality written down") + 機: the
     camera in Sino — the register twin of M20's 카메라 (speech). 사진기
     is what the museum label and the grandfather say.
   - 청소기 — M8's 청소하다 ("Polite: 청소해요. Rooms and houses — wiping
     objects is 닦다") dismounted to 청소 + 機: the vacuum — pays M37's
     돌리다 verbatim: "청소기를 돌려요". 로봇 청소기 (로봇 frozen) is the
     one that bumps into the 소파 (M13 — verify owner).
   - 도자기 — 陶瓷器: 陶 earthenware + 瓷 porcelain (both FIRSTS) + the
     vessel-器 of M51's 악기: ceramics as a category — 고려청자 (celadon)
     and 조선백자 (white porcelain), both frozen and glossed, the two
     dynasties' signatures; 이천 도자기 축제 (M25's 축제) one line. 器 is
     the vessel-hanja in its literal job at last.
