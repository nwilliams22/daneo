**Glue:** **-(으)려고 (하다)** — intention, spoken register.
1. [stem]-(으)려고 + action = in-order-to (시장에 가려고 일찍 나왔어요).
2. -(으)려고 하다 = be planning to / be about to — 지금 나가려고 해요, and the
   on-the-verge job: 버스가 출발하려고 해요 (even non-volitional: 비가 오려고
   해요 — the sky "intends").
The TWO radars this glue must run:
- vs M12's -(으)러 (verify module-12.md): -(으)러 only rides the 가다/오다
  family and names an errand; -(으)려고 rides any verb, can stand alone with
  하다, and carries intent. 사러 가요 vs 사려고 해요 — an errand vs a plan.
- vs M44's -기 위해(서) (verify): 위해 is the written/formal purpose; -(으)려고
  is what mouths actually say. Register pair, exactly like M43's 월급/임금
  framing (verify that pair's note style if you imitate it).
≥2 glue sentences (one bare -(으)려고, one -(으)려고 해요).
