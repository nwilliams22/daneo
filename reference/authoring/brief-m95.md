**Theme:** giving ground and taking sides — the nouns of contest and
the endings of concession. 31 words: CONTEST (갈등 경쟁 경쟁력 협력 승부

the cost of), **-든지** (whichever — the indifferent choice), **대신에 /
반면에** (instead of / whereas), **-는 한** (as long as), **-기만 하면**
(if only). Organizing thesis: **concession has a ladder** — -아/어도
(M39) is the mouth's even-if, -더라도 is firmer, -(으)ㄹ지라도 is the
essay's, -(으)ㄹ망정 concedes the worst — and the choice-endings are the
same ladder walked the other way.
