# Brief — M74 "Two Words, One Verb" (order 83, ring 2, band 6)

Read SHARED-BRIEF.md first; follow exactly. Band 6: garnish-free. NO
morpheme-possessive phrasings ("M14's -지 마세요" breaks the verifier —
write "-지 마세요 from M14"; hang a possessive only on a whole taught
word). Every quote from a shipped note must be VERBATIM — the reviewer
greps each one. Native-root morphology is where drafters invent
etymology: every split below is a split of TAUGHT words; do not add
hanja to native words.

**Theme:** the fused-verb factory — verbs that are two owned words
welded into one — plus the 편-family of adjectives and the native
nouns of true and false (real/fake/free, others, style, appetite).
The learner already owns every part; today they learn that Korean
welds. Three shelves.

**Organizing machines (all owners verified — cite exactly these):**

1. **The weld shelf — verb + verb, noun + verb, adverb + verb.**
   Teach the rails, then the verbs on them:
   - **X + 나다 (the erupt-door):** M28's 나다 ("The eruption verb
     hiding everywhere… Feelings and happenings COME OUT in Korean;
     나다 is the door they use"); M67 shipped the feelings through it.
     Today's: 생겨나다 — 생기다 (M28: "Things sprout: 문제가 생겼어요")
     + 나다: come-into-being-and-OUT — the narrated emergence (새로운
     문제가 생겨났어요); radar: 생기다 is the casual arising, 생겨나다
     the chronicled one. 자라나다 — 자라다 (M35: "Children 자라요; so do
     plants, cities, and rumors") + 나다: grow-up-and-out, the sprout —
     자라나는 아이들 (아이 M4; badge -는 M24), the newspaper phrase.
     소문나다 — 소문 (M24: "Things-heard… travels faster than 뉴스") +
     나다: the rumor erupts = to become known: 소문난 맛집 (맛집 M18 —
     "the place worth the pilgrimage and the line") is on every
     restaurant's sign.
   - **X + 내다 (the put-forth door):** M28's 내다 ("The put-it-out
     verb… 시간을 내다 — MAKE time"). 찾아내다 — 찾다 (M6: "One verb
     covers both the searching and the finding") + 내다: the FINDING
     half, forced out — 답을 찾아냈어요 (답 M15), 방법을 찾아내다 (방법
     M32). 알아내다 (find out, untaught) rides frozen on the same rail.
   - **X + 들다 (the enter door):** M28's 들다 ("sleep is entered (잠이
     들어요)"), and M28's 잠 note: "잠이 들다 — you fall IN". 잠들다 —
     잠 + 들다 welded: to fall asleep, the moment of crossing; 아기가
     잠들었어요 (아기 M25). The sleep shelf assembles here: 자다 (M1,
     the state) / 잠 (M28, the noun) / 잠들다 (the crossing) / 졸다 (the
     failed crossing, below).
   - **X + 가다 (M28's compound-motion system):** 흘러가다 — 흐르다
     (M35: "Water, time, tears, music — Korean lets them all 흐르다")
     + 가다: flow-AWAY — 시간이 흘러가요, 물이 흘러가요 (물 M1). 르-family
     from M7's table (흘러). Radar: 흐르다 flows in place, 흘러가다
     flows past, 흘리다 (M66: "the run-out spill verb") spills.
   - **X + 치다 (the intensifier tail):** two verbs share a -치다 that
     is NOT M17's 치다 ("The strike-play verb… If it's hit or plucked,
     it's 치다") — it's a suffix that means "with force". 넘치다 — 넘다
     (M36: "To go over… 선을 넘다") + 치다: over-with-force = to
     overflow: 물이 넘쳐요, 자신감이 넘쳐요 (자신감 M39). 합치다 — 合
     (join, frozen; the 합 of 합격 — verify owner, cite if taught) +
     치다: to combine, merge — 두 반을 합쳤어요 (반 M15's class). Radar:
     더하다 adds (arithmetic), 합치다 merges (two become one), 모으다
     (M17) collects.
   - **더하다** — 더 (M9) + 하다: to add — 2 더하기 3 (더하기 rides free:
     "plus"); and the second life: 더하다 = to be worse/more so — 겨울에는
     더해요 (겨울 M11) = it's even worse in winter.
   - **잘살다** — 잘 (M10) + 살다 (M4) welded: to be well-off — 잘사는
     나라 (나라 M23), 잘살아요. Spacing radar, exactly M63's 잘못 note
     ("Spacing radar: 잘 못 해요 pleads inability; 잘못했어요 admits
     guilt"): 잘 살아요 spaced = lives well; 잘살아요 fused = is rich.
   - **밤늦다** — 밤 (M8) + 늦다 (M8) welded: to be late at night; lives
     90% as 밤늦게 (-게 from M33): 밤늦게까지 공부했어요 (까지, 공부하다
     M1); 밤늦은 시간 with the badge.
   - **없애다** — 없다 (M1) + 애: the odd sixth causative. M37's party
     taught 이/히/리/기/우; 애 is the one-off that makes 없다 transitive:
     make-not-exist = get rid of. 없애 버렸어요 (버리다 from M52: gone
     for good). Radar vs M61's 없어지다 ("just stops being where you
     left it") — 없어지다 happens, 없애다 is done: the launcher/launched
     pair.
   - **들르다** — stop by, drop in: 르-family (M7's table: 들러요,
     들렀어요) — 편의점에 들렀어요 (편의점 M6), 잠깐 들를게요 (잠깐 M28).
     Two radars, both real: 들다 (M28) is enter/hold/cost; 들리다 (M36:
     "to be heard") is 들려요 — one vowel from 들러요. Say them side by
     side.
   - **졸다** — to doze (sitting up, losing): 수업 시간에 졸았어요 (수업
     M10). ㄹ-verb: 좁니다. 졸리다 (sleepy, untaught) rides frozen —
     졸려요 is the sentence everyone says. Radar: 잠들다 crosses; 졸다
     fights and loses.
   - **빨다** — pays M54's 빨래 IOU verbatim: "frozen inside rides 빨다,
     the wash-clothes verb, untaught and waiting in the pool". NIKL
     sense collapse — the B-row is 옷을 빨다 (wash clothes); a C-row
     빨다 is 젖을 빨다 (to suck — 빨대, the straw, is suck-stick, frozen
     wink); cover both. ㄹ-drop: 빱니다. Register: 빨래하다 (M8) is the
     chore; 빨다 is the verb on the clothes.
2. **The 편 family completes, and four adjectives.**
   - 편하다 M12 ("comfortable / convenient") has covered two jobs for
     sixty modules; M42's 편안하다 split off "at ease" ("편하다 says the
     sofa is comfortable; 편안하다 says YOU are at peace"). Today the
     other side splits: 편리하다 — 便利 convenient-benefit: the 편 of
     편하다 + the benefit-利 of M56's 이용하다 ("利用 benefit-use") —
     convenient ONLY, of things and services: 교통이 편리해요 (교통
     M12), 편리한 앱. And 불편하다 — 不便: the 불- ledger M42's 불만 note
     told you to keep ("불가능, 불편, 불안, 불만 — file the prefix, it
     keeps paying") and M67's 불행하다 note re-ran — 불편 lands:
     uncomfortable, inconvenient, AND socially awkward (불편한 자리,
     자리 M12); the sign register 몸이 불편하신 분 = persons with
     disabilities (euphemism — one line). Three-way split in one
     sentence: 편리하다 things, 편안하다 people, 불편하다 negates both.
   - 화려하다 — 華麗 flowery-beautiful (both frozen, first appearance):
     gorgeous, flashy — 화려한 무대 (무대 M51: "the raised floor
     performance happens on"), 화려한 옷 (옷 — verify owner). Can be a
     dig: 화려한 싱글 (the flashy single — 싱글 frozen).
   - 귀엽다 — cute: ㅂ-irregular, 귀여워요 (M7's table: "ㅂ → 워"). The
     aegyo adjective; radar vs M1's 예쁘다 (pretty = beauty; 귀엽다 =
     small-and-charming) — 강아지 (M22), 아기 (M25), and boyfriends.
   - 가득하다 — M62's 가득 ("Brimful — a container meeting its limit…
     The frequency list files it with its verb, 가득 차다") + 하다: the
     STATE adjective — 사랑이 가득한 집 (사랑 M7, 집 M4) vs 가득 차다 the
     event (M66's 차다 note lists "full (가득 차다)" among its four).
   - 씩씩하다 — spirited, plucky: the mimetic-shaped mold M71's 깜빡
     note cited ("the same sound-as-shape mold as M54's 튼튼하다 and
     M60's 딱딱하다"). 씩씩한 아이, 씩씩하게 대답하다 (대답하다 M10) — the
     kindergarten virtue. 용감하다 (brave, untaught) rides frozen for
     the radar: courage vs pep.
3. **The native nouns of true and false.**
   - 멋 — M21's 멋있다 note pre-split it: "멋 (style/flair) + 있다:
     style-exists" — the root lands: 멋을 부리다 (부리다 untaught —
     frozen, glossed: to put on airs/dress up), 멋을 내다 (내다 M28).
     멋지다 (splendid, untaught) rides frozen as 멋있다's twin.
   - 거짓 — M19's 거짓말 note pre-split it too: "거짓 (false) + 말
     (speech): false-speech" — the root: falsehood; 거짓 정보 (정보
     M33) = false information; 거짓이에요 = it's false.
   - 가짜 — fake: the -짜 family. 진짜 (M11) / 가짜 / 공짜 — real, fake,
     free — three -짜's; name the family once (짜 is a native tail
     meaning roughly "the thing itself"; 진 and 가 are 眞 true and 假
     false — 假 frozen, first appearance). 가짜 뉴스 (뉴스 M24), 가짜
     명품 (명품 frozen = luxury goods) = a knockoff.
   - 공짜 — free of charge: 空 empty (frozen — the same 공 sits
     undecoded inside M12's 공항 and M22's 공기; decode it here as a
     first) + 짜. 공짜로 받았어요 (받다 M9). Radar vs M20's 무료 ("무
     (without) + 료 (fee…) Fee-less. Opposite: 유료"): 무료 is the sign,
     공짜 is the grin. Proverb is a gap candidate.
   - 남 — others, other people, not-us: 남의 일 (일 M2's work sense) =
     someone else's business; 남 앞에서 = in front of others; 남 이야기
     (이야기 M24) = gossip. Radar: NOT the 남 of M4's 남자 (男) nor M31's
     compass 남 — this one is native and means everyone who isn't 우리
     (M4). 남남 = strangers. Proverb is a gap candidate; M18's 떡 note
     and the shipped gap item 그림의 떡 are the receipts to cite.
   - 볼일 — errand, business to see to: 보다 (M1) + the -(으)ㄹ badge
     from M24 + 일 (M2: "Also means 'day' and 'work'"): a thing-to-see-
     to. 볼일이 있어요 = I've got something to take care of — the
     polite exit line. Mandatory wink: 볼일(을) 보다 = to use the
     restroom. Rom is bollil (ㄴ-insertion then ㄹ-assimilation, the
     큰일 class — the linter has the exception).
   - 밥맛 — 밥 (M1) + 맛 (M18: "The noun hiding inside 맛있다") = the
     taste of rice = appetite: 밥맛이 없어요 = no appetite (입맛,
     untaught, rides frozen as the near-twin). REGISTER LANDMINE, the
     way M71 handled 새끼: 밥맛이야 / 밥맛 없어 (반말) = you're gross,
     you turn my stomach — an insult, not a food review. Say it 밤맏
     (rom bammat).
