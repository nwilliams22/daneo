# Brief — M81 "The Notice Board" (order 90, ring 2, band 6)

Read SHARED-BRIEF.md first; follow exactly. Band 6: garnish-free. NO
morpheme-possessive phrasings ("M14's -지 마세요" breaks the verifier —
write "-지 마세요 from M14"; hang a possessive only on a whole taught
word). Every quote from a shipped note must be VERBATIM — the reviewer
greps each one. Read M75's md and every m75 word note (the Dismount
precedent: verb → bare noun "the way print writes") and M57's md (the
abstract-noun toolkit) BEFORE drafting — this module is Dismount II
plus the compounds print builds from the dismounted parts. All 32
words are nouns.

**Theme:** 32 Sino nouns that live on signs, forms, and front pages.
Eleven are DISMOUNTS — roots you have only met inside a -하다 verb or a
longer compound, now standing bare as print writes them: 안전 (M36's
안전하다), 유명 (M17's 유명하다), 친절 (M4's 친절하다), 중요 (M16's
중요하다), 적극 (M45's 적극적), 고속 (M50's 고속도로), 사무 (M16's
사무실), 이동, 보호, 공동, 정식. Ten are WELDS of owned parts (글자,
글쓰기, 우리말, 각국, 대중문화, 중요성, 생활환경, 등록금, 한국적, 독일어,
신문사, 야외, 시외) — several paying IOUs verbatim. The rest are the
radar-heavy singles (종, 질, 고급, 예금, 주택, 동기, 부작용, 국립).
Organizing thesis: **the notice board speaks in bare Sino roots; speech
puts 하다 or 적 back on** — 안전 운전 on the sign, 안전하게 운전하세요 in
the mouth. Firsts this module decodes: 立 (국립), 副 (부작용), 住/宅
(주택), 級 (고급), 預 (예금), 共 (공동), 護 (보호), 切 (친절).
