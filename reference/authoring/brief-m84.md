# Brief — M84 "Loose Ends" (order 93, ring 2, band 6 — the Ring 2 closeout)

Read SHARED-BRIEF.md first; follow exactly. Band 6: garnish-free. NO
morpheme-possessive phrasings ("M14's -지 마세요" breaks the verifier —
write "-지 마세요 from M14"; hang a possessive only on a whole taught
word). Every quote from a shipped note must be VERBATIM — the reviewer
greps each one. Read M10's md (Part 3: can / must / because — the
glue this module extends), M49's md Part 3 §2 (the volunteer's -겠-),
M50's md (-(으)려고), M29's 때문 and 왜냐하면 notes, and M81's 고급 note
BEFORE drafting.

**This module is different from the Band 6 packs.** It is SMALL (16
words) and GLUE-LED: it closes Ring 2 by paying the six Grade-A words
that slipped through Ring 1 and teaching the four workhorse patterns
that no module owns although drafters and notes keep reaching for
them. The md therefore has FOUR glue parts (one per pattern), like a
Band 4 module, and one short vocab part. Word notes stay at full house
depth. All 16 words are nouns (급히 is an adverb — house rule: tag
`noun`).

**Theme:** the receipts. Six Grade-A words that Ring 1 walked past —
three of them named as "riding frozen" or "waits its own turn" in
shipped notes — plus ten Grade-C companions that sit in the same
drawers, plus the four endings everybody has been paraphrasing around:
**-(으)ㄹ게요** (the promise), **-기 때문에** (the formal because — an
IOU M29's 때문 note wrote out in words), **-(으)ㄹ 수도 있다** (the
might), **-(으)려면** (if you mean to). Organizing thesis: **the last
module of a ring is where the promises get kept** — the notes said
"later"; this is later.

**Ledger note:** the six Grade-A words get NO ledger rows (the
ring2-slices ledger rejects grade-A rows as Ring 1 debt — the
coverage script counts them straight from words.json). The ten Grade-C
words get rows with `(C)` appended.

**Organizing machines (all owners verified — cite exactly these):**

1. **-(으)ㄹ게요 — the promise ending (glue part 1).** First person
   only, spoken, made TO the listener: 제가 할게요 (I'll do it), 먼저
   갈게요 (I'll go ahead), 나중에 전화할게요, 다시 올게요, 금방 갈게요.
   Vowel/ㄹ stems + ㄹ게요, consonant stems + 을게요; said 할께요 (the
   ㄱ tenses — unwritten, like 할 거예요 says 할 꺼예요). Casual: -(으)ㄹ게.
   Three-way contrast, all owners verified:
   - M8 owns -(으)ㄹ 거예요. g8_future_probably: "-(으)ㄹ 거예요 does two
     jobs: about yourself it's a plan (갈 거예요 — I'm going to go);
     about others or the world it's an educated guess (지금 바쁠 거예요
     — she's probably busy). Same ending, plan or prediction — who the
     subject is decides." A plan is announced; -(으)ㄹ게요 is OFFERED —
     it answers the other person and can be withdrawn if they object.
   - M49 owns the volunteer -겠-. M49's md: "First person, -겠- pledges:
     제가 하**겠**습니다 = I'll do it (hand raised, will engaged) —
     stronger than 할 거예요's mere plan." -(으)ㄹ게요 is that pledge in
     shirtsleeves: 제가 하겠습니다 in the meeting, 제가 할게요 in the
     kitchen. And M18's 잘 먹겠습니다 has a 요-register twin: 잘 먹을게요
     (to a friend who is paying).
   - Radar: -(으)ㄹ게요 NEVER takes a third-person subject (no *비가
     올게요) and never asks a question — that is what makes it a
     promise and not a forecast.
   - Sentence slot: ≥1 sentence with -(으)ㄹ게요 answering an implied
     request (e.g. the 양복 or the 공중전화 shelf).

2. **-기 때문에 — the formal because (glue part 2).** PAYS M29's 때문
   note VERBATIM: "The formal -기 때문이에요 answers 왜냐하면 — recognize
   now, produce in Ring 2." Also M29's 왜냐하면: "'If you ask why—':
   M10's 왜 + 하면. Opens the answer and usually lands on -기 때문이에요.
   English says because mid-sentence; Korean announces it first."
   Build: the -기 nominalizer from M13 + M29's 때문 + 에 — "because of
   [verb]-ing". Also [noun]이기 때문에 (학생이기 때문에) and the past
   -았/었기 때문에. Sentence-final: -기 때문이에요 (the 왜냐하면 answer).
   The because-family, three rows — owners verified:
   - -아/어서 (Ring 1): neutral, spoken, cannot precede a command.
   - -(으)니까 = M10. M10's md table: "-(으)니까 | requests & commands |
     바쁘**니까** 나중에 전화해 주세요" and "Rule of thumb: if the
     sentence ends in 주세요/-(으)세요/-(으)ㄹ까요, the reason takes
     **니까**."
   - -기 때문에: written, formal, objective — the newspaper's, the
     essay's, the announcement's because; ALSO cannot precede a
     command (같이 -아/어서). Calibration: 늦어서 죄송해요 (mouth), 늦었기
     때문에 (report). Radar vs M29's noun-때문에 (눈 때문에) — same 때문,
     the verb version just needs -기 to become a noun first.
   - Sentence slot: ≥1 sentence with -기 때문에 (a 긴급/공중전화 or 급
     fact stated formally), and the md should show -기 때문이에요 once.

3. **-(으)ㄹ 수도 있다 — the might (glue part 3).** M10 owns -(으)ㄹ 수
   있다 — M10's md: "Same hook as the future (-(으)ㄹ), then 수 (a way)
   + 있다/없다" and "Ability as existence — the 있다 factory's grandest
   product". Add the even-도 from M2 to 수 and ability becomes
   possibility: 비가 올 수도 있어요 (it might rain), 늦을 수도 있으니까
   먼저 드세요, 그럴 수도 있죠 (that's possible — the conceding shrug,
   -죠 from M51). Sort it against the guess-family, owners verified:
   M8's -(으)ㄹ 거예요 (a prediction you'd bet on), M34's 것 같다 (a
   hunch — M34's md: "[badge] + 것 같다 — I think / it seems"), M34's
   -나 보다 ("-나 보다 wants a clue you can point at"), M49's evidence
   -겠- (reading the sky in front of you). -(으)ㄹ 수도 있다 is the
   weakest: it only says the door is not locked. Its most-used form is
   the hedge with -(으)니까 from M10: ~수도 있으니까 + advice. Radar:
   M55's -(으)ㄹ 줄 알다 is know-how, not possibility (M55's md drew the
   line: "-(으)ㄹ 수 있다 says a way EXISTS; -(으)ㄹ 줄 알다 says you've
   LEARNED the trick").
   - Sentence slot: ≥1 sentence with -(으)ㄹ 수도 있다 hedging (weather,
     lateness, an exam grade — 급).

4. **-(으)려면 — if you mean to (glue part 4).** M50 owns -(으)려고 —
   M50's md: "Vowel and ㄹ stems take **려고**, consonant stems
   **으려고** — then say what you did about it" — and M20 owns -(으)면.
   -(으)려면 is the two welded: -(으)려고 하면 contracted — "if you intend
   to X, (then you must / should)". Its natural closer is M10's
   -아/어야 돼요 or -(으)세요 from M9: 인천공항에 가려면 공항버스를
   타세요 (M83's 인천공항 and 공항버스), 합격하려면 열심히 공부해야 돼요
   (M10's 열심히), 양복을 사려면 백화점에 가세요. Radar: -(으)면 alone
   is "if it happens"; -(으)려면 is "if you MEAN to make it happen" —
   the difference between 비가 오면 and 비를 피하려면 (M36's 피하다). Also the fixed phrase 시간이 걸리다 after it:
   한국어를 잘하려면 시간이 걸려요 (M12's 걸리다).
   - Sentence slot: ≥1 sentence with -(으)려면 + -아/어야 돼요 or
     -(으)세요 (a route: 호실/번지/공항).
