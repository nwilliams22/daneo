**The honorific shelf (a module thesis):** Korean grammar has TWO ladders — the
politeness endings (M4/M19's registers) and the SUBJECT-honoring machinery. This
module ships the second: 께서 marks the honored subject, -(으)시- agrees, and a
small vocabulary REPLACES plain words entirely: 나이→연세, 생일→생신, 밥→진지
(note-only if untaught — verify), 자다→주무시다, 먹다→드시다 (M4 — verify)/잡수시다,
있다→계시다 (M4 — verify), 만나다→뵙다, 말→말씀 (M27 — verify). Teach the shelf AS
a system; each entry's note names its plain twin.
- **잡수시다 is a grade-A debt word** (rank 6878, one of the course's 9 missing
  grade-A words): teach as a full entry, NO ledger row (the M44 졸업 precedent) —
  드시다's more ceremonious sibling, the verb grandmothers deploy on grandchildren.
- 뵙다: 처음 뵙겠습니다 (verify whether this formula already appeared — grep);
  the humble-see verb: YOU 뵙다 an elder, an elder 보다-s you.
- 모시다: attend/serve/escort — 부모님을 모시고 살아요, the live-with-and-care
  verb; also 모시고 가다 (escorting an elder somewhere).
- 돌아가시다 (pass away — the honorific death) is NOT on the NIKL list: a
  beyond-list garnish candidate (max 3 total), decoded from M23's 돌아가다
  (return — verify): the honored return. Culturally near-mandatory here.
