**Theme:** the native verbs of the body in motion and things going
wrong — 34 verbs and the dependent noun 채. Drawers: PUT AND WEAR
(걸치다 두르다 대다 삼다 감추다 둘러싸다 덧붙이다 매달리다 들이다 익히다
거두다 넘기다), GO WRONG (저지르다 때리다 치르다 쓰러지다 흩어지다
빠져나가다 퍼지다 외치다 일어서다 앞장서다), LIGHT AND WEIGHT (비치다
비추다 띄다 빛나다 실리다 기울이다 다지다 맺다 아끼다 더불다), and the two
do-verbs 이러다 / 그러다. Glue — the ENDINGS OF MISHAP: **-(으)ㄴ 채(로)**
(in the state of), **-다가** (in the middle of, and then), **-다 보니 /
-다 보면** (having kept at it, one finds / if you keep at it), **-(으)ㄹ
뻔하다** (almost), **-고 말다** (ended up), **-아/어 대다** (keeps on,
annoyingly), **-기 일쑤다** (is always doing). Organizing thesis: **Korean
narrates accidents with auxiliaries** — the verb says what you were
doing, the ending says how it went wrong — and the native verb shelf is
where those endings live.
