# Brief — M45 "Character" (order 53, ring 2, band 5)

Read SHARED-BRIEF.md in this directory first and follow it exactly (including the
hard-rules section — it's new since the last batch).

**Theme:** the personality module — how Korean describes what people (and things)
are LIKE. Ring 1 taught the judgment adjectives (M24: 착하다/친절하다/무섭다 etc.
— verify) and M21-era traits (부지런하다/게으르다/성격 — verify modules). This
module's real cargo is three WORD-FACTORIES — the suffixes that manufacture
character vocabulary. It's a glue-heavy module by design; the vocabulary IS the
glue's output.

**Glue — three factories, the module's spine:**
1. **-적 (的)** — the "-ish/-al" suffix: noun + 적 = adjective-noun (needs 이다 or
   -으로): 개인적인 질문, 일반적으로. The slice hands you TEN products
   (구체적/적극적/일반적/대표적/사회적/세계적/정신적/전통적/경제적/역사적/과학적/
   개인적/정치적/긍정적 — count them yourself) — teach the MACHINE once, then let
   each word entry decode its noun root (most roots are taught: 개인? 사회 m32,
   세계, 전통 m26, 경제 m32, 역사, 과학, 정치? — verify every root and cross-ref).
   The bare 적 gets its own word entry (pos noun, dependent-noun sense; ledger row
   exists). RADAR REQUIRED: NIKL also lists 적 = 敵 enemy (rank 2665, grade B, same
   base string — the ledger collapses them), so the 적 entry's note must cover
   enemy-적 too (적이 많다; 라이벌). M42's 감동 note already promised "-적: a later
   module owns the factory" — this is that module; pay the IOU explicitly.
2. **-스럽다** — noun + 스럽다 = "has the quality of": the slice gives
   자연스럽다/조심스럽다/걱정스럽다; taught precedents to retro-decode: 자랑스럽다
   (M26), 사랑스럽다 (M42 — its note promised the formalization), 만족스러워요
   (M39's note). ㅂ-family conjugation (자연스러워요).
3. **-답다** — noun + 답다 = "worthy of the name": 남자답다, 어른답다, 학생답다,
   너답다 (all built from taught nouns — verify each; none are slice words, the
   factory runs on demonstration). The 답다/스럽다 split is the lesson:
   여성스럽다 (has feminine quality — said of anyone/anything) vs 여성답다
   (lives up to being a woman — only said OF a woman). X답다 requires being X.
4. **-롭다** as a footnote factory (same family): 자유롭다 (slice!) from 자유
   (verify taught), 여유롭다 from slice-mate 여유; 새롭다 (taught — verify module)
   retro-decoded as its oldest product.
≥1 sentence per factory (-적/-스럽다/-답다), 4th sentence free.
