**Theme:** the measuring shelf's last shipment, in three drawers. The
metric loans (그램/킬로그램/킬로/밀리미터/킬로미터/톤) and the

five number-nouns (숫자/계산/절반/다수/일등) and six more counters
(인분/부/통/송이/호선/년도 + the yen and the clipped 땜) fill the
counter drawer M5 opened; and eight position words (가로/세로/위아래/
중간/정상/꼭대기/곁/너머) extend M6's position nouns to the summit and
the far side. Organizing thesis: **Korean measures with borrowed
units, counts with native and Sino numbers, and places things with
nouns, not prepositions** — M6's 위 note ("A NOUN, not a preposition" — it goes on: 책상 위 =
"the desk's topside." with straight double quotes; quote only the first clause) is the rule this whole shelf obeys.
