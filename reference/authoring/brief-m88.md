**Theme:** time lived through, and the ending that reports what you
SAW. 33 words: the LIFE nouns (삶 과정 단계 시기 시점 초기 직후 이래 흐름
전환 회복 운명 인연 짝 한마디 움직임) and the VERBS of going through
(겪다 거치다 깨닫다 살아오다 살아가다 지니다 갖추다 잇다 이어지다 나아가다
머물다 유지하다 지켜보다 애쓰다 이룩하다 흔들리다 무너지다). Glue: the
retrospective -더- family — **-던**, **-더니**, **-았/었더니**,
**-더라고요**, **-던데(요)** — the one piece of tense Korean has that
English lacks: "as I witnessed it". Organizing thesis: **-더- is the
eyewitness marker** — every ending here reports something the speaker
saw or lived, not something they know, and the words on the shelf are
the nouns and verbs of a life witnessed.

2. **-더니 and -았/었더니 — I saw X, then Y (glue part 2).** -더니: a
   change the speaker WATCHED, subject usually not "I": 아침에는 맑더니
   오후에 비가 왔어요 (M11's 맑다, M2's 오후/비) = it was clear in the
   morning, then it rained; 열심히 하더니 합격했어요 (M10's 열심히, M44's
   합격) = he worked hard and, sure enough, passed. -았/었더니: I DID X,
   and found Y — first-person: 약을 먹었더니 나았어요 (M10's 약, M14's
   낫다) = I took the medicine and (found that) I got better; 문을
   열었더니 아무도 없었어요 (M13's 열다, M?? 아무도 — verify owner). Sort
   against -(으)니까 from M10 (a reason) and -자마자 from M38 (a
   sequence): -더니 is a sequence you witnessed with a turn in it.
   Sentence slot: ≥1 each.

3. **-더라고요 — I found that / I saw that (glue part 3).** The spoken
   retrospective: 그 식당 정말 맛있더라고요 (M3's 식당, M11's 정말, M1's
   맛있다) = that restaurant turned out to be really good (I ate there);
   생각보다 어렵더라고요 (M10's 생각 + M22's 보다-particle, M7's 어렵다) =
   it was harder than I expected, I found. Casual: -더라. Cannot report
   your own present feeling directly (no *내가 슬프더라고요 — but 슬프더라고요
   about someone you watched is fine) — the eyewitness rule. Sort against
   -았/었어요 (a fact) and -잖아요 from M42 (you know it too): -더라고요 =
   I was there, you were not, here is what I saw. Sentence slot: ≥1.

4. **-던데(요) — the retrospective background (glue part 4).** -는데
   from M53 ("the corner-turner" — verify the M53 md's own phrase before
   quoting) with -더- inside: 어제 갔는데 → 어제 가 봤더니… no — 어제

   there were a lot of people (I saw); 그 영화 재미있던데 같이 볼래요? (M2's
   영화, M7's 재미있다) = that film looked good, want to see it? Soft
   contrast or background, witnessed. Sentence slot: ≥1 (may share a
   sentence with another part).
