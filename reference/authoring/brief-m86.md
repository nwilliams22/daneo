**Theme:** why things happen, in the written register. 31 words: the
GROUNDS nouns (원인 근거 바탕 탓 영향 작용 결론 필요성), the four
GRAMMAR-VERBS that only exist to build a preposition (인하다 → -(으)로
인해, 비하다 → 에 비해, 불구하다 → 에도 불구하고, 비롯하다 → 을 비롯해)
plus 비롯되다 and 당하다 (the have-it-done-to-you verb), the CIRCUMSTANCE
nouns (사정 형편 처지 실정 여건 — five words for "the situation" and
the register between them), and the PRESSURE nouns (피해 부담 압력 위기
우려 반응 요구 대책 방안 해당하다 의존하다). Organizing thesis: **Korean
builds its prepositions out of verbs** — "because of" is a verb (인하다),
"compared to" is a verb (비하다), "despite" is a verb (불구하다),
"including" is a verb (비롯하다) — and once you see the verb inside the
preposition, the whole newspaper opens.

**Organizing machines (all owners verified — cite exactly these):**

1. **The grammar-verbs (glue part 1): -(으)로 인해(서) · 에 비해(서) ·
   에도 불구하고 · 을/를 비롯해(서).**
   - 인하다 (因-) — to be due to. Only ever in 로 인해(서)/로 인한: 폭우로
     인해 — because of the downpour; 사고로 인한 피해 (badge form, M24).
     因 = cause (a first; also inside 원인 below). Written twin of M29's
     때문 ("[noun] 때문에 — because of it: 눈 때문에, 너 때문에 (credit or
     blame; tone decides)") — 때문에 is the mouth's, 로 인해 the report's.
   - 비하다 (比-) — to compare. Only in 에 비해(서)/에 비하면: 작년에 비해
     = compared to last year. The compare-比 M56's 비교하다 decoded
     ("比較 compare-compare" — verify span) and M73's 비교 carries. RADAR:
     NOT M11's 비 (rain).
   - 불구하다 (不拘-) — to not be bound by. Only in 에도 불구하고:
     비에도 불구하고 = despite the rain. 不 (the not-不 of M39's 부족하다
     — "不足 not-enough-foot"; verify) + 拘 hold (a first; 구속 is a C
     word, leave frozen). Written twin of -아/어도 from M39 and 그래도
     (verify owner).
   - 비롯하다 — to include as the first of; 비롯되다 — to originate.
     Native. 서울을 비롯한 대도시 = big cities, Seoul first among them;
     그 문제에서 비롯된 갈등 = the conflict that originated there (갈등
     — riding frozen; it belongs to M95).
   - Sentence slot: ≥2 sentences using these (one 로 인해, one 에도
     불구하고 or 에 비해).

2. **The causal endings (glue part 2): -는 바람에 · -느라고 · -(으)ㄴ
   탓에 / 덕분에 · -(으)ㄴ 나머지.**
   - -는 바람에 — because of (an unexpected event), always a bad
     result: 버스를 놓치는 바람에 늦었어요. M11's 바람 (wind) in a
     dependent-noun job: "in the wind of". Contrast -기 때문에 (M84,
     neutral, formal) — -는 바람에 is spoken and always a mishap.
   - -느라고 — because (I was busy doing), same subject, a cost paid:
     공부하느라고 못 잤어요. Verb stems only, always the speaker's own
     activity; 하느라 (clipped) in speech.
   - -(으)ㄴ 탓에 / -(으)ㄴ 덕분에 — blame vs credit. 탓 PAYS TWO IOUs
     VERBATIM: M69's 조상 note "(못되면 조상 탓, with this word riding
     froz" — quote the full span (lookup.py 조상) — and the M63 gap deck
     g_m63_jal_tat: "탓 (blame — the accusatory cou" — quote in full.
     M69's 덕분 is the credit side (read and quote its note). 탓 =
     blame-noun: 남 탓 = blaming others, 내 탓이에요 = my fault (M19's 내
     — verify). -(으)ㄴ 탓에 늦었다, -(으)ㄴ 덕분에 합격했다.
   - -(으)ㄴ 나머지 — as a result of (an excess of): 너무 기쁜 나머지
     울었다 = so happy that (she) cried. 나머지 (the rest, remainder —
     verify owner; if untaught it rides frozen and glossed, and the
     ending is taught as a fixed frame).
   - Sentence slot: ≥2 sentences (one 탓에/덕분에, one -는 바람에 or
     -느라고).
