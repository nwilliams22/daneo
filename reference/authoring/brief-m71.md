# Brief — M71 "The Spoken Floor" (order 80, ring 2, band 6)

Read SHARED-BRIEF.md first; follow exactly. Band 6: garnish-free.

**Theme:** the noises Korean makes between sentences. 야 (rank 821)
is the highest-ranked word the app hasn't taught; 응, 자, 음 sit
right behind it. M19 built the register ladder and M29 opened the
interjection drawer (아, 아이고, 그래, 그럼); today the drawer fills:
the 반말 floor, the floor-holders every register uses, the discourse
gear-shifts, and the humble verbs that set the register in the
first place. REGISTER is the organizing idea — every note says WHO
you may say this to. Interjections take pos `phrase` (matching
M29's 아/아이고/그래); adverbs take `noun` (house rule); 어떡하다 is
`verb`, 새끼/한잔 are `noun`. NO morpheme-possessive phrasings.

**Organizing machines (all owners verified — cite exactly these):**

1. **The 반말 floor (M19's rules).**
   - 야 — hey! To friends, juniors, kids; NEVER upward. Radar three
     ways: (a) M19's md §4 taught the vocative — "vowel-ending names
     take 야 (민수야!)" — bare 야 is that vocative unhooked from any
     name; (b) not the night-야 of 야근 M16 nor the field-야 of 야구
     M17 (both notes exist — 야구's note already radars 야근); (c) 야!
     alone is also the yelp of surprise.
   - 응 — casual yes. M33's 예 note said it: "반말 drops it to 어 or
     응 (M19's rules)." S1's ㅇㅇ entry is "응 (casual yes) stripped
     to its consonant, twice." The answer to 밥 먹었어?; doubled 응응
     as the listening-nod.
   - 으응 — the drawn-out 응: reluctant agreement, or the rising
     "hm?" that asks you to repeat. NIKL files it separately; same
     word, longer breath. Also the toddler's whine.
   - 아니야 — M19's md gave the form ("아니에요 → 아니야. This is the
     one genuinely new form"); NIKL files it as its own interjection,
     so it gets a seat: the flat casual no. M37's 장난 note ran 장난
     아니야 (= seriously intense); S4's 아이가 is its regional shape.
   - 아뇨 — 아니요 (M1) squeezed: the polite no as actually said.
   - 어때 — 어때요 (M24's 어떻다: "어때요? — how is it?") minus the
     요: 이거 어때? = how about this one? 어때, 괜찮아?
   - 그렇지 — 그렇다 M29 + the bare tag M51's md filed ("반말 keeps
     just -지 (알지?)"): 그렇지! = that's it / exactly; 그렇지? =
     right? Polite face = 그렇죠 (M51's -죠). RADAR vs 그렇지만 M29 —
     one syllable apart, "right?" vs "however."
2. **The floor-holders (every register).**
   - 자 — 자, 시작합시다 (M9's -(으)ㅂ시다) = now then, let's begin;
     the attention-gatherer of teachers, hosts, tour guides. Radar:
     pure noise — not 자다 M1's stem, not the person-者 of 소비자
     M43, not the ruler.
   - 음 — hmm, the thinking noise; 음… 글쎄. Also 음, 맛있다 = mmm.
     Radar: the 음 inside 음식 (M3) and 음악 (M2) is hanja; this one
     is breath.
   - 글쎄 — M24's 글쎄요 note said "Drop the 요 among friends; M19
     rules apply" — receipt. 글쎄… = well, dunno; 글쎄, 모르겠어;
     and the set reply 글쎄 말이야 = I know, right? / tell me about
     it (gap candidate).
   - 어머 — oh my! Surprise, feminine-coded (men default to 어 or
     M29's 아이고); 어머, 어떡해 — the pair this module completes;
     longer form 어머나. Radar: not 어머니 M4, though it sounds like
     a truncated call to mother — it isn't.
   - 하하 — laughter spelled out; NIKL files it as an adverb (pos
     `noun`, house rule). S1's ㅎㅎ note: "the h of 하하." 하하 웃다
     (웃다 M7); the vowel is the demographic — 호호 women, 허허
     grandfathers, 히히 kids, 하하 everyone.
3. **The gear-shifts (adverbs).**
   - 하여튼 — 何如- "however it is": anyway. M33's 어쨌든 note built
     "어찌+했+든 ('however it went')" and said "the -든 piece is a
     Ring 2 pattern still in the mail" — still in the mail (no -든지
     entry exists; don't teach it). 하여튼's 튼 is that same 든.
   - 아무튼 — the third anyway, the most spoken one; 아무 M29 ("The
     any-pointer… 아무도 없어요 — nobody") + 튼. Register the trio: 어쨌든 M33 (neutral),
     하여튼 (a shade formal), 아무튼 (the mouth's favorite).
   - 분명 — the bare adverb: 분명하다 M34, 분명히 M62 (whose note
     says M34 promised it). 분명 = before a judgment: 분명 뭔가 있어요
     = there's DEFINITELY something. Radar: 분명히 swears to what the
     eyes saw (M62: "the eyewitness word"); 분명 infers.
   - 아마도 — 아마 M24 + 도 M2: probably, I'd say — the 도 hedges it
     one notch softer; 아마도 그럴 거예요 (M8's guessing future).
   - 깜빡 — the blink, and the slip: 깜빡 잊었어요 (잊다 M15) = it slipped my mind; 깜빡했어요 (깜빡하다 rides free) =
     I forgot; 눈을 깜빡이다, 불이 깜빡이다 = blink, flicker. A
     mimetic — Korean's sound-and-shape words (if any module names
     의태어, cite it; else don't).
   - 자꾸만 — 자꾸 M38 ("Repetition, minus the control") + M30's 만
     (only/just): keeps on and ON — the ballad line 자꾸만 생각나.
     The 만 adds the exasperation.
4. **The humble and speech verbs.**
   - 말씀드리다 — M27's 말씀 note PROMISED it: "Humble twist: it also
     lowers YOUR words to an elder (말씀 드릴게요 — Ring 2 machinery,
     previewed)" — receipt. 말씀 M27 + 드리다 M9 ("giving UPWARD").
     말씀드릴 게 있어요 = there's something I need to tell you; 다시
     말씀드리면 = to put it again (the presenter's phrase). RADAR vs
     말씀하시다 — the ELDER speaks (M4's -(으)시-): 말씀하세요 (you,
     honored, speak) vs 말씀드려요 (I, lowered, speak): same 말씀,
     opposite direction.
   - 뵈다 — NIKL SENSE COLLAPSE, cover both: its B headword 뵈다01 =
     보이다 (M36) CONTRACTED — alive in set phrases: 눈에 뵈는 게 없다
     = nothing is visible to the eye = blind with rage (gap
     candidate). Second sense 뵈다02 = the humble see-meet — the
     dictionary form behind M47's 뵙다 note ("Before vowels it writes
     뵈-: 내일 봬요, 뵈러 가요"): 뵈다 and 뵙다 are TWIN verbs, 뵈다
     before vowel endings (봬요, 뵈러), 뵙다 before consonant endings
     (뵙겠습니다 — M15's 처음 note filed 처음 뵙겠습니다; 뵙고
     싶어요). Pay M47's note and correct its shorthand gently.
   - 답하다 — 답 M15 ("The noun inside M10's 대답하다") + 하다: the
     short, written twin — 질문에 답하다 (질문 M10) on
     forms and exams; 대답하다 in mouths.
   - 대화하다 — 대화 M24 ("face-to-face speech… 대화가 필요해요") +
     하다. Radar vs 이야기하다 M10 (chat, tell): 대화 is two-way, the
     counselor's and the couple's word.
   - 수고하다 — the verb behind the gap deck's 수고하셨습니다 (M16's
     gap g16_sugo: "honors the effort") and 수고하세요 (the leaving-
     you-to-it sign-off); 수고 많으셨어요 (많다 M7) with M4's
     -(으)시- riding every time. Register rule worth a whole note:
     never aim 수고하세요 UP at a boss — equals and downward only.
   - 한잔 — 한 + M5's counter 잔 ("커피 한 잔"), FUSED into one noun:
     한잔 = a drink, the invitation, not the count. 한잔 할까요?;
     한잔하자 (M19's -자). Radar: 커피 한 잔 (spaced, counted, M5) vs
     한잔 (fused, alcoholic) — the spacing is the entire difference.
   - 한잔하다 — to have a drink: 퇴근하고 한잔해요 (퇴근하다 M16: "the
     day's sweetest verb"). The engine of 회식 M16 ("officially
     optional and practically not").
   - 어떡하다 — 어떻게 하다 contracted (NIKL: 어떠하게 하다,
     irregular). Lives in 어떡해(요)? = what do I do? / oh no — M29's
     아이고 note already ran "아이고, 어떡해"; 어떡하지? / 어떡할까요?
     Spelling trap: 어떡해 never 어떻해 — radar vs 어떻게 M10 (how),
     which is 어떻다 M24's adverb. The learner's most-needed panic
     word.
   - 새끼 — NIKL sense "자식~": an animal's young: 새끼 고양이 (M22),
     새끼를 낳다 (낳다 M47). Second: a parent's rough-fond
     word for their own kids (내 새끼) — M47's 자식 note shows the same
     double agent ("이 자식! — 'you punk'"). Third, and the reason
     this note exists: aimed at a person it is the language's
     heaviest common curse. REGISTER LANDMINE — teach the animal
     sense as primary, sentences stay with animals, and say plainly
     the learner must never say it AT anyone. No cute framing.
