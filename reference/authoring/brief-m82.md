All 32 words are nouns.

**Theme:** 32 native words for the world outside the door, in three
drawers. WELDS (12): native Korean names the outdoors by welding owned
parts, mostly with the linking-ㅅ — 강물, 바닷물, 빗물, 나뭇가지, 달빛,
꽃잎, 뒷산, 산속, 새소리, 불꽃, 동물원, 줄기 (+ 먼지). ANIMALS AND
FLOWERS (11): 쥐, 뱀, 개구리, 여우, 나비, 오리, 원숭이, 사자, 코끼리,
진달래, 개나리 — each arriving with the idiom or the folk-tale it lives
in, and three paying frozen IOUs (원숭이, 개나리, 쥐). THE WALK (9):
큰길 → 네거리 → 길가 → 정거장 → 이발소 → 책방 → 등산로 → 이곳저곳 — the
route from the front door to the 뒷산. Organizing thesis: **native
Korean names nature by welding and animals by their stories** — the
compound machine M3's 김밥 note named ("the name is the recipe") runs on
water, light, and branches too, and every animal here is a proverb
with legs.
